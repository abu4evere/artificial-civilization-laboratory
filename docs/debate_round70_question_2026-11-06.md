## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 70 (ACL, 6 November 2026): the design of HK1, 'who holds the kill', the first hand limit to remove after the law-limits council (all three agreed the forced equal hunt split is a hand limit; 2 of 3 put it first). Code facts, read today in nature v14. resolve_hunts: everyone who hunts on one tile in a week is one party; one success roll for the party, chance = hunt_chance(n, mean skill, mean tool factor); on success one take_prey (largest single take 55 meat; a 10-meat-or-less species is taken in a batch near 10); hide and each bone go to a hunter drawn by coin; meat is split evenly, share = meat / n, and each hunter gets min(share, carry room) where carry room = 100 x carry tool - food and seeds held; meat beyond carry room is deleted (not set down), but HN1 measured that loss at 0.000 with the even split; each hunter's reward is 0.3 x min(1, got / need). The hunt option (mind.py) expects chance x expected_take / n (FF2's F8). Theft of food needs the victim to hold more than 4 food, the thief hunger above 0.3, not kin, not partner, not a child; it takes half; a noticed theft makes a grudge, and attack needs a grudge above 0.15; winning a fight moves no goods. Forager evidence read (Gurven 2004, Hiwi and Ache): the acquirer decides how much to keep and who gets shares; sharing is conditional and unequal; violent deaths are 4-28% of deaths in the forager samples listed by Our World in Data, while ACL v13 had 2 killings against 2,029 starvation deaths. Proposed HK law (one change, in a copy; arms live / HK / sham, 32 new seeds, 200 years, years 100-200): on a success the meat goes to one party member, the one who lands it, drawn with weights = that member's own hunting skill term x tool factor (the same factors the party chance uses); the others get no meat from the hunt step; everything else unchanged (hide and bone by coin, gives, theft, care, the carry rule). The sham makes the same draw and logs it but splits evenly. Questions: (1) Who lands the kill: weighted by own skill x tool as proposed, an even draw, or something else? Should 'supported' mean no harm guard raised plus a manipulation check (meat within a party becomes unequal: HK above the sham), or require an outcome? And the killing guard: in CI1, killings rising by more than 0.05 per 1,000 living-years (lower bound above 0) count as harm. Here more theft and fighting after unequal kills is the point of the test. Keep killing as a harm guard, make it described against the forager range (for example killings as a share of deaths), or something else? Population, starvation, child deaths, extinctions and gifts to kin's children stay as guards. (5) Measures to add: thefts tried and noticed, attacks, killings, grudges formed, food gifts by holders after a kill and to whom, party size and the share of hunts by parties, meat per hunter-week, inequality of meat within parties.
