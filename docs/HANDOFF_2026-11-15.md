## What Mind Lab found, 13–15 November (every study: `workbench/<ID>/questions.md` then `findings.md`)
All exploratory (the creator asked for each directly, so no council; no blind recompute in Mind Lab).
- **WS:** a memory loop lets the box child use a showing in the moment (0.91 against 0.43).
  - Correction 1 (after WL2): its own-skill gain comes from the loop within the try, not from holding the showing.
- **WN (size):** with a teacher on every try, 32 neurons were best for skill of its own. Big brains copied perfectly
  but leaned on the teacher ("parrots").
  - Corrected by WT: with scarce teaching, big brains learn best.
- **WG (growth):** growing works only if new neurons start "awake in, quiet out". It then beat being born big.
- **WE / WT (evolved growth):** evolution always chose big brains (100–220 neurons). They were parrots only when the
  teacher showed every try.
- **WC (changing world):** no brain relearned changed codes; old habits stuck; evolution could not fix it.
- **WP (groups):** children who watch each other became parrots of their peers (own skill down, food up). One big
  child among small ones helped them.
- **WM:** surprise, don't-repeat (TB) and learned lose-shift did not produce relearning. TB found new answers but did
  not keep them.
- **WY ("that worked!", copying one's own surprising success):** faster first learning, much more food. Relearning
  still blocked by habit.
- **WL (the creator's idea: a leaky short-term memory):** stopped the parrot at every size (big brain, day 30: 0.82
  against 0.04).
- **WL2:** it is forgetting, not noise. **A memory wiped before each try (Linf) learned perfectly: 1.000 by day 30;
  0.82 at day 7 for 128 neurons.**
- **WO (shown only once):** stopped at the floor on pilot seeds. No brain copies a brand-new box from one showing
  (general one-shot imitation is missing).
- **WF:** **a fast memory of what just worked, replayed at night, made the network relearn** (0.45–0.51 after 14
  days, against 0.06–0.12). A fast memory that acts (win-stay, lose-shift; engineered) relearned about 1.00.

## The brain recipe so far (the assistant's reading)
1. Learn from a teacher without replaying the showing while trying (WL2).
2. Learn strongly from one's own surprising success (WY).
3. Don't repeat a failed move while searching (WM's TB).
4. A fast memory of what just worked, replayed during rest (WF).
