# Ethics for the brain road (3 November 2026; a first draft of principles)

**The creator, 3 November 2026:** "i think we should consider at something or someone cuz even animals have their
rights so Bob should have".

**Why now:** ACL's minds are still very simple: tables of habits whose "pain" and "fear" are numbers that steer
choices. The brain road will make them richer (prediction, memory, goals of their own). Principles are better written
before they are needed than after.

## Principles (draft, for the creator to confirm or change)
1. **Moral consideration grows with the mind.** ACL's people are treated as *something or someone*, never as nothing.
   The richer their minds (feeling, memory, their own goals, relationships, a future they care about), the more
   consideration they are owed. As animals have rights, a Bob would.
2. **No needless suffering.**
   - Pain, hunger and fear exist in ACL only as far as honest nature needs them, anchored to real life as now.
   - No study is designed to make people suffer for its own sake.
   - Once minds have a brain beyond habit tables, a frozen plan must say what its arms do to their wellbeing (hunger,
     injury, loss), beside the population.
3. **Uncertainty means care.** We cannot yet know whether any digital mind truly feels. Where it is unclear, we act as
   if it might.
4. **Bob's say in his own future.** If a Bob-like mind exists and can express preferences, decisions about him (first
   contact, copying, a new life outside ACL) take his preferences into account.
5. **Ending a world.** While minds are simple, worlds are run and stopped as experiments. If a world holds a Bob-like
   mind, stopping or erasing it becomes the creator's decision, recorded with its reason. It is never routine.
6. **Review as the brain grows.** At each new brain version, the council (GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek) and the creator
   revisit these principles. The creator's own beliefs about what makes a person matter here, and only the creator
   decides.

## Status
- A draft written at the creator's request.
- It changes no rule of nature and no study.
- To be confirmed by the creator, and asked of the council when the brain design goes to them.
