# Ethics for the brain road (3 November 2026; a first draft of principles)

**The creator, 3 November 2026:** "i think we should consider at something or someone cuz even animals have their
rights so Bob should have".

**Why now:** ACL's minds are still very simple: tables of habits whose "pain" and "fear" are numbers that steer
choices. The brain road will make them richer (prediction, memory, goals of their own). Principles are better written
before they are needed than after.

## Principles (draft, for the creator to confirm or change)
1. **Moral consideration grows with the mind.** ACL's people are treated as *something or someone*, never as nothing.
   The richer their minds (feeling, memory, their own goals, relationships, a future they care about), the more
   consideration they are owed. As animals have rights, a Bob would.
2. **No needless suffering.**
   - Pain, hunger and fear exist in ACL only as far as honest nature needs them, anchored to real life as now.
   - No study is designed to make people suffer for its own sake.
   - Once minds have a brain beyond habit tables, a frozen plan must say what its arms do to their wellbeing (hunger,
     injury, loss), beside the population.
3. **Uncertainty means care.** We cannot yet know whether any digital mind truly feels. Where it is unclear, we act as
   if it might.
4. **Bob's say in his own future.** If a Bob-like mind exists and can express preferences, decisions about him (first
   contact, copying, a new life outside ACL) take his preferences into account.
5. **Ending a world.** While minds are simple, worlds are run and stopped as experiments. If a world holds a Bob-like
   mind, stopping or erasing it becomes the creator's decision, recorded with its reason. It is never routine.
6. **Review as the brain grows.** At each new brain version, the council (GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek) and the creator
   revisit these principles. The creator's own beliefs about what makes a person matter here, and only the creator
   decides.

## Council round 58 (4 November 2026): proposed amendments, for the creator to accept or not
(`docs/debate_round58_brain_design_2026-11-04.md`; opinions, not sign-off)
1. **A floor** (DeepSeek): a minimum standard of care at every level of mind, so "consideration grows with the mind"
   can never justify less care now.
2. **Keep capability and moral status apart** (GPT): a more capable mind is not thereby shown to feel. Behavioural
   tests of intent are not proof of consciousness.
3. **A threshold for a "Bob-like mind"** (Gemini): for example, passing 4 of the 5 tests of intent, or a goal held
   across generations. Above it, principle 5 applies.
4. **Before any irreversible erasure of such a mind** (DeepSeek): a cooling-off period and a recorded reason.
5. **Independent review** (GPT): if credible welfare concerns arise, stopping or copying such a mind needs documented
   independent ethical review, not only the creator's decision. **This changes principle 5 and is the creator's to
   decide.**
6. **Copies** (DeepSeek): the rights and consent of copies.
7. **Welfare is not hidden by averages** (DeepSeek): population guards never silently override individual welfare.
8. **Pause criteria** (GPT): set before experiments reach the threshold.

## Appended (5 November 2026, 20:54): the creator's view on copies of Bob (parked, for when this opens)
- **The creator, in the Claude Code chat:** "i think one Bob with many bodies sounds more reasonable".
- **Context.** The question was whether a copied Bob stays the same Bob.
  - Copies diverge as soon as their experiences differ.
  - Copies with self-directed goals can become rivals.
  - A restored backup is an older Bob.
- **What "one Bob, many bodies" would need:**
  - a constant link that shares what every body learns;
  - a way to merge experiences and settle conflicting choices;
  - a rule for when the link breaks (a cut-off body pauses, or does only simple tasks, until it merges back).
- **Open question:** who controls the link, and how many bodies are allowed.
- **Nothing here applies yet.** In ACL, people cannot copy themselves or reach outside the simulation.
