# What ACL's nature is still missing: possibilities that could matter for a civilization

Asked for by the creator (23 October 2026): "our acl world lack so many things with possibilities try to find more
like these which may effect to civilization to emerge also discuss with gpt". A working list, not a plan. Each item
would be its own Law 126 study: one change to nature, frozen first, the creator signs off, tested against the current
nature. The rule stays the same: **program physics, bodies and feelings, never the cultural answer.**

## GPT's top eight (GPT's opinion, 23 October 2026; not verified sources)

Ranked by GPT by how much each could affect a civilization's emergence. The assistant's check of the code is in the
last column.

| rank | possibility (GPT) | bottleneck (GPT) | in ACL now (the assistant's check of v3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contagious disease**: infection passes through contact, shared places, care, bad water; severity and recovery vary | demography, cooperation | **Missing.** Illness is drawn for each person alone; crowding raises the chance, but nobody catches it from anyone |

## The assistant's own additions

- **Carrying people:** a parent carrying an infant, someone carrying the sick or the dead. Without it, moving camp
  with small children and burying someone elsewhere are impossible. (Needed for burial after digging.)
- **Scavengers at bodies:** wolves and bears drawn to carcasses and bodies (left out of BD1 on GPT's advice, to keep
  one change at a time).
- **Dogs:** wolves that hang around people's food and bodies can become tame over generations; dogs helped hunting and
  guarded camps. A long-range possibility (the oldest dogs are well over ten thousand years old).
- **Bad water:** bodies or waste in water making drinkers ill (links bodies, water and contagion).

## The assistant's recommended order after BD1

1. Digging (the creator's order; it also opens storage pits, bottleneck B).
2. Water takes things.
3. **Contagious disease** (GPT's first; it shapes how big groups can get and who cares for whom).
4. **Lasting injury and care** (the frail and the injured, and who keeps them alive: the "harsh" side of life the
   creator asked for, as possibility, not rule).
5. Carrying people (with burial in mind).
6. Tool repair.
7. Meeting outsiders (needs separate founding groups: a bigger change to how worlds start).

Each is the creator's decision before any plan is frozen.
