## Bob
- Bob is the hoped-for individual whose identity emerges from a particular life: temperament, habits, memories,
  beliefs, skills, attachments, history-dependent goals; recognisable through change. The creator has considered
  carrying Bob to another world or a robot.
- 4 October framework (accepted by the creator): fixed behavioural probes before and after transfer; compare with
  Bob's own natural drift and with a stranger floor; layered retention (temperament, habits, skills, beliefs,
  attachments); handle missing familiar places and people. A faithful copy is not proof of continuity of experience.
- GPT's suggestion: judge ACL on two axes, individual development and civilization, giving the first priority. A
  thriving civilization of interchangeable people would not deliver Bob.
