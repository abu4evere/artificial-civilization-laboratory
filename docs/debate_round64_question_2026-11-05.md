The same text goes to GPT (the main chat), Gemini and DeepSeek. Their answers are opinions, not sign-off.

## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 64 (ACL, 5 November 2026): after LD1. LD1 (read-only on v13, final, recomputed blind by GPT and Gemini; 32 new seeds, worlds v13 and v13 with discount 0) re-scored 4.66 million real adult decisions with shadow habit tables that split q into the part learned from this week's r (q_r) and the look-ahead part (q_f, from 0.8 x the max habit value of next week's situation). Part A: removing the look-ahead's differences between actions recovers 77% [71%, 83%] of the no-look-ahead make share within single choices (shrinking all habit differences to the no-look-ahead scale recovers 91%); at moments when making is offered, the look-ahead part scores making 0.245 below the options' mean (q_r part -0.107); the look-ahead term at learning is lowest after make, play, explore and hunt and highest after talk, give, care and forage; and next week's best-fed situation is valued LEAST (+0.26 against +0.93 to +1.04 for the other food bands), because people are rarely well fed and a rarely visited situation's habit values stay near their start, so the max favours familiar situations, not better ones; values are large (habit term 1.3 x all written pulls together, against 0.23 without look-ahead) but almost never clamped (0.06%). Earlier, PB3: a look-ahead through the person's own actual next-week reward (P+, on-policy) also lowered chains against no look-ahead (-0.0114), so even a correct one-week look-ahead charges making for its real one-week cost (a week not spent feeding). Part B (where remaking fails, each new kind followed 5 years in its inventor's weeks): in v13, 88% of new kinds stop at 'held the recipe but never with its inputs in hand'; without the look-ahead people invent 11x more kinds and 64% are forgotten by the first week's end (a new recipe enters at value 0 and is first evicted past the 12-recipe cap); only 2% are ever learned by anyone else; a new kind is offered as a make option only if valued above 0.05 and not already held. Questions: (1) Is the habit look-ahead now a fault (its comment says 'how much next week's prospects count', but it measures familiarity and values being well fed least), or a law to replace? Which change is a general law and not tuning: removing the look-ahead from habits (foresight left to other systems), a look-ahead that counts a situation's value only as far as it has been learned (by visits), an expected value, a longer horizon, or something else? (2) What is the first test toward remaking: in v13 the bottleneck is opportunity (inventors never hold the inputs again: no intention to gather for a past success; new kinds start unvalued), and once making rises it is memory (the 12-recipe cap); should the next study target value-at-invention (a new kind valued by what it did when made), an intention to remake, recipe memory, or the look-ahead first? (3) Your estimate (0-100%) that your recommended next study is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
