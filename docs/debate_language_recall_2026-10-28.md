# Debate round 22 (28 October 2026): the language plan, recalled (opinions, not sign-off)

**The creator asked GPT and Gemini to recall** the earlier plan (about 27 October, with the other Claude account) to
remove ACL's built-in "telepathic" talk so that people start from basics and their own language may emerge. Same text
to both; each asked to separate what it finds in the earlier chat from what it adds now. The written record of that
discussion is `docs/gpt_view_language_2026-10-27.md`.

## What both recall (it matches the written record)
**The creator's rules:** no map or coordinates in signals; nobody writes the dictionary; no LLMs inside people; add
conditions and capacities, never meanings or outcomes; one law at a time, frozen and tested.

**The steps, in order:**
1. **Audit the telepathy:** list every direct channel (places, recipes, habit tables, beliefs, stories, reputation,
   marks). Done: IC1.
2. **Find a real need:** shared attention, repeated meetings, and moments when one knows what the other lacks. Done:
   IC1 and IC2.
3. **Change one channel only:** replace its direct transfer with a few meaningless signals whose meanings people must
   learn from experience.
4. **Test:** do people come to share meanings, use them to communicate, and pass them to children?
5. **Expand slowly:** other channels, combining signals, and in the end teaching through signals.

GPT adds: this was a research roadmap, not an approved set of nature changes; no language law was adopted. Learned
place names were judged fair only if they call up the listener's own memory.

## Should the plan change after IC1 and IC2?
- **GPT:** "Change the immediate experiment, not the long-term goal." Keep steps 1–2 as done; don't go straight to a
  water naming law. First find out, read-only, **why useful gaps are so rare** (the 1-tile talk range, overlapping
  sight). Any later change to talk range or perception must be justified independently and never tuned to make
  language appear. Supports the creator's choice to work on erosion first.
- **Gemini:** "the plan must change before Step 1 can succeed." People talking only within 1 tile share the same view,
  so signals must be able to **travel further (it suggests 3–4 tiles)** so a speaker can report unseen game or danger,
  and signals should refer to remembered or distant things. Also agrees erosion first: "language cannot build cultural
  accumulation if underlying craft skills continually decay to zero."

## Council
- **Agreed by all three:** the long-term goal stands; the next language step must create a real need without tuning;
  erosion (FS1) comes first.
- **Gemini's "3–4 tiles" is not adopted as a number:** any hearing range must come from human evidence (how far a call
  carries), not be chosen to make language work, as GPT says.
