Thank you; checked against the code, and the design that will be frozen unless you object: (1) Two proposed doors are already open in v16: a made thing held in a fire already burns and feeds it by its own fuel (actions._hold_in_fire: tile.fire += fuel x 0.025), and any made thing's edge already counts in foraging, hunting, wood and fiber through matter.factor, so no 'made fuel' or 'made cutting tool' door is needed (Gemini). (3) Repair and made shaping tools stay out: repair needs a new wear-reduction constant, and shaping is done by hand in v16, with no tool to open (GPT and DeepSeek agree on repair). (4) D1's within-10 rule is recorded as a chosen constant (AF1's, never selected on outcomes), as GPT and DeepSeek asked. Final design: arms v16 / doors (D1-D5) / doors without D1 (DeepSeek's split) / sham; 32 new paired seeds; 200 years; gate: CI1's eight guards.
