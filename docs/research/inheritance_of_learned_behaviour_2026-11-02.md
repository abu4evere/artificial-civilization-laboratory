# Does biology pass learned content to offspring? Evidence for the creator's BN1 decision (2 November 2026)

**Why:** BN1 tested, in a copy, whether ACL's newborns should start with half their parents' learned habit values (v11)
or with none. The result was inconclusive by the frozen rule: a small early cost for "born at zero", no later cost, no
guard. The decision is the creator's. This note asks what real biology says about inheriting **learned** content.
**A source suggests; only the creator decides.**

**Labels:** what was read is stated for each source.

## Sources
1. **Dias & Ressler 2014**, "Parental olfactory experience influences behavior and neural structure in subsequent
   generations", *Nature Neuroscience* 17:89–96, doi 10.1038/nn.3594.
   - **Read:** the abstract (Europe PMC).
   - **What it says:** mice taught to fear one odour before conception had offspring (F1 and F2) more sensitive to that
     odour only, with changes in its neural pathway and in sperm DNA methylation. In vitro fertilisation and
     cross-fostering pointed to inheritance through the gametes.
2. **Francis 2014**, "Too much success for recent groundbreaking epigenetic experiments", *Genetics* 198:449–451, doi
   10.1534/genetics.114.163998.
   - **Read:** the abstract (Europe PMC).
   - **What it says:** the reported experiments "were uniformly successful", an outcome expected with probability
     0.004 if the effects are real but sampling is random. The evidence is therefore doubtful.

## What this suggests for BN1 (the assistant's reading; for the creator)
- **The best-known claim is narrow.** It is a heightened sensitivity to **one cue** (an odour), passed by gametes.
- **It is also contested.** Francis's statistical critique says the results look too good to be true.
- **Nothing found here supports what ACL's v11 does:** passing a newborn **half the parents' learned value for every
  situation and action**, a whole learned policy.
- **Together with the IQ1 report and developmental science** (`docs/research/iq1_islamic_sources_report_2026-11-01.md`:
  "born knowing nothing", with faculties developing gradually), the realistic reading leans toward born without learned
  content.
- **BN1 also matters, and it is mixed.** Removing birth inheritance costs a little early calibration (about 7%) and
  nothing later.
- **The choice stays the creator's.** Possible paths:
  1. keep v11;
  2. confirm "born at zero" on new seeds with a pre-registered margin, then decide;
  3. decide directly.
