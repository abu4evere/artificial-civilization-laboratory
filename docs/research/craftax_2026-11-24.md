# Craftax: the next outside exam (24 Nov 2026; the roadmap "mind lab -> Crafter -> Craaftax -> Maybe ACL")

**What was read: the ABSTRACT only** (arXiv 2402.16801, through a page fetch). A source suggests; only a frozen study
decides.

Matthews, Beukman, Ellis, Samvelyan, Jackson, Coward, Foerster 2024, "Craftax: A Lightning-Fast Benchmark for
Open-Ended RL":
- **Craftax-Classic:** Crafter rewritten in JAX, "up to 250x faster"; PPO with 1 billion interactions in under an hour on
  one GPU reaches about 90% of the optimal reward.
- **Craftax (full):** Crafter plus NetHack-like mechanics; needs "deep exploration, long term planning and memory, as
  well as continual adaptation to novel situations"; PPO, exploration methods and environment design "fail to make
  material progress".

## What it means for us (proposals)
1. **Crafter first, Craftax later.** CX1 put MBb at 0.56% in Crafter (above random choices through the same bridge,
   below Crafter's own random agent): the next Crafter test should be a brain that LEARNS within a life there (W3r's
   line), not a bigger score from training on Crafter.
2. **Craftax-Classic is Crafter at 250x the speed but needs JAX**, and its speed is a GPU's. On our CPUs (PC, Oracle,
   Google) JAX still runs, slower; worth a timing check before any plan. Full Craftax is the harder exam for a later
   Bob.
3. The GPT principle the creator adopted ("every new world should require Bob to learn more, not require us to program
   more") argues for the same brain in Crafter and Craftax with no per-world code beyond the bridge for the eyes.
