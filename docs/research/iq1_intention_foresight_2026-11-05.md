# IQ1 re-opened: intention, foresight and a practice that outlives its starter (5 November 2026)

**Why now:**

## The sources (all verified on 5 November 2026)
1. **[S] Sahih al-Bukhari 1** (sunnah.com, read on the page; in-book Book 1, Hadith 1). Narrated by 'Umar bin
   Al-Khattab: "The reward of deeds depends upon the intentions and every person will get the reward according to
   what he has intended…"
2. **[S] Q 59:18** (quran.com API, the translations as given):
   - Saheeh International: "O you who have believed, fear Allāh. And let every soul look to what it has put forth for
     tomorrow - and fear Allāh. Indeed, Allāh is Aware of what you do."
   - Abdel Haleem: "…let every soul consider carefully what it sends ahead for tomorrow…"
3. **[I] Ibn Kathir on 59:18** (abridged English, quran.com API):
   - "hold yourselves accountable before you are recompensed, and contemplate what you have kept for yourselves of
     good deeds for the Day of your return".
   - **"Tomorrow" here is the Day of Return.** The verse is about preparing deeds for the hereafter, not worldly
     planning.
4. **[S] Sahih Muslim 1017a** (sunnah.com, read on the page; Book of Zakat, in-book Book 12, Hadith 88; Ibn Kathir
   quotes it at 59:18): "He who sets a good precedent in Islam, there is a reward for him for this (act of goodness)
   and reward of that also who acted according to it subsequently, without any deduction from their rewards…"
   - Its setting is charity: after the Prophet's call, one man brought a heavy bag and "the people followed
     continuously".

## What they suggest for ACL ([C]: the assistant's analogies, for the council)
1. **Intention organises action (Bukhari 1, as an analogy).**
   - The hadith speaks of the moral worth of deeds by their intention. It is not a theory of cognition.
   - Read as an analogy, it says that what a person does is organised by what they intend.
   - LD1 shows exactly that absence in ACL: inventors hold their recipe, but nothing turns "I made this, and it
     helped" into a held aim that steers gathering its inputs. 88% of new kinds stop there.
   - **A general law it suggests:** an intention formed toward a state that proved good in one's own experience, held
     across weeks, and steering the steps toward it. It would be the same for any valued state, never named for
     inventions and never rewarding making as such.
   - **Secular science proposes the same mechanism:** goal-directed behaviour and "implementation intentions"
     (named here from general knowledge; not read today).
2. **Foresight as preparing, and as taking stock (59:18, by analogy only).**
   - Ibn Kathir reads the verse as self-accounting and preparation for the hereafter.
   - Taken loosely, it describes a foresight based on **what one has prepared**: the deeds sent ahead, or in ACL, the
     things made, stored and kept. It is not based on how familiar the next situation is, which is what LD1 found the
     habit look-ahead to be.
   - This is a weak analogy, stated as such. It fits the council's view (round 64) that foresight belongs to a
     separate predictive system, not to habits.
3. **A practice that outlives its starter (Muslim 1017a).**
   - The hadith describes a good practice that others follow after the one who began it. That is the very
     phenomenon cumulative culture needs.
   - LD1: only 2% of new kinds in ACL are ever learned by anyone but their inventor.
   - **The limit, stated plainly:** the hadith attaches a reward to the starter of a followed practice. ACL's rules
     forbid rewards for inventing or for being copied, so this text points at the **phenomenon** to look for, not at a
     mechanism to write into nature.

## Where this leads (proposals, not decisions)
- **Value at invention first** (council round 64). An intention needs something already valued.
- **Then an intention study:** a held aim toward a state that one's own experience valued, steering the gathering of
  what it needs. Bukhari 1 is its inspiration ([C]); goal research is its secular twin.
- **Measures that would show the 1017a phenomenon:** a kind's practice continuing after its inventor dies, and the
  share of kinds others take up.

Each goes to the council and a frozen plan.
