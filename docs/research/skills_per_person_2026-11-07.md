# How many skills does one person hold? (for the 12-recipe memory, 7 November 2026)

**Why:** in PM1's smoke run (not data), the most common stop for a new kind was **P0a**: no one living still knows the
recipe of one of its parts. One suspect is ACL's memory: a person holds at most **12 recipes**. When it is full, the
least valued one is dropped (`knowhow.remember_recipe`; FF3 made ties a fair draw). The real PM1 run will say whether
P0a is the main stop; this note is the evidence beside it. A source suggests; only a frozen study decides.

## Schniter, Gurven, Kaplan, Wilcox & Hooper 2015 — **full text read** (author PDF, text extracted with PyMuPDF)
Skill Ontogeny Among Tsimane Forager-Horticulturalists. *American Journal of Physical Anthropology* 158: 3–18.

**What it reports:**
- **The skill list.** Interviews gave 101 skills in 12 categories. A panel of Tsimane researchers refined these to
  **92 essential skills**: 51 unisex, 11 female-specific and 30 male-specific. They cover:
  - food production;
  - craft production (baskets, textiles, houses, canoes, hunting, fishing and gardening tools, food-processing tools,
    musical instruments);
  - chores, childcare, social and market skills, music and story-telling.
- **Who holds them.** Of the 421 people surveyed, almost everyone holds one or more skills in each category. Music and
  story-telling are the exception, held by 228.
- **When they are learned.** Median ages of acquiring a skill (able to do it alone) are **13–16 for women and 14–17
  for men**. Food skills come first, then chores and crafts, then childcare, then music and story-telling.
- **Old age.** Older adults keep adding know-how, and are judged the most expert, especially in music and
  story-telling.

**Not found in the parts read:** a count of how many craft skills a typical adult holds. The full list is in the online
supplement, which was not opened.

## Bearing on ACL
- **A person in a small-scale society holds dozens of essential skills,** 92 on the Tsimane list. Most are acquired
  by the late teens, and many are craft procedures.
- **ACL's people hold at most 12 recipes for everything they can make.** The earlier note
  (`toolkit_sizes_2026-11-02.md`) found 12 below the smallest single-domain toolkit (13 marine tool types).
- **If PM1 finds P0a (part recipes forgotten) as the main stop,** the recipe-memory cap is the first suspect. A
  general, evidence-anchored change would be a larger memory, or memory limited by use rather than by a fixed count.
  That would go to the council as a law question, with a frozen test, never a tuned number.
