# How many made things do real foraging societies know? An anchor for ACL's 12-recipe memory (2 November 2026)

**Why:** the rules review (`docs/rules_review_2026-11-02.md`, R1) found that a person holds at most 12 recipes. A new
recipe starts at value 0, so a person whose 12 recipes are all valued forgets a new invention at once (21% of new
kinds in one SC1 world). Is 12 near human scale? **A source suggests; only a frozen study decides.**

## Source
**Kline & Boyd 2010,** "Population size predicts technological complexity in Oceania", *Proceedings of the Royal
Society B* 277:2559–2564, doi 10.1098/rspb.2010.0452, PMCID PMC2894932.
- **Read:** the abstract (Europe PMC record), and Table 1 of the full text on PubMed Central, through the fetch tool's
  summary of the page. The table values below are as the tool reported them; they were not checked against a PDF.
- **What it measures:** marine foraging tools only (fishing and sea-hunting gear) in 10 Oceanic fisher-farmer groups
  near European contact. A "techno-unit" is a distinct part of a tool.

| population | people | tools | mean techno-units per tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malekula | 1,100 | 13 | 3.2 |
| Tikopia | 1,500 | 22 | 4.7 |
| Santa Cruz | 3,600 | 24 | 4.0 |
| Yap | 4,791 | 43 | 5.0 |
| Lau Fiji | 7,400 | 33 | 5.0 |
| Trobriand | 8,000 | 19 | 4.0 |
| Chuuk | 9,200 | 40 | 3.8 |
| Manus | 13,000 | 28 | 6.6 |
| Tonga | 17,500 | 55 | 5.4 |
| Hawaii | 275,000 | 71 | 6.6 |

## What it suggests for ACL (the assistant's reading)
- **The toolkits:** even the smallest **marine** toolkit alone has 13 tool types, of 3–7 parts each. Food-getting at
  sea is only one domain; houses, clothing, containers and fire are others.
- **ACL's cap:** every intermediate part has its own recipe, and the 12-recipe cap per person covers **everything**.
- **This is population-level data.** It says what a society holds, not what one person remembers. Specialists exist.
  Still, a cap of 12 for all of a person's know-how sits **below the smallest single-domain toolkit** in this sample.
- **The study's own point** bears on Bob's goal: larger, better-connected populations held more and more complex tools
  (cumulative culture depends on demography). ACL's LP worlds (80 founders) are small by this table. The cap and the
  population size may both limit accumulation.
- **The use for the council:** if R1 is tested, a larger cap can be anchored to evidence like this, as AG1 anchored
  ageing to the !Kung, rather than chosen to make remaking happen. MO1 (v7) showed that keeping recipes longer did not
  by itself change making. So this is a realism fix, not expected to unlock remaking alone.
