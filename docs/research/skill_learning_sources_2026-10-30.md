## 1. Morgan et al. 2015, *Nature Communications* 6: 6029 — teaching, language and stone-tool making
"Experimental evidence for the co-evolution of hominin tool-making teaching and language."
https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms7029 — **read: abstract.**
- 184 adult participants in transmission chains learned Oldowan stone-flake making under five mechanisms
  (reverse engineering, imitation/emulation, basic teaching, gestural teaching, verbal teaching).
- Across six measures, transmission **improved with teaching, and particularly with language, but not with imitation or
  emulation.**
- **For ACL:** a law where onlookers merely *watch* making may pass little technique; real gains came from being
  *shown* (teaching). Consistent with the scout's smoke hint (watching at nature's existing chance adds about 3% to
  children's crafting practice; `docs/design_note_apprenticeship_autonomy_2026-10-30.md`). The apprenticeship study
  should expect a small effect from watching alone, and may want a later arm where a maker *shows* (ACL already has
  teaching of recipes; it never passes skill).

## 2. Lew-Levy et al. 2017, *Human Nature* — how hunter-gatherer children learn subsistence skills
"How do hunter-gatherer children learn subsistence skills? A meta-ethnographic review."
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28994008/ — **read: abstract.**
- Learning starts in infancy: parents take children foraging and give them **toy versions of tools**.
- In early and middle childhood, children learn in **multi-age playgroups through play, observation and
  participation**; by the end of middle childhood most are proficient food collectors.
- **Direct teaching of complex skills (hunting, complex tool making) starts in adolescence, by adults not necessarily
  parents.** Teaching does exist among hunter-gatherers.
- **For ACL:** (a) ACL children under 12 cannot make anything (`mind.py:279`), and all their crafting skill comes from
  play (PL1); forager children use toy tools and join in. (b) Watching and joining in fit early and middle childhood;
  teaching of complex making fits adolescence (ACL's teens 12–15 can make). An apprenticeship law anchored in this
  evidence would let children who watch or join in gain technique, with direct showing for teens.

## 3. Talent (for the later debate)
- Macnamara, Hambrick & Oswald 2014, *Psychological Science*: deliberate practice explained 26% of performance
  differences in games and 21% in music. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/07/140702111033.htm — **read:
  press summary and search summary of the paper.**
- Campitelli & Gobet (2007; 2011 summary): chess players needed about 3,000 to 24,000 hours of practice to reach master
  level (about 1:8); some with over 20,000 hours never did.
  https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/deliberate-practice-necessary-but-not-sufficient.html — **read:
  press release and search summary.**

## 4. Ageing and skill (for AG1's reading)
- **Koster et al. 2020, *Science Advances* 6: eaax9070**, "The life history of human foraging: cross-cultural and
  individual variation." https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aax9070 — **read: abstract and search summary.**
  About 23,000 hunting records from more than 1,800 individuals at 40 locations: average **peak hunting productivity
  between 30 and 35** (about 33), high skill maintained through much of adulthood; individuals vary substantially in
  skill; the rate of reaching peak varies across sites. **For ACL:** in AG1's v arm, adults die at a mean age of 42
  (read-only count), so many die soon after a forager's skill peak; and "individuals vary substantially" bears on the
  talent item.
- **Hawkes, O'Connell & Blurton Jones 1998, *PNAS* 95: 1336–1339**, "Grandmothering, menopause, and the evolution of
  human life histories." https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.95.3.1336 — **read: abstract.** Long post-menopausal
  lives are uniquely human among primates; proposed to have evolved with mother–child food sharing, ageing females
  raising their daughters' fertility and so selecting against senescence. **For ACL:** ACL women are fertile 16–45; with
  realistic ageing (AG1 arms a and b) more women would live past 45. Whether they feed grandchildren depends on ACL's
  giving and care rules (not checked here); a read-only look after AG1 could ask.
