# Uptake of new inventions: published work bearing on SC1's gap (2 November 2026)

**Why:** SC1 found that no kind invented after year 100 is ever made again (0 of 3,883). An exploratory check of one
world then showed most such kinds are kept, used and valued, yet never remade. **A source suggests; only a frozen
study decides.**

**Labels:** what was read is stated for each source.

## Sources
1. **Henrich 2001**, "Cultural Transmission and the Diffusion of Innovations: Adoption Dynamics Indicate That Biased
   Cultural Transmission Is the Predominate Force in Behavioral Change", *American Anthropologist* 103:992–1013, doi
   10.1525/aa.2001.103.4.992.
   - **Read:** the abstract (Crossref record).
   - **What it says:** individual "environmental learning alone never produces" the S-shaped adoption curves seen when
     new practices spread. Biased cultural transmission always does. The two together do so only when biased
     transmission predominates.
2. **Mesoudi 2011**, "Variable cultural acquisition costs constrain cumulative cultural evolution", *PLoS ONE* 6,
   doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0018239.
   - **Read:** the abstract (Europe PMC).
   - **What it says:** when acquiring culture costs more as it grows more complex, cumulative culture reaches "an
     upper limit ... at which point ... further innovation is not possible". The models also vary transmission rules,
     population size, and innovations that lower costs.

## What this suggests for ACL (the assistant's reading; for the council)
- **ACL's uptake path is mostly individual.**
  - A new kind spreads only if its inventor values it by their own use and then teaches it (lessons offer only things
    valued above 0.15). Copying by watching is rare (KL1: about 10 gains).
  - By Henrich's argument, individual evaluation alone would give slow, non-S-shaped spread at best. SC1's "never" is
    stronger than that: the inventors themselves do not remake.
- **ACL has fixed capacity limits:** 12 recipes and 3 carried things. These are a hard form of Mesoudi's acquisition
  cost. A new kind competes for a slot with old, proven ones: 14 of 66 were dropped on arrival in the check.
- **The open question is inside the make choice.** Kept, used, valued new kinds are still not remade. Before any
  change to nature, a read-only audit should find which condition blocks them:
  - holding one already;
  - parts not in hand;
  - an older kind valued more;
  - "make" losing to other actions.
