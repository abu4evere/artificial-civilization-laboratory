# A caution for HE1: real habit learning looks ahead (research note, 5 November 2026)

Written while HE1's zero checks ran. Council round 64 named a law, "habits learn only from experienced outcomes
(no look-ahead); foresight is left to other systems", and HE1 tests it. **Before any adoption, this note records the
evidence against the law's literal claim.** A source suggests; only a frozen study decides.

## Sources
1. **Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997).** "A neural substrate of prediction and reward". *Science* 275(5306),
   1593–1599. DOI [10.1126/science.275.5306.1593](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593); PMID 9054347.
   - **Read: the abstract** (PubMed record; volume, issue and pages checked there).
   - What it says: dopamine neurons in primates signal "changes or errors in the predictions of future salient and
     rewarding events", understood through "quantitative theories of adaptive optimizing control". The theory behind
     it is temporal-difference learning, which learns from the reward **plus the predicted value of what comes next**,
     that is, with a look-ahead. That last point is known from the wider literature, not from the abstract.
2. **Already in the project** (`docs/research/dopamine_max_or_expected_2026-10-31.md`; abstracts read):
   - Morris et al. 2006: monkeys' dopamine neurons reflect the value of the **upcoming** action, an on-policy
     look-ahead;
   - Roesch et al. 2007: rats' dopamine neurons reflect the **better** option, a max look-ahead.

## Bearing on HE1 (the assistant's reading)
- **The evidence says habit-like (model-free) learning in brains does look ahead.** A literal "habits never look
  ahead" is not nature's way by this account.
- **What LD1 found wrong is not the look-ahead as such.** It is a look-ahead over ACL's coarse situations (32
  situations; food band × company × season), where rarely visited situations keep near-initial values, so the max
  favours familiarity. Brains bootstrap over far richer states.
- **Earlier ACL evidence points the same way:**
  - ES1 (an expected look-ahead) and ES2 (a look-ahead by the real next choice) were not supported on their frozen
    primaries, but both raised making about 3–5 times and population about 16–20%.
  - PB3's P+ (a look-ahead through the person's own next week) also lowered chains against no look-ahead.

  Every look-ahead tried so far costs making. Part of that cost is real: a week spent making is a week not feeding.
- **What this means:**
  - **HE1 stays a valid test.** It measures harm and effect.
  - **But "supported" should not be read as "nature has no habit look-ahead".** Before adoption, the council should
    weigh two options:
    1. adopting the separation as an ACL law (habits from experience; foresight through the brain road's predictor
       and E9's anticipation);
    2. a look-ahead that bootstraps only from **well-learned** situations, closer to how brains work and a fix of the
       familiarity bias LD1 measured.
  - This caution goes into HE1's findings and into the council question on adoption.
