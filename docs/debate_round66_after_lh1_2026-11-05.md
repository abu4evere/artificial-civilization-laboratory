2. **The funnel first**, all three agreeing. GPT adds two stages worth recording: whether the input was obtainable at

3. **The tie-break.** All three call the name order a fault. They split on the remedy.
   - **Gemini's "deploy at once, without a sham" is not allowed** by CLAUDE.md. Even a plain fault fix needs zero
     checks, the tests, a recorded check showing the fault, and a harm check (as frozen in FX1).
   - **GPT's split is the most careful, and it is adopted.**
     - The fault correction must not bring in a new principle. A name-free tie-break settled by its own random
       stream, so no other draw shifts, removes the bias and adds no theory.
     - "The longest-unused is forgotten" is a law of memory, supported by Anderson & Schooler 1991. It is tested later,
       as a law, against the corrected baseline.
   - DeepSeek's power caution stands. The tie fires mostly at value-0 ties when the memory is full (130 of 1,194
     kinds in VA1's window).
   - Order: the funnel (read-only, on v13) comes first. The tie-break fault fix can follow by the fault-fix path. It
     does not block the funnel.

## Merged plan
1. **RF1, a read-only remaking funnel on v13** (to be frozen next). Every person-week in which a recipe is known,
   valued above the make threshold (0.05) and its thing not held is followed. For each missing input it records:
   - its type (raw material, made part, binder);
   - whether it was obtainable (stock within sight, in that season);
   - whether a gather or make option for it was offered, its score and rank, chosen, completed;
   - what was chosen instead;
   - whether inputs gathered were lost or used up before assembly.

   **The reading rule (to be frozen):** "cannot obtain", "not offered", "offered but out-competed", or "gathered but
   lost". Only "offered but out-competed" leads to an intention law.
2. **Then the tie-break fault fix**, by the fault-fix path: a name-free tie-break with its own random stream; zero
   checks (off = v13), tests, a recorded check of the fault, and a harm check.
3. **Later:** recency forgetting as a law, and an intention law if RF1 points there.
4. **The look-ahead line is closed for now.**
