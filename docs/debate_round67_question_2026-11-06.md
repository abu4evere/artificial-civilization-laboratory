# Council round 67: after RF1, an intention law and the life-stage gate (the question, 6 November 2026)

The same text goes to GPT (the main chat), Gemini and DeepSeek. Their answers are opinions, not sign-off.

## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 67 (ACL, 6 November 2026): after RF1. RF1 (final, read-only on v13, 32 new seeds, 1,246 world-new kinds from years 130-165, each followed 5 years; recomputed blind by GPT and Gemini with code, DeepSeek by hand) followed every week in which a grown inventor knew, valued and lacked their new kind. Of the 69 kinds that had such weeks: cannot obtain 0.029, not offered 0.174, out-competed 0.029, started but never complete 0.768 [0.657, 0.857], complete 0. The frozen reading is 'pursuit fails' (stages 3+4 = 0.797 [0.702, 0.878]). Every one of the 53 started kinds saw a completed gather of a missing input, but in 45 of them a gathered piece was later lower before the set was complete (used in another make, given, set down, or lost). The missing pieces were mostly made parts (10,044 piece-weeks) rather than raw materials (4,071); 35% of wanting weeks were at the 20-unit carry limit; instead people chose forage 0.40, gathering another material 0.19, put 0.12, talk 0.11. The bigger gate: 1,062 of the 1,246 kinds were invented by people not yet grown. New kinds arise in play (from age 3; children play 1.6x as much), but only the grown (12+) are ever offered 'make again'. Evidence: Lew-Levy et al. 2017 (Human Nature, a meta-ethnography of 58 publications; read as a summary) report hunter-gatherer children making their own simple tools, such as small bows, by about 4-5, with complex manufacture learned in adolescence. Sutton, Precup & Singh 1999 (options) give a general form for a held course of action with a start, an end, and interruption when something better arises. Questions: (1) An intention law, minimal and general: when a valued kind's thing is gone and its recipe is known, the person holds an aim; while it is held, (a) the pieces they hold that the recipe needs are not spent on other makes, gifts or setting down; (b) the gather and make options toward its missing pieces, including making a missing part, get the pull the make option already uses (worth_weight x the value x (0.6 + 0.4 diligence) x (1 - 0.5 hunger), as making is now); (c) the aim ends when the set is complete, the value falls to 0.05 or below, or the recipe is gone. Which parts are general laws and which are tuning; what would you drop or add; and what harm guards (starvation, killing, gifts to kin)? (2) The life-stage gate: test 'children may make again what they know' (a general ability, in line with the evidence) before, after, or together with the intention law? (3) Order with FF3, the fault fix of memory ties settled by name (an exploratory count in one world: 64% of recipe evictions, 34% of mark evictions, 15% of source-record evictions and 9% of puzzle evictions are decided by the tie-break; the fix is a draw from a dedicated random stream). (4) Your estimate (0-100%) that your recommended next study is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
