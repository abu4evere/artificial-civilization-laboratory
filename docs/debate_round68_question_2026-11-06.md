## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 68 (ACL, 6 November 2026): before freezing CI1. Since round 67, FF3 found no harm (living-weeks f/v13 0.964 [0.851, 1.087], sham 0.958) and nature v14 is live (ties at a full memory drawn evenly instead of by name; new kinds kept at birth 0.298 -> 0.387). CI1 runs on v14 with arms v14, LG, IN, LG+IN, sham, as round 67 merged. Drafting CI1 changed one thing, so it comes back to you before the freeze. A code fact: the pull toward a valued thing's missing pieces already exists in v13/v14. _material_wants wants each missing raw material of a valued recipe whose thing is not held, at that recipe's value; _wanted_parts values a missing made part at 0.8 x the value of what it goes into; and RF1 saw all 53 started kinds gather a missing input. So round 67's part (b), the pull, is not new. What is missing is keeping the pieces: in v14 a held piece leaves by another make (actions.make), by being set down (put, chosen in 0.117 of wanting weeks), by lighting a fire or tipping embers (fiber, wood, char), by baking at a fire (soft earth), by gifts, and by wear. The draft law IN: one aim at a time, forming when a recipe is known, valued above the existing 0.05 floor, and its thing not held fresh (the most valued such recipe). While held, a piece it needs, up to the number needed, is not set down, not given, and not used in another make of lower value; a make of higher value interrupts the aim. Survival uses (lighting a fire, tipping embers) are never blocked. It ends when the inputs are in hand (the make option then appears as now), the value falls to 0.05 or below, the recipe is forgotten, or a higher-valued make interrupts it. No new pull, no new weight, no time limit. LG: the make option (inside 'if grown:') is offered to children 3 and over exactly as to the grown, with their own diligence and hunger; the rest of the grown block (put, heat, fill) stays for the grown. Harm guards per arm against v14: starvation per 1,000 living-years (ratio above 1.25 with the lower bound above 1), killing (difference above 0.05 per 1,000 living-years), adult health (below -5), living-weeks (below 0.90), extinctions (3+ extra), child deaths per 1,000 child-years, and gifts to kin's children. Co-primary: remaking, the share of world-new kinds made again by their inventor within 5 years. Questions: (1) Is IN as 'keeping the pieces only' the right minimal law, given that the pull exists, or does it now miss something essential (for example the order of making parts first)? (2) LG: only the make option, or the whole grown block? (3) The co-primary: remaking per new kind was 1 in about 3,000 in FF3 (near zero), so is a better co-primary RF1's funnel (the share of wanting kinds reaching a complete set), with remaking described? (4) Any missing harm guard, and the guard limits for child deaths and gifts to kin's children (proposed: the same ratio rule as starvation, 1.25 with the lower bound above 1)? (5) Your estimate (0-100%) that CI1 as you would freeze it is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
