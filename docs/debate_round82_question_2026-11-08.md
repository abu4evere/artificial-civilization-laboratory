## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 82 (ACL, 8 November 2026). The creator decided that ACL's people get a neural-network brain: 'I think our current structure is more predetermined ... bob's brain will emerge from neural network'. After reading Polyworld (Yaeger 1994), Hinton and Nowlan 1987, Najarro and Risi 2020, Miconi 2018, Pritzel 2017 (episodic control), Ndousse 2021 and Bhoopchand 2023 (social learning needs memory, experts, attention to others and costly solo exploration), the approved direction is: program the body, not the decisions (drives become feelings: the existing signal r = reward + 2 x mood change); a small network decides; genes carry the starting weights and the learning-rule numbers and evolve (Baldwinian: learned weights are not inherited); fast learning within a life; people see what others nearby did and how it went; deep time with selection only by survival and children. Today, in mind.decide, each candidate option (action plus a target chosen by per-action rules) gets a hand-written instinct utility u plus a learned habit value q (a table of 32 situations x 19 actions), then a softmax pick. Proposed first test (NB1, in a copy of the frozen nature v17): the network scores each candidate option from raw features only (the body: energy, health, food held, thirst, cold, age stage, kin and dependents near; the option: action kind, distance, simple target features; others: the share of people nearby who did that action last week and their mood change after it), NOT from u; hidden layer of 16; about 700 weights; softmax pick as now; within-life learning by a reward-modulated Hebbian (three-factor) rule on the chosen option's eligibility, with an evolved rate; children inherit the average of both parents' genome weights plus small Gaussian mutation. Open questions: (a) start: (A) pure network from random genomes, with dead worlds restarted from the genomes of the longest-lived people of the last attempt (selection by survival only), or (B) a hybrid start where u still counts, times an inherited gene that evolution can turn down, so the network can take over (no restarts needed); which is less predetermined yet viable? (b) should per-action target choice (which tile, which person) stay rule-based in NB1 and move to the network later? (c) learning rule: three-factor Hebbian, or REINFORCE-like with a baseline, or add an episodic memory (PB1b/PB2 showed memory helps) from the start? (d) deep time: a 200-year world has only 8-10 generations; is 1,000 years (about 40-50 generations, about 2 hours per world on our cloud) enough for a first test, or how long? (e) primary outcome and guards: survival and population against v17 over the same years, the trend across generations (do later generations do better?), and descriptives (making, inventions, remaking); what would show 'the brain improves by itself'? (f) the biggest way this could fail or be quietly predetermined. (g) your estimate (0-100%) that NB1 is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
