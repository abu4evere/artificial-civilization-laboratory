## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 84 (ACL, 11 November 2026). ACL is an agent-based hunter-gatherer simulation whose people now have an inherited, evolving neural-network brain. NB2 (final) gave that brain every action the body allows (the old hand-written rule-brain had hidden many acts behind thresholds such as 'attack only with a grudge'), let it see the facts, and learn by actor-critic and from others; the old instinct scores stay as an inborn bias weighted by an evolving gene g. Result: not viable - 13 of 16 worlds died out (old minds 4), and even the version with learning off collapsed (9 of 16). A read-only autopsy by exact replay found: killed is 17.7% of adult deaths (old minds 0.5%) and 33% of teens'; 55-64% of attacks had no grudge, only 3% of attackers were hungry, 20% of attacks hit children under 12, 95% were chosen (not retaliation); the world gives the attacker +0.1 feeling on every attack (a law written for the old brain's grudge-gated revenge); starvation is 33% of adult deaths (old minds 21%) and about a quarter of those who starved had kin within reach holding more than twice their need; children gave food in 3% of weeks (old minds 10%); old age is 18% of adult deaths (old minds 46%). The creator approved adding human feelings - the body, not the decisions; each feeling is a change in the weekly feeling number r = reward + 2 x mood change, each with an inherited gene (start value, range 0-2) so evolution can tune it: (0) remove the +0.1 attack reward and the kill bonus; (1) empathy E: r -= E x weighted mean distress of people within reach (distress = (hunger + 1 - health/100)/2, +1 if hurt by violence this week; weights kin and partner 1, affection > 0.3 0.5, others 0.2); (2) guilt G: after one's own attack +0.3, kill +1.0 (x2 for kin), noticed theft +0.15; r -= G x guilt; guilt x 0.9 each week; (3) love of one's own child L: r += L x mean over own children under 6 of (fed and healthy and with their carer), +0.1 x L for a week caring for or giving to one's own child; (4) approval and shame A: r += A x sum of the week's changes in others' affection toward one, a new grudge against one negative; (5) curiosity C: r += C x 0.1 / (1 + times this action was done in this situation); (6) an inborn reluctance to harm H: the instinct score of every attack and steal option gets -H (start 1.0), not scaled by g. Arms (16 worlds each, 600 years): c old minds; n2 NB2 unchanged; f0 no attack reward; fH f0 + reluctance; f all feelings; f-E all but empathy. Please answer briefly and concretely: (a) does the autopsy support this list, and what is missing or wrong (for example a feeling for being hurt, fear of retaliation, hunger of one's own children, or anything that would just re-impose the old rules)? (b) start values and ranges for the genes, chosen before any data, and how to keep them from being outcome-tuning; (c) is the inborn reluctance a 'rule in disguise', and is that acceptable if evolution can set H to 0? (d) the arms: are these the right ablations, and what primary outcome and reading (viability against c, and what else)? (e) risks: could feelings make people passive (never hunt, never defend), or could empathy for strangers stall the group? (f) your probability (0-100%) that f is viable against c (no more than 3 extra extinct worlds and population ratio upper bound >= 0.80).
