# Living inside one of ACL's people: a hand-played probe (10 November 2026)

**What this is.** A probe, not a study, and not nature. The creator (Telegram, 10 November 2026, 15:24): "another claude
lives inside acl as an agent and then discovered some problems that we haven't seen before. So what i am asking just
live inside acl as one agent just do what they do and don't show that ur ai". So the assistant took the controls of one
person in a **throwaway world** and lived week by week by hand.

**What was and was not changed.** Nothing in `acl/` was touched. The mind still ran in full for this person (attention,
habits, the weights, the thoughts); the only difference is that the assistant picked which of the weighed options the
body carried out, instead of the draw picking it. No study, no findings about nature, no nature change. The world is
frozen at v17 until the review on **7 December 2026**; everything below is **noted for that review**, not acted on.

The tool: `tools/mind_views/inhabit.py` (new, this session).

    python tools/mind_views/inhabit.py start --seed 7103 --years 20
    python tools/mind_views/inhabit.py look            # this week through their eyes; saves nothing
    python tools/mind_views/inhabit.py act --option K  # carry out one option; the week runs; saves

`look` recovers the **whole** weighed menu by watching the one call `mind.decide` makes to `thought.steer(p, options)`,
which receives every option with its real target and its weight. This matters: a person's own record, `p.considered`,
keeps only the best four plus the one drawn, so reading that alone makes the menu look far shorter than it is. The
assistant's first impression this session ("there is no `make` option") was wrong for exactly that reason, and was
corrected by this before anything was written down.

## What it felt like from inside, and what the code says

**1. Every social option outweighs every productive one.** Week 1042, fed (food 20.8), rested (energy 94), holding
stone 18.5, fiber 12.7 and five made things, with twelve recipes known: the top nine of twenty-two options were *give
food to someone* or *talk with someone* or *walk to the water* (give 0.92, 0.82; join 0.69; talk 0.63…). *Make a blade*
sat fourteenth at 0.38, *pick up clay* seventeenth at 0.32. Making was available all along — it is simply never what the
week is about. Living it, the pull is unmistakable: a healthy adult's week is eat, give, talk.

**2. Two weeks of deliberate work did build something, and that part works.** Blade (week 1042) → spear (week 1043),
each step using the last. Cumulative building inside one life is possible the moment somebody *chooses* it. That is the
clearest thing the probe found: the world permits it; the weights make it rare.

**3. What you make is not what you knew, when the parts differ.** The recipe held was
`((fiber+flint/flint)#fiber+wood)#fiber`. What came out of the probe's hands was
`((fiber+flint/stone/stone/stone/stone/stone/stone)#fiber+wood)#fiber` — a *different kind*, because the parts in hand
were substituted by their properties (OD1's doing, which is wanted). After both makes, `p00027`'s recipes were still the
same twelve, and `p.itemval` had no entry for either new kind (checked by reading the saved world).

**4. Deliberate making never records what it actually produced.** In `acl/actions.py` the only call to
`knowhow.remember_recipe` is at line 633, inside the **play** path ("fiddle with what you have"). The `make` path does
not call it. So a new kind that comes out of purposeful work is not remembered as a recipe; new kinds enter memory
through idle fiddling. (This sits beside RF1's finding that most new kinds came from people under 12 — children play
most.)

**5. So the value a thing earns can land where nobody can pass it on.** Teaching rides on talk
(`acl/actions.py:782` → `knowhow.teach` → `information.show_how`): the teacher offers only recipes or beliefs valued
above 0.15, and then **one of their best three at random**. Value is learned per signature
(`knowhow.py:56`, keyed by the made thing's sig). If use teaches you that the thing in your hands is good, that value
attaches to a signature that is in nobody's recipe book — not even your own. The recipe you could teach stays at its old
worth.

**6. Talk passed gossip and a water place, not know-how.** Week 1044's events: two `told` (who was born, who paired),
one `vouched` about a mark, and one `learn … "spot": "w:7,25", "how": "told"`. Rasin took a place, not a recipe.

## Where this leaves things

Nothing here is a finding about nature: one person, one seed, chosen by hand. Three of the six points (1, 4, 5) are
checkable mechanisms in the code rather than impressions, and each bears on the creator's open problem "knowledge
doesn't spread" (RF1: 0 of about 11,500 new kinds was ever made by anyone but its inventor).

They are **noted for the 7 December review**, as the freeze requires. Two of them are brain-shaped rather than
world-shaped, which is where the work is going anyway: the weights that make a week social, and the fact that nobody
keeps a project across weeks unless something outside them chooses it.

The probe tool stays, so any session can sit in a person's week again.
