## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 62 (ACL, 5 November 2026): what follows PB2? PB2 (final, recomputed blind by GPT and Gemini) tested an episodic brain in a copy of nature v13: each adult keeps a memory of own weeks (inputs, action, 9 observable changes, the learning signal r) and of others' visible weeks; arm P replaces the habit value q in choices by the memory's own-experience estimate r-hat (exact cell mean, else the 8 nearest own weeks; scaled to q's running spread); G adds a seasonal goal chosen by learning progress on 7 observable outputs (a curiosity-sized bonus); G0 a random goal; 32 seeds, 170 years, brain from year 100, measured in years 130-170. Results: validity passes (correlation with r: r-hat 0.90, q 0.59); the frozen primary failed: G - P -0.0007 [-0.0020, +0.0005], G - G0 +0.0002 [-0.0009, +0.0013], so goals add nothing; but the reported steering effect is large: M3 (gather-then-make chains completed, beyond matched chance) 0.0034 -> 0.0210, P - c +0.0176 [0.0157, 0.0194]; living-weeks +28% [24%, 34%], no extinction; makes per adult-year x4; world-new kinds 37 -> 69 per world; acts aimed at people +4.9 points (care x6, courting x3); food per adult slightly lower (-0.29 [-0.65, +0.08]); M2 (sequence structure) lower; R (a new kind made again within 5 years) still near 0 (P: 5 of 2,214, all by inventors). A confound stated before data: q carries a one-step look-ahead (discount 0.8 on next week's best habit) and r-hat has none, so P - c mixes 'a better predictor' with 'no look-ahead'; a clean control exists: v13 with discount 0. Questions: (1) Should the next step be a frozen test of memory steering itself as a candidate general law (primary P against v13 on M3, guards first, new seeds), and must it include the discount-0 control (and/or a P+ arm with a one-step look-ahead through the memory) to separate the two causes? Which primary and minimum effect? (2) Why did the goal add nothing, and is another goal design worth testing now, or should goals wait? (3) R stays near 0 in every arm: which general condition (no named inventions, no rewards for inventing) is most likely missing for people to make a new kind again on purpose, and should it be tested before or after memory steering? (4) Your estimate (0-100%) that your recommended next study is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
