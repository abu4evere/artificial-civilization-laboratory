## Step 2: the design
- **The council's proposal:** since B = q − G, the change in B over time is Δq − ΔG.
  - H1 predicts G rises.
  - H2 predicts G stays flat while q rises.
  - A third case is G falling.
- **The primary is ΔG,** composition-standardized, between years 100–124 and 175–199. The reading is "returns rising",
  "stable", "falling" or "mixed" against ±0.05.
- **Secondaries:** Δq, the decade means, newborns' starting values by birth decade, B by cohort, and tools' share.
- **GPT: accept with one change, 95%.**
