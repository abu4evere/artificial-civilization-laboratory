## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council round 66 (ACL, 5 November 2026): after LH1 and VA1. LH1 (final; v13 / e / m / sham, 32 new paired seeds, 200 years, years 100-200; recomputed blind by GPT, Gemini and DeepSeek's script) tested two fairer habit look-aheads, as round 65 asked. Neither is supported. e (the real next choice, ES2's rule): starvation per 1,000 living-years 4.52 -> 6.22, ratio 1.377 [1.267, 1.496] (hunger guard, limit 1.25, raised); killing +0.035 per 1,000 living-years (below the 0.05 guard); living-weeks x1.29; makes x3.2. m (the experienced value of the next situation): starvation x1.750 [1.562, 1.939], killing +0.19 [+0.10, +0.31] (both raised); makes x5.7. With HE1 (no look-ahead: making x6.1, starvation x1.64), three rules show one pattern: whatever releases making raises starvation. An exploratory check after the data: population does not predict the starvation rate within arms, and adjusting for it leaves the effects unchanged, so crowding does not explain it. Remakes stayed at 2 and 2 against 16,089 and 25,279 new kinds. VA1 (final; recomputed blind by GPT and Gemini, and by DeepSeek's script): value is not the bottleneck. 82% of new kinds are used by their inventor before the recipe is lost; similar experience predicts usefulness modestly (r 0.297 vs the plain mean's 0.267). An exploratory follow-up (870 kinds, years 30-55): the made thing wears out (98.7% gone within 5 years, median 32 weeks), so a second one is needed. But recipes are lost only at invention when the 12-recipe memory is full (58% early, 13% later), and then always. When the recipe is still known and valued and the thing is gone (261 cases), the inputs came together only twice, with 1 remake. Also: when the memory is full, a tie at value 0 is settled by the recipe's name in alphabetical order (knowhow.remember_recipe: min of (value, name)). Made kinds are named '(a+b)#binder', so the more nested kind sorts first and is forgotten; this happened in 129 of 130 ties. Human evidence (Anderson & Schooler 1991): the chance a memory is needed falls with time since last use. Questions: (1) Should the look-ahead line close for now (no tuning), or is there a principled next step? (2) For remaking: first a read-only funnel of why the inputs never come together (is a gather option for a missing input offered, chosen, completed; are the missing inputs raw materials, made parts or binders; what wins instead), or go straight to a frozen test of an intention law (a held aim toward a state one's own experience valued, kept across weeks and steering the steps toward it)? (3) The tie-break by name: is replacing it with 'on a tie, the longest-unused recipe is forgotten' a fault fix (an arbitrary rule that biases against complexity) or a new law needing a full study with a sham? (4) Your estimate (0-100%) that your recommended next study is valid and informative. Please answer briefly, point by point.
