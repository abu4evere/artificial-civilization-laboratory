# Debate round 5 (27 October 2026): which study first, after MK1 and TF1? (opinions, not sign-off)

**The creator:** "ask gpt and gemini which one to do first". The same text went to GPT and Gemini, with Claude as the
council. The candidates:
1. a **read-only split of what raised the other options' scores** (U_other, MK1), by action, by habit against instinct,
   and by the number of option entries;
2. A4 + A2: new things not deleted at once, and things able to lie in the world;
3. C1: learning by watching someone make or use a tool;
4. A1: handing over made things;
5. B1: materials on piles retrievable.

## Both: (1) first, and no nature change yet
- **GPT:** "my vote is unequivocally (1) ... MK1 has changed my priorities. I previously ranked destructive eviction
  first." The mystery is no longer why tools seem worthless, but why competing actions become so dominant. GPT warned:
  "Otherwise we risk building a richer world around a choice mechanism that becomes less willing to act as the world
  becomes more social."
- **Gemini:** (1) first, "zero nature risk". If the softmax is flooded with options, adding new actions (such as giving
  things) would add even more entries and make it worse.

**After (1):**
- **GPT:** A2/A4, C1, A1, B1, but conditional on (1). If option count dominates, look at the choice architecture
  first.
- **Gemini:** A4/A2, then C1.

## Council checks
- **Is it a mechanical artefact (more options as more people are near)?**
  - Gemini first said the risk is "extremely high".
  - Arithmetic (Claude and GPT, independently): at T = 0.2 a rise of 0.64 in U_other means about 24.5 times the
    competing softmax weight. Doubling equal-score entries adds only 0.14. The decision keeps at most the 12 nearest
    people, and population grew only 39%.
  - So option count can explain only part; rising scores per entry must carry the rest. **Gemini revised.**
- **Vicarious worth vs learning a recipe by watching:** GPT says these are separate capacities to test separately;
  Gemini had folded them together. **Gemini accepted.**
- **GPT's design for (1):**
  - for every making opportunity, record the competing weight by action family (entry counts and total softmax
    weight), instinct against habit, and the multiplicity effect;
  - then diagnostic calculations only: making's probability with options grouped by family, and with early option
    counts but late utilities, and the reverse.
  - Pre-register the grouping rules, and do not treat different recipients or resources as identical just because the
    action has the same name.
- **GPT on TF1:** wear (0.58) and being built into bigger things (0.39) are not necessarily knowledge loss: "Incorporation
  can be technological reuse." The 12% of new things deleted the same week matters more for novelty than for the stock
  of tools.

## Predictions on record
- **GPT:** the rise in competing utilities explains more than option-count growth, but multiplicity contributes
  measurably. Look at social actions growing with density, and whether foraging's instinct utility rises with need or
  ecology.
- **Gemini (revised):** the same.
