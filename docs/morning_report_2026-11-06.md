## What we learned tonight
1. **The look-ahead is not what blocks cumulative culture (LH1, final).**
   - Two fairer ways for habits to look one week ahead both made people invent 3 to 6 times more, but both raised
     hunger deaths, and one raised killings. Nothing changed in the live world.
   - With HE1, three rules now show the same trade-off: freeing up making costs food.
2. **Why inventions are never made again (RF1, final).**
   - When a grown inventor wants their thing again, they start gathering, but the pieces get used up or lost before
     the set is complete. This is "pursuit fails", 80% of cases.
   - The missing pieces are mostly made parts, which need their own making first.
   - **The surprise: 85% of new inventions are made by children, in play, and in ACL only people aged 12 and over may
     "make again".** Studies of hunter-gatherer children (Lew-Levy et al. 2017) report children making their own simple
     tools by about age 4–5.
3. **A small fault found in four memories.** When a memory is full and several items are equally valued, the item's
   *name* decides which is forgotten. For new inventions, more complex things lose those ties. In one world this
   decided 64% of recipe forgetting and 34% of mark forgetting. FF3 is testing the fix now.
