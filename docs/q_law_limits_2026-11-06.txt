ACL council question (6 Nov 2026). Context: ACL is an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers; the rule is that nature (laws) is programmed but culture/history must emerge, never be written; laws must be general conditions (physics, body, general abilities), never outcomes. The creator says the world looks too peaceful and asked us to re-check every law for hand-written limits, because laws are the base everything else builds on. Records: in study LH1 (32 worlds, years 100-200) deaths were 2 by killing vs 2,029 by starvation. Each week every person picks ONE action by softmax over scored options; an option that is not added to the list can never be chosen. My read-only audit of the code (nature v14) found these hand gates: (1) attack is offered only if the person already holds a grudge > 0.15, and grudges come only from harm already done, so no attack from hunger, rivalry, a contested kill, a mate, a place, or fear; (2) stealing is offered only if the victim holds > 4 food, thief hunger > 0.3, victim not kin and not partner, with a fixed penalty -0.25 - 0.4*fear; only food can be stolen, always half; (3) taking another person's food cache only when hunger > 0.4; (4) everyone hunting the same tile is automatically one party and the meat is split evenly, hide and bone by coin; nobody can keep a kill or drive others off; (5) pairs last for life (a pair ends only at a death), no leaving, no rivalry, courting only between unpaired adults of opposite sex, not kin, at most 15 years apart, acceptance = fixed threshold 0.55 then a 60% coin; children only from partners; (6) after a noticed theft the victim's counter-attack is rolled by formula, not chosen in the victim's own decision; (7) care only for kin, partner or affection > 0.3, never a stranger; (8) flee only from someone distrusted (trust < -0.4) and about as strong; (9) no group act except hunting on one tile: no sides, no joining a fight, no defending another person; (10) no territory, nobody can guard a cache or a spring; (11) no threat or display short of a blow; (12) teaching happens automatically inside talk, the teacher does not choose what or whom; (13) imitation copies all 608 habit values at once toward a respected model; (14) written rewards: giving pays 0.1 + 0.2 kindness + 0.2 kin, a kill pays 0.3 temper - 0.2 kindness, pairing +0.5, talk 0.1 + 0.1 sociability; (15) forage/hunt targets are only the current tile, home and 6 random tiles, make offers only the best recipe, one action a week; (16) 3 made things, 20 materials, containers hold only water or embers, exact recipes; (17) age gates (hunt, make, steal, attack, care from 12; court 16-49). My proposed direction: replace the gates with one general law: any person may try any act (take, keep, strike, guard, threaten, follow, leave a partner) on any person or thing within reach, weighed by the same need, temper, ties, fear and learned values, with outcomes from strength, who sees it and how others respond; one change at a time, each a frozen test in a copy with a sham. Please answer briefly and concretely: (a) which of the 17 are real hand limits that shape history, and which are defensible as physics/body/instinct? (b) which 3 should be opened first, in what order, and why? (c) what risks (e.g. extinction by violence, tuning by the back door) and what safeguards or harm checks should the frozen tests use? (d) is anything I missed, or anything I called a limit that is not? Say where you disagree with me.
