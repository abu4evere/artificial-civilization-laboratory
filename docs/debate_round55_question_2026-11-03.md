# ACL council, round 55: other ways to build ACL toward Bob (3 November 2026)

The same question goes to GPT, Gemini and DeepSeek. Your answer is an opinion, not sign-off.

## The creator's question
"Do we have other options to build ACL differently?"

The goal is Bob: an autonomous, cumulative mind **with its own drive**. The creator's words: "you maybe autonomous
cognitive mind but you do not have your own drive". So Bob is not a capable agent whose goals are handed to it. His
wants grow from being alive in a world, and he builds goals that were not built in. No LLMs inside ACL's people.

## What the studies show so far (live nature v12)
- **The mind:** habits are learned per situation × action (a tabular Q-learning with a max look-ahead), plus written
  pulls (hunger, fear, attachment, curiosity) and temperament genes. People choose from a fixed list of about 20
  actions with targets picked by formula.
- **Knowledge leaks:** about 98% of what a person knows is lost at death (LS2).
- **Habits pull people back to common actions** (CO1/CO2: habit carries 0.87 of making's decline). Worlds without
  habit steering complete gather-then-make chains about 8 times more often, but survive less (AM1).
- **Late inventions are never made again** (UP1, AF1). Tools add about 1% of food (TR1).
- **Hunting returns 2% of foraging** (HN1). The world is forgiving.

## Options the assistant listed (not ranked by evidence yet)
1. **A different brain:** a small neural network per person that learns from experience and **predicts** outcomes (a
   world model), so it can plan and imagine. Still no LLM and no human data.
2. **Brains that evolve:** children inherit how their brain learns (curiosity, memory, learning speed); selection works
   over generations.
3. **Open actions:** a few general abilities (grab, hit, carry, combine, put, push), with physics deciding outcomes,
   instead of a menu and recipes.
4. **Fewer scripted feelings:** only bodily needs (energy, warmth, pain, fatigue) are given; feelings grow from
   learning.
5. **A signal channel with no meanings,** so shared meanings (a first language) can emerge.
6. **Much bigger and longer worlds** (thousands of people, thousands of years).

The assistant's suggested order: 1, then 3, then 2; 4–6 later. Each is to be tested as a frozen study, never tuned
toward an outcome.
