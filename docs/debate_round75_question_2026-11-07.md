## Follow-up (sent as one paragraph to all three, after checking their answers against the code)
Council round 75, follow-up with facts from the code (v15); please say briefly whether you revise. (a) Theft from children: the steal rule dates from the first commit; its comment is 'Steal: hunger, without kindness or love to hold you back'; its 'not a child' check gates the actor, as attack and flee also do (children do not start aggression); victims are filtered only by holding over 4 food, not kin (kin < 0.5) and not the partner, and the thief's kindness, affection and fear of the victim lower the score. No record says children were meant to be off-limits. A rule 'never rob a child' would write a moral norm into nature, which ACL leaves to culture. So I read it as not a fault by ACL's definition (a rule that does not do what its own comment or plan says). Do you agree, or is there a physical (not moral) reason to change it? (b) Pots: containers already help keep food: the carry limit (100 food) is multiplied by 1 + contain (a pot of contain 60 lets one carry 160); loose food rots 4% a week, a quarter as fast near a fire's smoke; so a family can already hold about 5-7 weeks of its need. The pot does not slow rot. So point 8 was overstated: storage is not what binds a lone parent; production is. So foraging on one's own tile every week is break-even when fed, and runs down only when moving, walking to water or starving.
