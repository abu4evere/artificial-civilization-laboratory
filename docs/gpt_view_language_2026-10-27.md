# Language: how could words emerge in ACL? (GPT's and Gemini's opinions, not sign-off)

**The creator's point (27 October 2026):** language may be the biggest driver of human development. ACL lacks it.
Information passes "like telepathy", and **as long as telepathy works, there is no reason for language to emerge.** So
conditions must be created under which signals become useful. The same question went to both; Claude was council.

## GPT
- **Strong support.** "Giving people signals is not giving them language. We need to see whether they discover useful
  meanings themselves."
- **Start with places, and water only first,** after a read-only audit of the information channels.
- **The law:** replace direct water-location transfer with about 8 meaningless tokens, emitted while attending to
  something observable. Listeners learn associations; nobody inherits a dictionary.
  - "Shared attention must be physically grounded … The program must not secretly tell the listener what the speaker
    intended."
  - **Caution:** "if signals refer only to water versus no water, that's not yet communicating where water is."
- **Tests:**
  - meaning above chance;
  - agreement between people;
  - children learning the code;
  - usefulness (finding water they couldn't see).
- **Primary outcome:** communicating unseen water locations, against chance and scrambled signals.
- **Arms:** v7, learned signals, a sham, and a shuffled-association negative control. Also independent groups.
- **Risk:** survival may fall. "Fair as an experiment … not automatically acceptable as a permanent Nature change."
  Freeze a demographic safety rule, and **don't compensate** by adding water or making agents smarter.
- **Chances:**

  | outcome | chance |
  |---|---|
  | the audit finds the telepathic channels | 95% |
  | useful associations are learned | 65% |
  | a shared code emerges | 40% |
  | children keep it | 25% |
  | open-ended language from this law alone | under 5% |

- "We should not give them the words … even if their first language has only two words."

## Gemini
- **Law 33 is the right start:** a concrete referent, survival value, clear shared attention.
- **The design:** 8 tokens; shared attention within sight of the same thing; an association table, starting at 0; the
  speaker emits its strongest token for what it sees; listeners strengthen co-occurring pairs; out of sight, a token is
  decoded as the best-associated referent.
- **Metrics:** communicative success above chance; agreement within a cluster; fidelity across generations.
- **Controls:**
  - v7 telepathy;
  - random tokens;
  - **"silence"** (telepathy removed and no signals), to measure the cost of losing the information.
- **Risk:** moderate to high early on; "a fair cost", since the pressure to communicate must be genuine.

## Council
- **Both agree:**
  - audit first;
  - one channel (places; GPT: water only);
  - about 8 meaningless tokens with meanings learned from shared attention;
  - no dictionary;
  - success = useful communication above chance, shared across people and passed to children, against scrambled
    controls;
  - survival risk is real, so a frozen safety rule is needed.
- **Adopted from Gemini:** the **"silence" arm** (telepathy removed, no signals). It shows what language must recover.
- **Watch:** Gemini mentions agents knowing x,y coordinates. Signals must not carry built-in coordinates (that would be
  a map language given by us). GPT's caution about "where" needs a design answer before freezing, perhaps a direction
  relative to what both can see.

## The creator's rule (27 October 2026)
"we cannot give them map for sure it means cheating." **Signals carry no built-in meaning, no coordinates and no map
language.** "Where" can only travel through honest physical means the listener must perceive or follow:
- pointing (a direction the listener sees);
- leading (walking together);
- landmarks named by words the people invented themselves.

The first language law need not include "where" at all.
