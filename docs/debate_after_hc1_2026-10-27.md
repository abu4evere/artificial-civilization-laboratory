  - It repeated "99% of new invention types vanish".

## Council
- **Both choose (2).** GPT's primary and rule are adopted. Gemini's "0% deletion" is used as the manipulation check:
  it is guaranteed by the code, so it cannot be the test. Gemini's population ratio (0.90) is used as a flag.
- **Checked:** tools on a dead body do **not** decay in v7, and kin pick them up passively when standing there
  (`bodies_week`). So OP1's things on the ground do not decay either, and pick-up is passive with room in hand. This is
  the existing precedent, with no new number (GPT's caution on decay is met).
- **Corrected again:** TF1's 98.8% is the share of the **vanished** things that were new kinds, not "99% of new
  invention types".
