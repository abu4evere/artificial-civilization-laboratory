# E13R autopsy: questions (written before any autopsy data)

**Read-only.** Replays E13R's Arm A (control) and Arm B (copying any better-fed adult), seeds 501–516, with a probe
checked not to touch the world (state hash) and replays checked against the saved runs (population at every 10-year
sample). Nothing here changes the world or the verdict of E13R; it explains it. Ordered by the creator's decision,
"autopsy first", after E13R.

## Part 1: the children puzzle

E13R: eight points more carers entered winter holding 20+ food in B, yet children's winter starvation was not lower
(1.39× pooled; seeds split 9 worse, 7 better). E9.2 had found a stocked carer protects children five-fold.

- **C1. Does a stocked carer still protect in B?** Children's winter starvation by their carer's food at the start of
  winter (no carer, under 5, 5–20, 20–50, 50+), A against B.
- **C2. Composition.** Child-winters in each band; dependents per carer; births per year; A against B. If protection
  holds within bands, the difference must come from who the children are and whom they depend on.
- **C3. Who starved.** For each starved child: their carer's band at the start of winter, whether the carer was alive at
  the end, the carer's last known food, the child's age, how many dependents the carer had, and the week of winter.
- **C4. Where a stocked carer's food goes.** For carers holding 20+ at the start of winter: food left 4, 8 and 12 weeks
  in, and food given to people who are not their dependents, A against B.

## Part 2: is the spread habit linked to preparation?

E13R: B had 2.9× the planner-autumns but repeat planning barely above the control, and planners' neighbours unchanged.
Copying updates were spread evenly over the seasons (27/26/25/22%).

- **H1. Is the extra foraging autumn-specific?** Adults' share of weeks spent foraging, by season, A against B. A
  preparation practice would raise autumn relative to the other seasons; a general habit raises all seasons alike.
- **H2. Do people enter winter with more, and keep it?** Adults' food at the start of winter (median, share holding 20+
  and 80+) and at the end of winter, A against B.
- **H3. Do adults go short less in winter?** Share of adult winter-weeks spent going short, and adult starvation deaths.
