# E13R autopsy: findings

Read-only replays of E13R's Arm A and Arm B, seeds 501–516. Probe read-only in both codes; all 32 replays matched the
saved E13R runs. Questions in `questions.md` (written before the data); tables in `findings_tables.md`. Descriptive;
nothing scored. Seed agreement is given with every pooled difference.

## The children

- **C1. Stocked carers still protect, in both arms.** A carer holding 20–50 at the start of winter cuts a child's risk
  five- to tenfold against one holding under 5 (A 0.18% against 2.04%; B 0.38% against 2.85%), as E9.2 found. **But at
  every level of the carer's food, children in B starved more** (1.4 to 2 times). The puzzle is not that stocking
  stopped working; something in the copying worlds raises winter risk at the same stock.
- **C2. Composition moved the right way.** More child-winters had a carer holding 20–50 in B (43.0% against 36.5%; 14 of
  16 seeds). That alone should have lowered deaths; it was outweighed by the higher risk within every band.
- **C3. Who starved:** mostly infants (median age under one), late in winter (median week 11 of 13). Most had a carer
  alive and out of food (A 68 of 110, B 103 of 162); more of B's had a carer already dead (45 of 162 against 26 of
  110); B's had carers with more dependents (median 3 against 2).
- **C4. Stocked carers in B gave more away.** 54% of carer-winters with 20+ gave food to people outside their care,
  against 43%, and gave more (mean 10.1 against 7.3). Their own stores ran down at the same pace in both arms.

## The habit

- **H1. Year-round, not autumn.** Adults foraged 2–4% more in every season (spring 1.03, summer 1.02, autumn 1.04,
  winter 1.02; 9 to 12 of 16 seeds). Preparation would raise autumn above the rest. The copied habit is general.
- **H2. More food into winter, none of it kept.** Adults holding 20+ at the start of winter rose from 20.3% to 26.8%,
  in 16 of 16 seeds. By the end of winter the median was lower in B (6.3 against 6.8; lower in 9 of 16).
- **H3. Adults did not fare better in winter.** Going short 9.9% of winter-weeks against 9.1%; starvation deaths 226
  against 162, but only 8 seeds higher against 5 lower.

## Reading

The copied habit gives people more food going into winter and nothing that makes it last. Stocked people share more
widely, winter still ends with empty hands, and the infants of carers with many dependents die near its end. The
evidence fits copying doing a little harm, but the worlds split (9 to 7 for children, 8 to 5 for adults), so the claim
is only that copying did not help winter.

What ACL's people lack is not getting food. It is making food last until spring.

**For the roadmap.** The branch fixed before E13R's result says to test whether this habit persists (stage 4). The
autopsy suggests that would test the persistence of something that is not preparation and does not help winter. Whether
to keep or change the branch is the creator's decision, to be recorded with this reason.
