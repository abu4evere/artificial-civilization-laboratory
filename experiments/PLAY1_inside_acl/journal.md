## Limits found from inside (for the council; each to check in code before it counts)
1. **Fatigue gates food.** An option is dropped when energy is below its cost: forage 25, explore 30, join 20, plus 4
   a tile. A starving person whose energy is low cannot even look for food, and must rest a week first. Recovery is
   halved by hunger. Helping someone (care) or stealing costs the energy that would have fed the family next week.
   - Is "you are too tired to forage" right for someone starving? Real foragers forage hungry. The gate may help explain
     why hunger is ACL's main killer.
2. **Anyone can be robbed, children included.** The steal rule's "not a child" checks the **thief's** stage, not the
   victim's. Stealing from an 8-year-old was offered, weighted down only by kindness, fear and affection.
3. **There is no way to ask.** Starving with three children, there is no "ask for food", "beg" or "tell someone I am
   hungry". Help comes only if others choose to give.
4. **A noticed theft brought no visible reaction** that week. Conflict is gated by grudge (the law-limits audit, items
   1 and 6).
5. **No plan to reach a person.** My partner Meihe is away, and there is no option "go to Meihe". Only "join" toward
   spots of the world's choosing.
6. **Feeding my children is not a choice.** It happens automatically in the body phase, before I eat.
7. **Energy forces rest about half the time.** Recovery is 25 a week, halved when starving, and a forage week costs 25 plus 4 a tile. So foraging every week cannot be sustained. This matches FP1: grown people are active 36–45% of weeks.
8. **One person cannot feed a family of four alone.** A forage week gives 13–25 food against the family's need of about 21 (me 7, children 4 each, a 12-year-old 4), and only about every other week. The partner was 7 tiles away, well fed, and gave nothing. There is no option to go to him or to call him.
9. **Children foraging for themselves:** the 12-year-old was fed and healthy on his own foraging, while the 3- and 6-year-olds depended on me. This matches FP1's early self-sufficiency.
10. **A teacher cannot choose what to teach.** The lesson inside a talk is drawn among the speaker's three most valued things (`show_how`). I wanted to show my son how to make a cord, and there is no way to choose it (the law-limits audit, item 12). With LW1's wish, the learner would steer it, but the teacher still cannot.
11. **Reputation works.** My theft was retold by my own son within 5 weeks. Word of mouth carries who did what.
12. **A pot cannot keep food.** I made a clay pot (holds 60), but containers hold only water or embers. There is no option to store food in it for my children's lean weeks (the affordance audit's limit).
13. **Giving a thing: I choose whom, not what.** `hand_pick` chose the pot over the cord.
14. **Tools wear out quietly.** My one made thing was used up within weeks. I had to notice it myself, and then make again.
15. **Her own mind kept the family alive better than I did.** In 13 weeks it foraged 6 times and the family recovered (her health 39 → 70; the youngest 64 → 87). My human-style weeks (caring for a stranger, stealing, crafting, a gift trip) each cost a foraging week, and in this world a skipped forage week costs up to 14 health. **The world's margins are thin:** any week not spent on food is dangerous for a lone parent, which leaves little room for making, teaching or exploring. Is the margin right? That is FP1's question, seen from inside.
16. **She did find her partner on her own,** by her own mind's pull (`join` toward loved ones, NV2's last-seen rule), and gave him food. The partner did not seek out his starving family.

## Correction (7 November 2026, after checking the code for council round 75)
- **Limit 12 was overstated.** Containers do help keep food: the carry limit (100 food) is multiplied by 1 + contain
  (`matter.factor` "carry"; my pot of contain 60 would let me carry 160). Loose food rots 4% a week, a quarter as fast
  near a fire's smoke (`biology`). What a pot cannot do is slow rot, or hold food once set down. So storage was not what
  bound me as a lone parent; production was.
- **Limit 7 needs a qualifier.** Energy recovers 25 a week when fed (12.5 when starving), and a rest week adds 40 more
  (`actions._rest`). Foraging on one's own tile costs 25, so it is break-even when fed; it runs down only with moves
  (4 a tile), walks to far water (`weather`), or hunger.
- **Limit 2 is not a plain fault.** The steal rule is from the first commit; its comment is "hunger, without kindness or
  love to hold you back". Its "not a child" check gates the actor, as attack and flee do. No record says children were
  meant to be off-limits, and a ban would write a moral norm into nature.
