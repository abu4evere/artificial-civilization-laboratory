Blind recompute for a study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study NB2: when a neural-network brain gets real choices, sees the facts and learns with a critic and from others, does the network make the decisions?). Please compute only from the attached files; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = the old rule-based minds; s = the new neural setup with learning off; o = s plus simple REINFORCE learning; l = s plus actor-critic learning; n = l plus social learning (the full design). The same 16 seeds (25601-25616) in every arm; 600 years. Files: nb2_worlds.csv (per world: extinct 1/0; living_weeks summed over years 100-600; known_births = people born in years 100-600 whose outcome is known, and reached16 = those of them who reached age 16); nb2_g.csv (per neural world: mean g at years 1, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600; g is an inherited gene weighting the old instinct score, starting at 1; blank after extinction); nb2_garden.csv (phase 2: genomes from generations 1 and 18 of each n world put as all founders into fresh identical worlds; arms g1Z and g18Z with g held at 0 and learning off, g18L and g18N with learning on or off; per source world: known = children born by year 44, reached16). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw the 16 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, and recompute each quantity from the drawn worlds; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) extinct worlds per arm; (2) the population ratio of each neural arm to c = sum living_weeks / sum living_weeks; (3) survival to 16 per birth for each arm = sum reached16 / sum known_births, and the paired differences n - s (the primary), o - s, l - o, n - l, s - c, n - c; (4) the mean over worlds of g at each listed year for s, o, l, n (worlds with a value); (5) phase 2: for each source seed, reached16/known in g18Z minus g1Z, the mean difference with a bootstrap over source seeds; and the same for g18L minus g18N. The reading: 'not viable' if n has more than 3 extinct worlds more than c, or the upper bound of the n/c population ratio is below 0.80; else 'the network decides' if the lower bounds of n - s and of g18Z - g1Z are both above 0; else 'learning helps, but the instincts still carry it' if the lower bound of n - s is above 0; else 'no improvement shown'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached files with your code tool.
