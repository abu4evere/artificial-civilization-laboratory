# AP1 — why are couples apart? Findings (described)

Frozen as `AP1-frozen` before any code or data. Read-only: CT1's C and K1 worlds (seeds 8201–8328, nature v3) replayed
exactly on GitHub (run 39812729272) with a read-only observer. Tables: `results.md`, from `analyse_ap1.py` (committed
before data).

## Validity

- Zero check passed.
- All 256 worlds are present, and every replay equals CT1's saved world.
- The observer's apart weeks equal CT1's F1 count in every world.

**Valid.**

## What it shows

1. **Separation is short and routine, not a breakdown.** A separation lasts **5 weeks on average**; 37% last a single
   week, and **97% end with the couple back together**. Partners drift about 5 tiles apart, then come back.
2. **Mostly food, but not only.** In apart weeks, she is getting food (foraging, gathering, fetching water) 68% of the
   time and he 56%. By pair:
   - both getting food in different places: 39%;
   - one exploring, seeking or fleeing: 19% (him twice as often as her);
   - one caring for someone: 15%;
   - one resting: 3%;
   - other combinations, mostly one getting food while the other talks or courts: 24%.
3. **Nobody in particular leaves.** When a couple splits, she moved away 38% of the time, he 39%, both 23%.
4. **Apart weeks are hungrier weeks:** either partner is starving in 9.6% of apart weeks against 6.6% of together weeks.
   Described only: hunger may drive people apart in search of food, or separation may cost food. AP1 cannot tell which.
5. **K1 changes none of this.** Every share is within ±0.5 points of C.

## Against the prior (frozen; kept as written)

"Over half of apart weeks are both getting food; one moving away is second": **wrong.** Both getting food is the
largest single reason (39%), but not over half. Moving away is the second named reason (19%), behind the mixed
"other" (24%).

## What it means for ACL

Couples in ACL are like real foraging partners who split up during the day to find food and meet again. The
difference is that ACL has no camp to come back to at night, so on a weekly record they look "apart" most of the time.
That is not a fault to fix by force: HB1 (a fixed home) cost food, and MC1 (a camp that follows the couple) raised
births without saving worlds. There is no single cause to target; separation is spread across food, moving, care and
talk.

Following GPT's advice and the plan's spirit (narrow, time-bounded), **the partner and pair line stops here for now.**
Next, by the open list: another bottleneck, **cumulative culture** (nine in ten useful inventions lost) or
**delayed-return strategies** (storage and sowing failing). The creator decides.
