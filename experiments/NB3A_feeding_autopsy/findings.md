## What it means (the assistant's reading)
1. **The feeling brain is not bad at finding food.** It forages and eats as well as the old minds, or better.
2. **It has many more children per adult, and gives less outside the household.** With the same food per adult and
   44% more births, caregivers come up short more often, and children starve. The deaths are the same kinds as v17's,
   just 2.4 times as many.
3. **So ACL's neural people are caught in a demographic trap:** high fertility without the sharing that would carry
   it. In v17 the old brain's thresholds held both back (fewer courtships, more giving). Note that love of one's own
   child (L) rewards the well-being of one's own young, but nothing in the feelings weighs the cost of another child.
4. **For the next brain step (to the council):**
   - (a) a feeling for the household's food balance before having more children;
   - (b) a reason to share beyond the household (reciprocity, as approval A almost is);
   - or (c) the learning signal could count children's survival, not births.
   - Also, W1b and W1c show that the network kind itself lacks spatial structure. The instincts still carry the
     foraging. So a better brain needs both.
