# E6.5: Autopsy · where the discovery chain breaks

Watch-only. Seeds 302 and 304 were replayed from week 1 with probes that only read:
- every decision: was there an opportunity, was the step among the person's top four options, what did they choose;
- every time something was held in a fire;
- the life of the one rare thing in each world.

Both replays matched the saved worlds exactly at year 100 (`autopsy.py`; data in `output/autopsy_302.json` and
`output/autopsy_304.json`).

The chain: **Opportunity → Interaction → Transformation → Observation → Reuse → Tradition**

## Seed 304: the watertight container

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who made it? | Reibik (p00014), a founder of about 38, very sociable (0.83), not curious (0.35). |
| What happened? | Year 12, in play: hide bound to bone with fibre. Holding 53, tightness 60: it could hold about 5 weeks of water. |
| Did he notice? | Yes, as a carrier. He used it 104 times (felt 0.12–0.33 each time), and its worth to him rose to about 0.28. |
| Did a puzzle trigger? | No. |
| Did he reuse it? | Yes. He made it again in year 14 and carried it for years. |
| Did he teach anyone? | **Yes.** He taught 3 people in years 15–16. The recipe then passed on, slightly garbled, to 4 more people over 50 years, and lasted until year 72. Learners never made it themselves. |
| Did anyone put water in it? | **Never.** He stood at fresh water holding it for **102 adult-weeks**. Filling it was never among his top four options. He chose foraging (38), giving food (22), talking (21) and going to people (20). |
| Why did the chain stop? | **At Interaction.** The opportunity was there week after week. Nothing pulled him to try. |

Why nothing pulled: filling something with water pulls only as something untried. The pull is about 0.02 plus
a little for curiosity, times how much thirst they have known. Someone who lives by water has known almost
none, so the pull is about 0.04. Ordinary options pull 0.3–1. The person holding the vessel was never thirsty,
and the thirsty people had no vessel. Its payoff would come only later and elsewhere: water drunk weeks
afterwards, far from a spring. A world that teaches only by immediate experience cannot teach that.

## Verdict: which link broke

| Link | Heat (fired clay) | Water (container) |
|---|---|---|
| **Opportunity** | ❌ **Very rare.** 2–3 adult-weeks per century at a fire with a fireable thing; fires are rare and brief | ✅ Plenty: 102 weeks |
| **Interaction** | ❌ **Never chosen on purpose**; the one success was a child's random play | ❌ **Never chosen**: 0 of 102 |
| Transformation | ✅ Worked when it happened | not reached |
| Observation | ⚠ No use, no puzzle | ⚠ Valued as a carrier only; its water-holding was never seen |
| Reuse | ❌ The maker died at 4 | ✅ Made again and used 104 times (as a carrier) |
| Tradition | ❌ Lost in 16 weeks | ✅ Taught to 7 people over 60 years (as a carrier) |

1. **Possibility 1 (no opportunity) is true for heat, false for water.** Fire is rare. But water and a vessel met
   102 times.
2. **Possibility 2 (opportunity ignored) is true for both.** This is the link that broke in both chains. People
   try new things almost only in play, and adults play in about 1% of their weeks. A new kind of act whose payoff
   comes later and elsewhere has nothing to pull on.
3. **Possibility 3 (transformed but nobody cared)** happened once. The fired clay was never used, but its maker
   died before he could care.
4. **Possibility 4 (knowledge died) is not the general bottleneck.** The container's recipe was taught and
   outlived its maker by decades. Transmission works. What spread was a carrier, not a water vessel: the
   container's world-changing use (water anywhere, so dry land becomes livable) was never seen by anyone.

**In short:** the world contained a "raft-like" discovery: a thing that could change where people can live. It
stayed latent because using it that way requires deliberately trying an act with no immediate reward, and
nothing in these people, or in the world, ever makes them try it. The raft spread because crossing water showed
its value the moment it happened. A vessel never shows its value unless someone fills it.

## What the evidence points to (for the creator to decide; nothing built)

The broken link is between Opportunity and Observation. It can be mended from either side:
- **The world shows the property by itself,** the way crossing showed the raft (Law 124: uses must be experienced).
  Examples of physics, not scripts: rain falls into an open, tight hollow thing and leaves water in it; a shaped
  thing left beside a fire dries and hardens.
  The person then meets the result without having to choose it. This adds no intelligence and fits "conditions,
  not outcomes".
- **People try things in context more often:** handling what they hold with what is around them, beyond play.
  This changes the agents, which the creator has preferred not to do for now.
