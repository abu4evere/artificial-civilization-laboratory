## Limits

The possible surplus is an upper bound: it uses each person's own food per autumn foraging act and assumes the
plants would not run out if they foraged every week. With half the spring growth rate in autumn, heavier foraging
would deplete tiles faster. Even so, the margin is wide: the median possible surplus is more than the 150 a winter
takes, and the median person entered winter with 13.

## What this says

**This is World A.** Autumn offers enough to lay by for winter, nearly every year, for nearly everyone. The world
allows it. People do not do it. They forage when hungry, which in autumn is less than half the time; they give
food away, talk, and spend time with others; they never hold more than a few weeks' food; and they walk into
winter with almost nothing.

The bottleneck for ACL's biggest killer is not the world's ecology. It is that nothing in the people looks ahead
to a hunger that has not happened yet.
