# E8.1 — Why do people stop making? A value autopsy · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Written:** 2026-10-05, after E8 Arms 0/1 and before any E8.1 run. Not to be edited after the results.
**Type:** read-only. Replays of the E7 code (E8 Arm 0), seeds 301–304, 120 years, each checked against the saved
E7 run's population at every 10-year sample. The probe changes nothing (state hash checked).

**Why:** E8 Arm 1 freed the holding check and making still collapsed. Rule R2 only lets a person remake a held
thing they value above zero. If later generations value their things at about nothing, no rule about holding,
and no help with practice, can make them choose to make.

**Known from the code (not predictions):** the pull to make a recipe is 2.0 × its worth × (0.6 + 0.4 ×
diligence) × (1 − 0.5 × hunger), plus other pulls (a puzzle about it, a belief about a place and season, or the
recipe being a part of something wanted). It must exceed 0.05 to be considered at all, so a recipe valued below
about 0.03–0.04 by worth alone is never an option.

## Measures (every 4th week, adults 16+ only)

By generation (0 = founders, 1, 2, 3+) and by decade:
1. worth of the things a person holds: mean, and share at or below zero;
2. the highest worth among all recipes the person knows;
3. weeks in which the person could make something (has the materials for at least one known recipe);
4. in those weeks: how often "make" was among the four options weighed, how often it was chosen, and which
   actions were chosen instead (top 10);
5. in those weeks: the score of the chosen action against the largest pull "make" could have had from worth
   alone.

## Predictions

1. **Worth falls by generation.** In every seed, the mean worth of held things for generation 3+ adults is less
   than half that of founders.
2. **Much of it reaches nothing.** In generation 3+, at least 40% of held things are worth zero or less, pooled.
3. **Make is rarely even weighed.** In generation 3+ weeks where a make was possible, "make" is among the four
   options weighed in under 10% of them, pooled.
4. **What wins is everyday life.** In those weeks, foraging, talking, joining others and giving together account
   for at least 70% of what was chosen instead.
5. **The gap is large.** In those weeks the chosen action's score is at least 3× the largest pull "make" could
   have had from worth alone, at the median.
