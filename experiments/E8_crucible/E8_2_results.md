## Frozen predictions, scored

1. **confirmed** — tools add at most 2.0% of food foraged (prediction: under 10%).
2. **confirmed** — the median made thing returns less than twice its cost; in fact the median returns nothing
   through foraging.
3. **wrong** — adults had a week's need on hand in 69% of adult-weeks (prediction: at least 70%).
4. **confirmed** — hunting takes 0.07% of adult-weeks (prediction: under 2%).

## Limits

- Payback counts only food from foraging. Rafts, wraps, clubs and carriers also help with crossing water, cold,
  fighting and carrying, which are not counted here, so their payback is understated. Foraging is 45% of what
  adults do, and the share of all food that tools add there is the measure that matters most for "is making worth
  it".
- "Food from tools" is an upper bound: it assumes the plants on the tile would not have run out first.

## What this says

In this world, tools barely change how much people eat. A week of foraging already brings 2.7 times an adult's
need, and the best tool adds about a fifth to a harvest that was not short. Hunting, where tools matter most, is
almost never chosen. So the people of E8.1 were not irrational to ignore making: the world gives them almost no
reason to make anything. Suspect #3, the economics of making, is now supported by direct measurement of payoff,
not only by what people choose.
