# E8.4 — Could they have prepared for winter? · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Written:** 2026-10-05, after E8.3 and before any E8.4 run. Not to be edited after the results.
**Type:** read-only replays of the E7 code (E8 Arm 0), seeds 301–304, 120 years, checked against the saved runs.

**Question (the creator's):** did people fail to survive winter because they could not prepare, or because they
did not? If autumn offers enough to lay by and nobody does, the bottleneck is foresight, back in the mind. If
autumn offers too little, it is the world.

**Known from the code (not predictions):** an adult needs 7 food a week; a 13-week winter therefore takes about 91,
or about 150 laid by in autumn once rot (4% a week for loose food) is counted. Seeds keep (0.5% a week). Plants
grow at half the spring rate in autumn. A person carries 100 food bare-handed. The pull to forage grows with
hunger.

## Measures (adults 16+, each autumn, pooled over years and seeds)

- **Possible surplus:** 13 × the person's mean food per autumn foraging act, minus 13 weeks of their need.
- **What they did:** share of autumn weeks spent foraging; what was chosen instead.
- **At the first week of winter:** food in hand, seeds in hand, and food stored in heaps within reach.
- **The carrying limit:** share of adults whose food in hand reached 80 or more at any point in autumn.

## Predictions

1. **Surplus exists.** For the median adult-autumn, the possible surplus is at least 100 food.
2. **It is not taken.** Adults forage in fewer than half of their autumn weeks.
3. **Winter is entered empty-handed.** The median adult has less than 30 food (in hand, seeds and stored within
   reach together) at the first week of winter.
4. **Hardly anyone stores.** Fewer than 10% of adults have 50 or more food stored within reach at the first week of
   winter.
5. **Bare hands are never the limit.** Fewer than 5% of adult-autumns reach 80 food in hand.

**Reading:** 1 true with 2–5 true points to foresight (World A). 1 false points to the world (World B).
