# E8.3 — Who starves, when, and next to what? · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Written:** 2026-10-05, after E8.2 and before any E8.3 run. Not to be edited after the results.
**Type:** read-only replays of the E7 code (E8 Arm 0), seeds 301–304, 120 years, checked against the saved runs.

**Why:** E8.2 found food plentiful on average (about 19 per foraging week against a need of 7), yet starvation is
the largest single cause of death in long-lived ACL worlds (about 40% in E5). Both cannot be the whole truth unless
hunger is concentrated somewhere. Where it is decides which problem a tool could solve.

Four candidate explanations, each pointing to a different kind of tool:
- **seasonal:** deaths cluster in winter, when plants barely grow → storage;
- **distribution:** deaths are of people who cannot forage for themselves → sharing, provisioning;
- **mobility:** food grows near enough, but out of reach of where the person is → carrying, moving;
- **storage:** there was plenty earlier, and nothing kept for later → storing, containers.

## Measures, for every starvation death

season and year; life stage (infant, child, teen, adult, elder); sickness; who was caring for them; food on hand
and need in each of the last 8 weeks; what they chose in those weeks; plant food on their own tile and on tiles
within reach; food stored (left in heaps) within reach; whether they carried something that holds food. The same
season and life-stage shares are measured over all person-weeks, as the baseline.

## Predictions

1. **Seasonal.** At least 60% of starvation deaths happen in winter or the first four weeks of spring.
2. **Dependents and elders.** At least 50% of starvation deaths are of infants, children or elders.
3. **Food nearby.** In at least 40% of deaths, plant food within reach in the final week exceeded a week's need.
4. **Nothing stored.** In at least 80% of deaths, no stored food lay within reach in the final week.
