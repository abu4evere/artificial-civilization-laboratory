# N7 — Evidence review for K2 (a camp that goes where the couple goes) and K1 (co-residence at the scale of a week)

**Chosen by the creator (October 2026).** Questions written before any search. **No code, no experiment, no choice.**
Sources marked "abstract read", "summary read", "full text read" or "record only"; no claim stronger than what was read.

## Questions

**For K2 (a camp that moves with foraging):**
1. **How often do forager camps move, and how far?** Residential moves per year; distance per move.
2. **How far do people forage from camp in a day**, and how does that compare with how far a camp moves?
3. **What makes a camp move?** Falling returns near camp (the reading HB1 lacked), season, water, social reasons.

**For K1 (counting partners within a day's trip as co-resident):**
4. **Do spouses who forage apart by day sleep in the same camp?** How general is the central place: nearly universal, or
   varying?
5. **How often, and for how long, are spouses apart overnight** (hunting trips, logistical forays, visits)?
6. **Does the evidence give any scale-free relation** between a day's foraging range and the camp, such that "within a
   day's trip" could stand for "in the same camp" at a weekly step without choosing a number to fit births?

**For both:**
7. **Nature or culture?** Which parts (returning to a central place; moving camp; spouses' separations) are plausibly
   species-typical, and which cultural (who decides moves; residence after marriage)?

## What would count as an anchor (written before any search)

- **K2** is anchored if sources read show that foragers return to a central place most nights **and** that the central
  place moves with foraging (not a fixed home), in a range of societies.
- **K1** is anchored only if sources read show that partners foraging within a day's range of each other share a camp
  at night as a general pattern, so that the weekly co-residence rule follows from the evidence rather than from births.
  If the evidence is weaker than that, K1 is dropped.
