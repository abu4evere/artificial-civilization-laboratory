# MC1 — A camp that goes where the couple goes (candidate K2 of N7) (pre-registration, SIGNED OFF and frozen as `MC1-frozen`)

**Signed off by the creator (October 2026):** the meeting tile is (a), the better of the two partners' tiles (more wild plant food; ties to the lower coordinates); the sham is re-run in this copy; thresholds as HB1's. Nothing has been run.

**Operational detail, written at freezing, before any data:** so that the hunger ledger exists for the control too, the control (C) is re-run in this copy with both settings off and the ledger on; every C run must reproduce F1's saved populations (a further validity check). F1's replays remain the reference.

## Why

- **C1:** couples are apart about half their weeks; each forages on their own; the pull back is outbid by food.
- **HB1 / HF1–HF4:** a **fixed** shared home kept couples together but made people hungrier (a broad fall in food held
  at eating, about half explained) and raised extinction (33 vs 22 of 64; p = 0.043).
- **N7 evidence:** foragers go out from a camp by day and come back to it (Garg et al. 2021), and **move the camp as the
  land around it gives less** (Venkataraman et al. 2017; Hamilton et al. 2016; Kelly 1983; Binford 1980). HB1 had the
  first half without the second. **K2 is anchored; K1 was dropped.**

MC1 tests K2: **couples end each week together, wherever they foraged; there is no fixed home.**

## The change (nature, not culture)

After the week's actions (where HB1's return was), for every couple whose partners are both adults and alive:

- **If the partners are within `reach` (3 tiles) of each other**, both end the week on one **meeting tile** (decision 1
  below). The week's actions have already happened where each partner went; only where they end the week changes.
- **If they are further apart**, nothing happens that week (a longer trip, as in real overnight absences).
- Nothing else changes: choices, foraging, giving, courtship, conception, children following their carer.

Because each week begins wherever the couple last met, **the camp moves with their foraging**: there is no home to
deplete. Nothing says who joins whom: the meeting tile is chosen the same way whichever partner stands on it.

**Anchors (rule 17):** returning to a central place each day; a central place that moves with the food (N7). No new
number: the meeting distance is ACL's existing `reach`.

## Measures (population beside every measure)

- **Manipulation check:** couples apart, per partnered non-pregnant woman-week (C1's measure via F1's observer): must
  fall to **at most half** of C's 57.8% (≤ 28.9%). Otherwise the outcome is not read.
- **Primary:** worlds died out (of 64), M against C and S against C, paired, with an exact two-sided sign test.
- **From the first week (HF4's lesson):** the hunger ledger (food held ÷ need at the moment of eating; median, bands,
  starving share), using HF4's instrumented logging copy as the base (shown to change nothing).
- Births per woman-year; women without a partner; gifts; median population at years 40, 80, 119; child deaths.

## Feasibility pilot (rule 12; manipulation only)

M on four new seeds (1701–1704) for 20 years with C1's observer. **Pass** if couples are apart in at most 24% of
couple-weeks (C1 found 48–49% in the control). **Fail:** stop; the design returns to the creator. Extinction is not
looked at. (Unlike HB1, apart weeks will not fall to zero by construction: partners who each forage up to 3 tiles in
different directions can end up to 6 apart, beyond `reach`, and then do not meet.)

## Outcome rule (as HB1's)

| | reading |
|---|---|
| M fewer extinctions than C, sign test p < 0.05 | **MC1 reduces extinction** |
| M more extinctions than C, p < 0.05 | **MC1 increases extinction** (examined read-only, starting with the ledger) |
| p ≥ 0.05 | **no detectable effect on extinction** at this sample |

Beside it: S against C (should not reach p < 0.05), the ledger's median food held at eating (M ÷ C) and starving share,
and births per woman-year, to show whether MC1 worked through the mechanism it targets and at what cost in hunger.

## Priors (written before any run; not predictions to test)

- **Assistant:** the manipulation passes; births rise; hunger rises less than under HB1 (no fixed home to deplete, and
  each week's foraging is not cut back to a home); extinction falls but the sign test may not reach p < 0.05. Held
