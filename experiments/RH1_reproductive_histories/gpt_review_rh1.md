opinion, in summary; not sign-off.**

  not a contest for one failure label.
- The first-rule-that-fits classes overlap (e.g. paired at 38, widowed at 39, five eligible weeks fits three classes).
  Primary: the funnel stage by stage with non-exclusive reasons and exact denominators; a mutually exclusive class only
  as a secondary summary with frozen priorities; women who die before 45 separate.
- 26 eligible weeks is a description, not a line between "no chance" and "bad luck" (P(0) 34.6% at 4%, 11.4% at 8%);
  use each woman's own P(0) = ∏(1 − p_t); it predicts no conception, while RH1 observes no live birth (account for
  maternal deaths and any losses).
- Add the **affection deadlock** per eligible encounter (attraction above threshold? existing affection? courting
  considered but beaten by food or another act? courted and rejected? did repeated contact raise affection?), and a
  light **men's** side (childless at 45, first pairing and child, partners and children, eligible-unpartnered time,
  widowhood and re-pairing, how births spread across fathers).
- Childlessness at 45 only among women seen reaching 45; report early deaths and the unconditional chance from 16.
- Do not require one class's lower bound to beat every other's upper bound; report ranked shares with intervals.
- Reusing M1's seeds is fine if both are still unfrozen and the overlap is declared before data (not independent
  replication); separate runs are fine too.
