# RH1 — Why does a third of women never have a child? Reproductive histories on nature v6 (DRAFT, Revision 1, 25 October 2026, sleep mode; read-only; not signed off, not frozen)

**Why.** A quick look on 24 October 2026 (4 worlds, v6, 80 founders on 40 × 40, 100 years; 43 in-world-born women
who lived through 16–45; a throwaway script, not a result) found: births per woman 3.14 (mothers 4.82, close to some
foragers); **15 of 43 (35%) never had a child**; partnered in 55% of fertile woman-weeks, partner within 2 tiles in
20%. GPT called 35% childlessness "a serious warning sign" for a natural-fertility setting (a Tsimane working paper,
not yet checked, reports ~3% at 45+), but not yet a demonstrated defect, and ranked explaining it first, before any
new drive (`../../docs/gpt_view_wish_for_children_2026-10-24.md`). This study explains it; it changes nothing.

**Related record:** CP1 (extinct 20-founder worlds break at encounter), PF1/PF2 (courting not tried; food wins), C1,
CB1, AP1 (couples apart most fertile weeks), TL1 (ageing out). None followed women one by one through their lives.

## Design

- **Read-only:** live v6 + a logging observer (state and random numbers unchanged: zero check). Two settings: **D80**
  (80 founders on 40 × 40, as the quick look) and **C20** (20 founders on 40 × 40, today's standard world); 120 years.
  **Seeds (a decision for the creator):** (a) its own 64 new seeds, 16901–16964, 128 worlds; or (b) **the first 120
  years of M1's D80 and C20 worlds** (seeds 16801–16864), with RH1's observer running beside M1's, if both are frozen
  together and the overlap is declared before any data (saves runs and links reproductive histories to later culture;
  not an independent replication). The assistant recommends (b) if M1 is approved.
- **Who is counted:** every woman born in a world who reaches 16, in birth cohorts with complete follow-up to 45
  (born by year 75); women who die before 45 are kept and reported by age at death (no selection on survival).

## Each woman's history (logged)

Weeks fertile (16–45, alive); weeks with a partner; **fully eligible weeks** (partnered, partner within 2 tiles,
healthy, not starving, not pregnant, not nursing a living youngest) and their conception chance (4% or 8%); age at
first eligible man in sight, first courting (given or received), first pairing, first birth; partner losses (death)
and time to re-pairing; eligible unrelated men alive in the world / within sight, by age band (16–19, 20–24, 25–29,
30–34, 35–39, 40–45); births and birth intervals (split by whether the previous child lived).

## Primary: where opportunity disappears (the funnel, non-exclusive)

For every woman, at each stage, with exact denominators and per-world means (bootstrap over worlds): an eligible
unrelated man alive → in sight → courting considered → courting tried (by either) → accepted → paired → fully eligible
weeks → conception → live birth. Each childless woman is given **every** reason that applies (not one label): died
before 45; pool empty most fertile weeks; men alive but rarely in sight; in sight but courting not tried; courting tried
and refused; paired only after 35; widowed and not re-paired; paired but rarely together; eligible and unlucky.
**Exposure:** each woman's own chance of no conception, P(0) = ∏(1 − p_t) over her fully eligible weeks (described;
no fixed cut). Childbirth deaths are counted apart from no conception.

**The affection deadlock (per eligible encounter):** attraction toward her above the acceptance threshold? her
affection toward him? courting considered and beaten (by what)? tried and refused? did repeated contact raise
affection over the weeks? This separates "no chance to meet" from "met, but the decision system never let them pair".

**Men, lightly:** childless at 45; ages at first pairing and first child; number of partners and children;
eligible-but-unpartnered weeks; widowhood and re-pairing; how births are spread across fathers.

**Secondary summary:** one mutually exclusive class per childless woman, by this frozen priority: died before 45 →
pool empty → never paired (men not in sight / in sight, not courted / courted, refused) → paired after 35 → widowed,
not re-paired → rarely together → eligible and unlucky. Reported as ranked shares with intervals; no "main path"
verdict is drawn from them.

## What would follow (stated now)

- Classes 2–3 lead → meeting and pairing (CP1/PF2's line): an evidence review of how real foragers meet and pair before
  any change to nature.
- Class 5 leads → re-pairing after loss.
- Class 6 leads → staying together (MC1 kept couples together and raised births; it was not adopted because survival
  did not change; the creator may revisit it).
- Class 7 leads → the conception process itself.

## The assistant's prior

Class 3 (never paired, men alive but rarely in sight or not courted) is the main path in both settings, then class 6.
Childlessness at 45 is 25–40% in D80 and higher in C20. Re-pairing after widowhood is slow (median over 5 years).
