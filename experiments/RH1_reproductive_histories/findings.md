# RH1 — Why a third of women never have a child: findings (PROVISIONAL until the independent recompute)

Frozen as `RH1-frozen`; read-only, in the first 120 years of M1's D80 and C20 worlds (seeds 16801–16864; declared
before data; not an independent replication). Numbers from `analyse_rh1.py` (written before data); full tables in
`results.md`. Valid: M1's zero checks (including RH1's observer) pass, all worlds present.

## The answer

**Childless at 45, among women seen reaching 45:**

| | D80 (3,396 women) | C20 (1,514 women) |
|---|---|---|
| childless at 45 | 0.31 [0.27, 0.36] | 0.53 [0.45, 0.61] |
| died between 16 and 45 | 0.68 | 0.62 |

**Where opportunity disappears** (women childless at 45 vs mothers):

| | D80 | C20 |
|---|---|---|
| an eligible man was alive | almost all | almost all |
| one came into sight | 85% | 52% |
| courting was tried by either | 64% | 28% |
| **ever paired** | **11%** | **4%** |

**The break is pairing, not conception.**
- Only 11% (D80) and 4% (C20) of childless women ever paired.
- Childless women almost never had real chances to conceive: their chance of no conception P(0) is above 0.5 for
  98–99% of them, with a median of 1.0.

**Reasons (non-exclusive, among childless women):**
- **D80:** courted and refused 42%; in sight but not courted 29%; men rarely in sight 28%.
- **C20:** men rarely in sight 55%; in sight but not courted 27%; refused 20%; pool empty 15%.

In bigger worlds the problem is refusal; in small worlds it is meeting at all.

**The affection deadlock** (never-paired women who had an eligible man in sight):
- In about 60% of weeks she would have accepted a man in sight, and one would have accepted her.
- When she considered courting and chose otherwise, she chose **foraging** most of the time (D80 4,812 of 6,554
  choices).
- This repeats PF1/PF2: courting is available but food wins.

**Also measured:**
- Re-pairing within 5 years of widowhood before 40: 31% (D80), 23% (C20).
- The median birth interval is about 3.3 years, inside forager ranges (about 2.6–4.1).
- Men: 22% (D80) and 35% (C20) childless at 45; the top fifth of men father 42–47% of births.

## Against the prior

- **Right:** the assistant expected "never paired" to lead.
- **Partly wrong:** in D80, "courted, refused" is the largest single class after early death, ahead of "not in sight".
- **Wrong on the level:** C20 childlessness (53%) is above the 25–40% range expected for D80 and "higher in C20".
- **Right on re-pairing:** it is slow. Only 23–31% re-pair within 5 years, so the median is over 5 years, as
  expected.

## What follows (frozen)

Classes 2–3 lead, so the frozen next step is an evidence review of how real foragers meet and pair, before any change
to nature. The deadlock adds a concrete question: why food beats courting even when both would accept.
