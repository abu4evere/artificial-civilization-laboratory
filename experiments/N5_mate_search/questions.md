# N5 — How do real foragers find partners? (evidence review; questions written before any search)

**Asked by the creator (October 2026), after F1:** "reopen stage 0 and find evidence on forager mate search."
**No code, no experiment, no choice.** Rules 12, 15, 16 and 17 apply: whatever this finds is evidence for the creator to
anchor nature to, not a value to be tuned toward survival.

## Why

F1 (confirmed on a holdout): a third of control worlds die out because, after the founders, people rarely pair; in a
small, scattered people a partner is almost never within sight. In the code a single adult courts only within perception
(4 tiles), walks toward someone within 6, or takes a random step of a few tiles, on a 40 × 40 world with 20 founders in
bands of five.

## A constraint found before searching (rule 12)

**ACL has no physical scale.** A tile is not a stated distance (people travel 3 tiles a week and see 4; nothing in
`DESIGN.md` or `design/` sets kilometres). Real marriage distances in kilometres therefore cannot be carried over
directly. The evidence must be read in **scale-free terms** wherever possible (people, bands, generations, shares), and
any distance found is recorded with the note that mapping it to tiles would itself need an anchored decision.

## Questions

1. **How large is the pool from which foragers find spouses?** (People or bands in a mating network; how often spouses
   come from outside the band.)
2. **How far apart do spouses' natal places lie?** (Recorded, with the scale caveat.)
3. **By what means do partners meet?** Individual search, visiting between bands, seasonal aggregation, arrangement by
   kin, bride service, exchange between groups.
4. **Who chooses?** The pair, parents, or wider kin; at what ages.
5. **What happens when local partners are lacking?** (Widowhood, sex imbalance, marrying out, age gaps, delayed
   marriage, polygyny.)
6. **How small can a forager population be and persist?** (Minimum mating network, demographic viability, evidence of
   small populations dying out.)
7. **How do residence and movement after marriage work?** (Who moves, how fluid band membership is.)
