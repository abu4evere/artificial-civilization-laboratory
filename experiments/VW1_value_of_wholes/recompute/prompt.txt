Blind recompute for a study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study VW1: one candidate law tested in a copy, with a sham). Please compute only from the attached file vw1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Columns per world (years 100-200 of 200): living_weeks; death_starvation and death_killed (deaths by those causes, all ages); census_adults and census_health (adults 16-49 counted at year ends, and the sum of their health); child_weeks (person-weeks of children under 12); child_deaths and child_death_starvation (deaths of children under 12); adult_weeks (adults 16 and over); gifts_kin_children (food gifts to a child under 12 of the giver's kin); extinct (1 or 0); kinds (new kinds invented in years 100-190 that need at least one made part); any_part_held (of those, kinds where someone who knows the kind held at least one of its made parts within 5 years); all_parts_held (kinds where one such person held all of its made parts at once within 5 years). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, recompute every quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. For w against c compute the guards: (1) starvation deaths per 1,000 living-years (living_weeks / 52 / 1,000) as a ratio w / c: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (2) killing deaths per 1,000 living-years as a difference w - c: raised if above 0.05 and its lower bound above 0; (3) adult health = sum census_health / sum census_adults, difference w - c: raised if below -5 and its upper bound below 0; (4) living_weeks ratio w / c: raised if below 0.90 and its upper bound below 1; (5) extinct worlds: raised if w has 3 or more more than c; (6) child deaths per 1,000 child-years (child_weeks / 52 / 1,000), ratio: raised if above 1.25, its lower bound above 1, and w's rate exceeds c's by 3 or more; (7) child starvation deaths per 1,000 child-years, ratio: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (8) gifts_kin_children per 1,000 adult_weeks, ratio: raised if below 0.80 and its upper bound below 1. Then the co-primaries: (9) any_part_held / kinds, w - c with its interval (and x - c); (10) all_parts_held / kinds, w - c with its interval (and x - c). The reading: 'harm' if any guard is raised; otherwise 'supported' if both co-primaries' lower bounds are above 0; 'partly supported' if only (9)'s lower bound is above 0; otherwise 'no harm, no effect'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals), the guards raised and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
