# N9 — How much do the unpartnered search? (questions, written and committed before any search)

**Asked by the creator (October 2026), after P2's pilot stop:** P2 (outward instead of random steps) barely acted,
because single adults in ACL choose to search in only about 4–7% of the weeks they have no one in sight; food, water,
warmth, family and home outbid it. The question is now **how much** people search, not where. In ACL a "how much" is a
number, and numbers come from evidence (rule 17). **This is a read-only evidence review: no code, no experiment, no
choice.**

## What ACL does today (for comparison only, not a target)

In the pilot (4 worlds, 20 years), seekers (adult, unpartnered, under 45) had no eligible partner in sight in 95% of
their weeks and took an exploratory step in under 7% of those weeks. A week in ACL is one action; travelling 3 tiles is
one week's move.

## Questions

1. **Visiting:** how often do forager adults travel to other camps or groups (visits per year, days away, share of
   time), in any society with quantitative data?
2. **The unpartnered:** do unmarried young adults visit or move between camps more than married ones, and by how much?
   Is such travel described as, or linked to, finding a spouse?
3. **Time budgets:** in forager time-allocation studies, what share of time goes to travel or visiting other camps,
   against food getting?
4. **Trade-off:** is visiting reduced when food is short, or does it rise (visiting as a response to scarcity)?
5. **Nature or culture:** is visiting other groups described as general across foragers (a candidate for nature) or as
   tied to particular institutions (gift exchange partners, ceremonies, arranged marriage: culture, not to be
   written)?

## Anchor criteria (fixed now)

- **Anchored:** at least **two independent sources** (different societies or different research teams) give a
  **quantitative** figure for how often adults, or specifically unpartnered adults, travel to other camps or groups
  (frequency or share of time), from field data. The figure must be convertible to ACL's terms (share of weeks, or
  weeks per year) without choosing a further number. If the figures are for adults generally, not the unpartnered,
  this is stated, and the figure can serve only as an adults-wide rate.
- **Partly anchored:** one quantitative source, or several qualitative ones agreeing on direction (e.g. "the unmarried
  visit more") without a figure. **Not enough to set a number**; recorded.
- **Not anchored:** no quantitative source. Then **no number is changed**, P2 is closed in this form, and this is
  recorded as a limit of the evidence.
- A figure that comes only from a culturally specific institution (e.g. hxaro gift partners, ceremonial gatherings,
  bride service) is recorded but **does not anchor nature**.
- **Not decided here:** how a figure would enter ACL (for example, as a share of weeks in which a seeker travels instead
  of choosing among other needs). That is a design for the creator, after this review.

## Sources and method

PubMed (via its MCP; PMIDs and DOIs cited), OpenAlex for abstracts, and full text where open. Each source is marked
"full text read", "abstract read" or "record only". Searches start from forager visiting, inter-camp mobility,
residential mobility of unmarried adults, hunter-gatherer time allocation, and camp composition flux (e.g. Hadza, !Kung,
Agta, BaYaka, Aché, Martu). Sources already in the record (N5, N7, N8) are reused and marked as carried.
