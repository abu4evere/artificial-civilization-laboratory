Blind recompute for a read-only audit in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study DF1: who feeds the children, and what does it cost them?). Please compute only from the attached file df1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. One arm, 32 worlds (seeds 25101-25132), years 100-200 of 200, one row per world. Columns: women_weeks and men_weeks (person-weeks of grown people, 16 and over, by sex); women_fcs and men_fcs (of those, feeding-caused short weeks: the person held at least their own need before feeding their dependent children, and ended the week short). Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 worlds with replacement, recompute each quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) feeding-caused short weeks per 1,000 person-weeks for women and for men, each with its interval; (2) the difference women - men with its interval; (3) women's share of all feeding-caused short weeks = sum women_fcs / (sum women_fcs + sum men_fcs), with its interval. The reading: 'feeding drives the gap' if the difference is above 0, its lower bound is above 0, and women's share is at least 0.75; 'feeding does not explain it' if the difference's interval includes 0 or men's rate is higher; otherwise 'feeding contributes, below the frozen bar'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
