Blind recompute for a read-only audit in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study PM1: why are the parts of newly invented kinds never made again?). Please compute only from the attached file pm1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. One arm, 32 worlds (seeds 25001-25032), one row per world. Each followed kind is a newly invented kind (years 100-190 of 200) whose recipe needs at least one made part; it is followed for 5 years and placed in the furthest part-making stage reached by anyone who knows its recipe, for any of its made parts: P0a = the part's recipe is known by no one living; P0b = the knower does not know the part's recipe but someone else does; P1 = known, but the inputs never in hand; P2 = inputs in hand, but never valued enough to make; P3 = eligible, but making it was never the make option offered; P4 = offered, never chosen; P5 = chosen, never made; P6 = made, but never all of the kind's parts held at once; P7 = all parts held at once. kinds = the sum of P0a ... P7. never_valued = kinds whose inventor never valued the kind itself in those 5 years; never_valued_P0a = of those, the ones at P0a. Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 worlds with replacement, recompute each share from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) the share of each stage P0a ... P7 among all kinds, with its interval; check that the stages sum to kinds in every row; (2) the reading: name the largest share among P0a, P0b, P1, P2, P3, P4, P5 and P6; call it 'clear' if its lower bound is above the upper bound of the next largest of those, otherwise 'shared'; (3) never_valued / kinds and never_valued_P0a / never_valued, each with its interval. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
