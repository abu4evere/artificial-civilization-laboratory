Blind recompute for a confirmatory run in an agent-based simulation (ACL study CI1-C: one candidate law tested in a copy, on new seeds). Please compute only from the attached file ci1c_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = baseline (v14), g = children may make again, x = sham; the same 32 seeds (24101-24132) in every arm. Columns per world (years 100-200 of 200): living_weeks; death_starvation and death_killed (deaths by those causes, all ages); census_adults and census_health (adults 16-49 counted at year ends, and the sum of their health); child_weeks (person-weeks of children under 12); child_deaths and child_death_starvation (deaths of children under 12, all causes and by starvation); adult_weeks (adults 16 and over); gifts_kin_children (food gifts to a child under 12 of the giver's kin); extinct (1 or 0); kinds (new kinds invented in a window); complete (how many of those had their inventor hold the complete input set within 5 years); remade (how many were made again by their inventor within 5 years); others (how many were made by a person other than their inventor within 5 years). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, recompute every quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. For g against c compute the guards: (1) starvation deaths per 1,000 living-years (living_weeks / 52 / 1,000) as a ratio g / c: raised if the ratio is above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (2) killing deaths per 1,000 living-years as a difference g - c: raised if above 0.05 and its lower bound above 0; (3) adult health = sum census_health / sum census_adults, difference g - c: raised if below -5 and its upper bound below 0; (4) living_weeks ratio g / c: raised if below 0.90 and its upper bound below 1; (5) extinct worlds: raised if g has 3 or more more than c; (6) child deaths per 1,000 child-years (child_weeks / 52 / 1,000), ratio: raised if above 1.25, its lower bound above 1, and g's rate exceeds c's by 3 or more; (7) child starvation deaths per 1,000 child-years, ratio: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (8) gifts_kin_children per 1,000 adult_weeks, ratio: raised if below 0.80 and its upper bound below 1. Then the co-primary share = sum complete / sum kinds: (9) the difference g - c with its interval; (10) the difference g - x with its interval. The rule: 'confirmed' only if no guard is raised, the lower bound of (9) is above 0, and the lower bound of (10) is above 0; otherwise 'not confirmed'. Also report (11) the secondary, the remade share = sum remade / sum kinds, difference g - c with its interval (not part of the rule), and (12) the others share = sum others / sum kinds for each arm. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals), the guards raised and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
