## Caveats written before the holdout

1. **Lineage by descent mixes the measure.** Lineage is the mother's founding band (frozen). The child of a couple
   across lineages carries the mother's lineage but may live among the father's kin, so once cross-lineage couples
   exist, some "between-lineage meeting" happens inside one living group. The rate therefore measures contact between
   descent lines, not only between separate groups. This favours C and P, where such couples are commoner. It is not
   corrected (the definition is frozen); it is how the numbers must be read.
2. **"Within sight" is an upper bound on contact** (correction of 12 October): even C's 0.05 does not show forager-like
   *interaction*, which runs at about a quarter of it.
3. **Lineages die out.** By year 120 a typical C world has one lineage left; its pairs then leave the measure.

## Discovery reading (provisional; stands only with the holdout)

In today's 20-founder worlds, descent lines do come within sight of each other at about the frozen line (0.05 a
pair-year), and one couple in ten crosses them. With 80 founders in the same land (P), about the same. **With 80
founders on four times the land (LP), the bands are effectively closed** and drift further apart over time, though LP
worlds are the ones that survive best (WS1): their survival does not come from bands meeting.
