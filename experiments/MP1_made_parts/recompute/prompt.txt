Blind recompute for a read-only audit in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study MP1: where do the made parts of newly invented kinds go?). Please compute only from the attached file mp1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. One arm, 32 worlds (seeds 24901-24932), one row per world. Each followed kind is a newly invented kind (years 100-190 of 200) whose recipe needs at least one made part; it is followed for 5 years after its invention week, and placed in the furthest stage it reached: stage_A = no one who knows its recipe ever held any of its made parts; stage_B = some parts held, never all at once by one person; stage_C = one person held all its made parts but could not make it (a raw material or binder missing); stage_D = one person could make it, but it was never made again; stage_E = it was made again. kinds = the number of followed kinds (stage_A + ... + stage_E). The other columns count tracked parts (made things whose kind is a made part of a followed kind, made in the hands of someone who knows that kind) by their first fate: used_into_kind, used_into_other, lost_in_fire, worn_use, worn_cold, dropped (left on the ground because a person carries at most 3 made things), handed, died_heir, died_body, still_held, unexplained. Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 worlds with replacement, recompute each share from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) the share of each stage A-E among all kinds, with its interval; check that A+B+C+D+E = kinds in every row; (2) the reading: name the largest share among A, B, C and D; call it 'clear' if its lower bound is above the upper bound of the next largest of A-D, otherwise 'shared'; (3) the share of each fate among all tracked parts, with its interval. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
