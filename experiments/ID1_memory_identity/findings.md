# ID1 — What makes an ACL person who they are? Findings

Frozen as `ID1-frozen`; correction of 12 October (17 actions); notes of 14 October (deep-copy snapshots); **Amendment 2**
approved 14 October (`ID1-amend2`; absorbs Amendment 1): the hard stop checks directly that every swap was applied and
restored, and a swap that leaves the world unchanged is an outcome. Discovery reading written before the holdout
(`findings_discovery.md`). Tables: `results_discovery.md`, `results_holdout.md`, from `analyse_id1.py`.

## Validity, and the record of how it got there

- **Valid in both sets.** One fingerprint; the original copy reproduces the uncopied world in every world (31 + 31 with
  focal people; seeds 513 and 1101 had none, skipped as frozen); the sham differs; **every swap was applied and every
  restore exact** (checked directly, field by field).
- **Three attempts, all kept:** the first (`output_firstattempt/`) stopped at seed 504, traced to a shared reference in
  ACL's knowledge record that copying through JSON broke (fixed by deep-copy snapshots; the quirk is listed for nature
  v2). The second (`output_secondattempt/`) stopped twice on swaps that were applied but left no trace (a person dead
  in week 3; another dead in week 41), which led to Amendments 1 and 2. The third is this one. **The worlds are identical
  in the second and third attempts**, and so are the numbers.
- **Disclosure:** the second attempt's tables were displayed before the amendments were approved (recorded with each).
  The analysis now hides outcomes of a run that is not valid.
- **Swaps that left no trace** (an outcome): 3 of 872, all of a part that steers little (beliefs, places, memories), in
  people who died.

109 focal people (median age about 40); population at the snapshot about 27.

## What stands (discovery and holdout agree)

1. **No single part makes a person who they are.** Swapping temperament, habits, memories, relationships, know-how,
   beliefs or places alone does not move a person's behaviour beyond what the world's own chaos does (the sham), in
   either set.
2. **Together, their acquired parts do.** Swapping everything except temperament moves people clearly beyond the sham in
   both sets (median extra drift +0.085 and +0.055; Holm p 1.5 × 10⁻⁷ and 4.4 × 10⁻⁵).
3. **Single parts mostly do not pull a person toward the donor** (six of seven agree "no" in both sets).

Also consistent in both sets (described, not a frozen reading): swapping **memories** or **know-how** alone moves a person
*less* than the sham does (both significant both times). Such a swap disturbs the world less than one extra random draw:
these parts barely steer what people do.

## What does not stand (the sets disagree; no reading)

- **Q2, pulled toward the donor** by beliefs, and by everything but temperament: yes in discovery, no in holdout.
- **Q3, is temperament enough:** "follows their parts" (discovery) against "changed, but not into the donor" (holdout).
- **Q4, does restoring bring them back:** "no detectable lasting change" against "a lasting change" (holdout, p = 0.025).

## Reading

An ACL person is not held together by any one part, and not by temperament: given another person's acquired parts, they
behave differently. Whether they become *like the donor*, and whether they fully return when their own parts are given
back, these 109 people cannot say.

**Why single parts matter so little, from BL0:** habits steer one choice in seven but are nearly the same in everyone
(a stranger's correlate 0.92 with one's own), so a donor's habits are hardly different; memories and know-how barely
steer at all. Identity in ACL is spread thin across parts that are each weak, and much of it is shared.

## The assistant's prior, against the result

| prior | result |
|---|---|
| habits and temperament will matter most | **wrong**: neither alone moves a person |
| memories the least | **consistent**: memories move people less than chance does |
| restoring will mostly bring people back, not completely | **no reading** (the sets disagree) |

## What it cannot show

How human identity works. ACL's parts are far simpler than a mind; the sham is a strict yardstick (any change makes the
whole world's future diverge), so small effects of single parts may be real but below what it can detect.
