# NL1 — Where does nature end? A read-only audit of ACL's nature against the line tests (12 October 2026)

**Asked by the creator (12 October 2026),** after a review of the book's founding rule: "Nature is programmed; culture
is written by nobody". How is the line drawn, and is there a meta-rule? The decision: **audit first**, then write the rule
and the book chapter, "so they reflect what the tests actually catch". Autopsy before intervention.

**Read-only.** Nothing in the world was changed. Sources:

- the code (`acl/`, as of `nature-v1`);
- `DESIGN.md`;
- the World Bible (`bible_dump.txt`);
- the register generated by `inventory.py` (`register.md`).

Verdicts are the assistant's judgement, written for the creator to check. **"Evidence" means only what is recorded in the
repository.** No new literature search was done for this audit, so "no source recorded" does **not** mean "no source
exists".

## The tests applied (as drafted in conversation; to be revised by what this audit finds)

- **T1 Capacity, not content.** Nature gives what a person *can* feel, sense, learn and do. Any particular *who, what,
  where, when* is culture.
- **T2 Evidence and variation.** Only what is universal and present without teaching may be nature, with evidence. What
  real groups differ on and pass on by learning is culture.
- **T3 No outcome in the rule.** A rule of nature may not name the result being studied.
- **T4 No knowledge without a channel.** People know only what their senses, memory, or being told could give them.
- **When evidence is silent:** no new mechanism. A value the world needs is labelled **unanchored**, never tuned, and
  tested for sensitivity.
- **Channel test for every change** (method, not the line): a change acts only through the channel it claims.

## The register of unanchored values (`register.md`)

- **85 settings:** 38 world, 30 body, 12 mind, 5 operational. **None has a recorded source.**
- All entered git in the repository's first commit (4 October 2026). Their history before that is not in the record, so
  **how 0.05, 0.6 or 0.2 were chosen cannot be traced**.
- **About 1,340 further fixed numbers** sit inside the nature code's formulas, in pulls, weights and thresholds. These
  are not settings at all, so no register, experiment or reviewer has ever seen them listed. The largest group is in
  `mind.py` (260) and `actions.py` (254).
- **Later studies used some of these values but anchored none.** N10 anchored the founder count *in direction only*.
  The hunger rule (U1) was changed on evidence.

## What the audit says the rule should say (input to the drafts, not the drafts)

1. **Rule 20 should consolidate, not invent.** Cite Laws 12–18 and 21, add the evidence test (T2) to the World Bible,
   and add the missing piece: **a check of existing code against the laws**, made routine (as AA1 was, once).
2. **The register must cover numbers inside formulas, not only settings.** Otherwise most unanchored values stay
   invisible, which is the reviewer's warning ("invisible numbers are the easiest to tune later").
3. **"Inheritance of the limited kinds the system explicitly allows"** (Law 12) is a loophole. DESIGN.md "explicitly
   allows" habit inheritance while saying temperament is the only biological inheritance. Any allowed inheritance should
   itself pass T1–T4.
4. **Who knows whose kin** is a channel question the laws do not answer. It touches courting, sharing and taking.
5. **Failures need a home.** A rule that fails but whose removal would break the world belongs in a register of **known
   compromises** (the book's Chapter 12), labelled, until a tested fix replaces it, as LK1 does for A5.

## Answers to the reviewer's remaining questions (from the audit)

1. **How were unanchored values chosen?** It is not recorded. They predate the repository's history. This should be said
   plainly in the book, not reconstructed.
2. **How is sensitivity tested?** Not yet defined. A proposal for the rule: vary one value by a fixed factor (×0.5 and
   ×2) on F1's 64 seeds and report the change in extinction with its interval. Values whose change exceeds half of F2's
   effect are flagged **sensitive**, and any finding that rests on them says so.
3. **Who decides when evidence is sufficient?** The creator, at sign-off, against a stated standard. Proposal: a
   cross-cultural sample or review for "universal"; a single ethnography is enough only to show that groups vary.
4. **What happens when a rule fails?** It is listed as a known compromise, measured (as AA1 did), and replaced only by a
   tested new version of nature. It is never removed silently.
