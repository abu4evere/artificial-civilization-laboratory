# Rule 20 — Where nature ends (DRAFT for the creator's review; not adopted)

Drafted 12 October 2026 from the NL1 audit (`audit.md`). The audit found that most of this rule already existed in the
World Bible, spread over Laws 12–18 and 21, and that **what was missing was the check, not the rule**: A5 broke Law 12
("Knowledge must have a source") and sat in the code for months because nobody compared the code with the laws. So this
rule mostly gathers what is written, adds the two pieces that were not (the evidence test, and the register), and makes
the check routine.

When adopted, it goes in two places: as rule 20 in `ROADMAP.md` (beside rules 1–19), and as a new law in the World
Bible, after Law 21. It starts with the short form; the parts after it say how it is applied.

---

## The rule (short form)

> **20. Nature gives capacities, never contents; it is anchored or labelled; and the code is checked against it.**
> A rule of nature may give people only what they *can* feel, sense, learn and do (not what, whom, where or when), must
> not name the outcome being studied, must not give knowledge without a channel, and must rest on evidence that it is
> universal. A number nature needs but evidence cannot set is labelled unanchored, chosen before any experiment, and
> never tuned. Existing code is checked against these tests, not only new changes; a rule that fails is recorded as a
> known compromise, measured, and replaced only by a tested new version of nature. The line moves only for new evidence
> or a found shortcut, never for a wanted outcome. *(Wolves; the hunger rule, U1; A5, AA1; newborn habits, NL1.)*

## 1. The four tests for a rule of nature

A rule belongs to nature only if it passes all four.

| | test | the question | already written as |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Capacity, not content** | Does it give a capacity (to fear, to grieve, to learn), or a particular object, person, place or time? | Laws 14, 15, 16 |
| T2 | **Evidence of universality** | Is there evidence that it is human-universal and present without teaching? If real peoples differ on it and pass the difference on by learning, it is culture. | **new to the World Bible** (from rule 17) |
| T3 | **No outcome in the rule** | Does it name, or obviously aim at, a result an experiment is studying? | Law 21 |
| T4 | **A channel for every piece of knowledge** | Does every fact the rule lets a person act on reach them through sight, memory of something they saw, being told, or inference from those? | Laws 12, 13, 18 |

**Standards of evidence (T2):** "universal" needs a cross-cultural sample or a review; one ethnography is enough only to
show that peoples **vary**. The creator decides at sign-off whether the evidence meets the standard, and the decision
and its source are recorded beside the rule.

**Grain (an edge, named, not settled):** a capacity can be so narrow that it is content in disguise (fear of snakes;
disgust at rot). Such a rule passes T1 only with evidence that the narrowness itself is prepared by biology, not
learned. Cases are decided one by one and recorded; no general cut is claimed.

**Inheritance (closing Law 12's loophole):** Law 12 allows "inheritance of the limited kinds the system explicitly
allows". Anything allowed to pass at birth must itself pass T1–T4. Temperament does. Learned habits do not (NL1).

## 2. When evidence is silent

- **A new mechanism with no evidence is not added.** Leaving the world as it is counts as the default. (N9: no source
  said how often people search, so the outward step was closed.)
- **A number the world needs but evidence cannot set is unanchored.** It is chosen before any experiment that rests on
  it, never tuned toward a result, entered in the register, and tested for sensitivity when a finding depends on it.

## 3. The register of numbers

Every number in nature, settings **and** the constants inside formulas, is listed in a register (`register.md`, made by
a committed script) with its value, where it is, its source or "unanchored", and its kind:

- **structural**: part of a rule's shape (the "1 −" in "1 − weeks ÷ 2"). It is reviewed with the rule, under T1–T4.
- **parameter**: a value the shape needs (the "2"). Anchored or unanchored, under section 2.
- **content in disguise**: a specific value that picks out a who, what, where or when, or aims at an outcome (a
  candidate: the 15-year courtship cut, if it decides who pairs rather than bounding the search). It is treated as a
  rule and must pass T1–T4.

**Sensitivity method:** one value at a time, × 0.5 and × 2, on F1's 64 control seeds, 120 years. If extinction changes
by more than half of F2's effect (with its bootstrap interval), the value is flagged **sensitive**, and every finding
that rests on it says so.

## 4. The check (the part that was missing)

- **When:** at every new version of nature; before freezing any study whose reading rests on a particular rule; and at
  least once for all existing code (NL1 was the first; it is to be completed).
- **How:** read-only. Every rule is listed with T1–T4 verdicts, and every information pathway with its channel. Where a
  pathway may be used without a channel, it is measured as AA1 measured A5 (how often a decision rests on it).
- **What a property test can enforce** is enforced in `tests/` (as Rule 3, water, now is). Knowledge pathways that a test
  cannot see are listed for review.

## 5. When a rule fails

1. It is recorded as a **known compromise** (the book's Chapter 12), with the test it fails.
2. It is **measured**: how much behaviour rests on it (read-only).
3. It is **replaced only by a tested new version of nature**: a controlled comparison against the preserved version,
   frozen in advance (LK1 for A5). The old version is kept, and every earlier result stays tied to it (`nature-v1`).
4. Nothing is removed silently because it looks unrealistic.

## 6. The method test for every change (not part of the line)

Any change, to nature, to the world or in an experiment, acts **only through the channel it claims**. People without
the relevant experience behave exactly as before (the leak tests of E9, E10 and E13; the zero checks). A change meant to
act on everyone, such as the hunger fix, claims everyone.

## 7. How the line may move

Only for **new evidence** about human nature, or a **found shortcut**. Never because an outcome is wanted (rule 17).
Every move is a new version of nature, signed off and recorded.
