## Per world, A1 (descriptive; the frozen measure is the pooled share above)

Corner crossings are **concentrated, not general**:

- 10 of the 15 worlds with any crossing have none.
- Corner crossings occur in 6 worlds: 501 (19% of that world's crossings), 503 (97%), 504 (78%), 510 (95%), 514 (2%)
  and 516 (73%).
- **Two worlds, 503 and 510, account for 20,499 of the 21,452.** In each, a corner gap joins two inhabited stretches
  of land, and people cross it as part of daily life.
- That is why the interval is so wide. The pooled share says how much of all crossing rests on the gap. The per-world
  picture says that where such a gap exists between settled land, it changes that world completely.
