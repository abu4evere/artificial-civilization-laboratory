Frozen as `CT1-frozen`; Amendment 1 approved before data (`CT1-amend1-approved`); Amendment 2 after the manipulation
checks, before any outcome. A Law 126 study, nature v3. 1,024 worlds: 256 new seeds 8201–8456 × C, K1, K2, K12;

- **K1** (`familiar_attraction` 0.3): attraction to a suitor gains 0.3 × familiarity.
- **K2** (`lonely_courting`): courting someone not yet a partner scores (1 + loneliness) times as much.

## Amendment 2: exploratory sensitivity analysis (`results_amend2.md`)

The signed plan named checks for K1 and K2 only. Following it, with GPT's safeguards (`gpt_review_ct1_amend2.md`):
everything below is **exploratory, not confirmatory**.

**Manipulation:**
- K1 acts: 513 acceptances v3 would have refused (about 3 per 100 attempts, in 171 of 256 worlds).
- K2 passes, but only marginally: +0.003 attempts per single adult-year, [−0.001, +0.008], p = 0.04. Per the
  described table, its rise is among the lonelier suitors (0.09 → 0.12 at loneliness 0.25–0.5).

**Survival** (C died out in 83 of 256):

| arm | died out | saved / lost | Holm p | effect (C − arm) | 95% interval | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | 81 | 12 / 10 | 0.83 | +0.008 | [−0.027, +0.043] | no meaningful effect |
| K2 | 70 | 44 / 31 | 0.50 | +0.051 | [−0.016, +0.117] | **unresolved** |
| K12 | 72 | 45 / 34 | 0.52 | +0.043 | [−0.023, +0.109] | no meaningful effect (its K2 part did not measurably act) |

**Mechanism:**
- Couples formed rose with K1 (+2.0 per world, [+0.5, +3.5]).
- Births per woman-year did not move in any arm.

**Harm:** K1 raised adult deaths by starvation (+1.16 per world, +7%, [+0.33, +2.02], p = 0.03). This is one of
several harm tests, and no correction for multiple tests was applied. Infant deaths did not change measurably.

**The adoption rule (frozen), applied to exploratory results:**
- **K1: not adopted** (a serious harm rose).
- **K2: not adopted** (survival unresolved).
- **K12: not proposed.**

**Nothing enters nature.**

## Against the prior (frozen; kept as written)

- "K1 changes survival (fewer die out)": **wrong**. No meaningful effect. K1 acts in about 3 attempts in 100,
  forms a few more couples, but changes neither births nor survival.
- "K2 alone has no meaningful effect": **not shown either way**; unresolved. Its point estimate, 13 fewer worlds
  dying out, leans toward helping, with an interval reaching the yardstick.
- "K12 is no better than K1": consistent (no meaningful effect).

## Corrections to the record

- **Amendment 1 rested on an unrepresentative smoke test.** Two 8-year worlds had shown no courting of anyone
  without affection. Across the full run, about 12% of attempts were on such people: 2,025 of 17,179 in C, and
  2,007 of 17,172 in K1. K1 raised their acceptances from 20 to 69. So the original K1 check could have been
  measured. K1 acts under either check, so no verdict changes. The claim "nobody courts strangers in 20-founder
  worlds" is withdrawn.
- **The committed analysis was stricter than the signed plan** (checks on K12). That is the assistant's error,
  recorded here.

## What it means for ACL

- Making familiar people more attractive (K1) changes little. In ACL's small worlds, most courting is already among
  people who like each other; refusals come mostly from the 40% chance of saying no. A few more couples form, but
  no more children.
- Loneliness pulling toward courting (K2) acts only weakly. Courting's share of the choice is so small (PF2: about
  1 in 1,000) that doubling it changes little. Yet K2's survival estimate is the only one that leans toward helping.
- PF2's bottleneck, food winning the choice, remains untouched by both.
- **Candidate next step (not proposed):** a confirmatory K2-only run with more seeds, or a study of why couples who
  form do not have more children.
