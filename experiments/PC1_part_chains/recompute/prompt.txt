Blind recompute for a read-only study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study PC1: why lessons of newly invented kinds fail on a missing part). Please compute only from the attached file pc1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Each row is one simulated world (32 worlds, seeds 24501-24532), counting kinds invented in years 100-190 of a 200-year run, followed to year 200. Columns: kinds; depth0, depth1, depth2, depth3 (kinds whose longest chain of made parts is 0, 1, 2, or 3 or more); failed (lessons of these kinds that failed because the learner could not make a made part); miss1, miss2, miss3 (failed lessons with 1, 2, or 3 or more missing made parts); deep1, deep2, deep3 (failed lessons whose deepest missing chain is 1, 2, or 3 or more levels); tk_all, tk_some, tk_none (failed lessons where the teacher knows all, some, or none of the missing parts); part_shown (failed lessons followed by a part lesson); part_knew, part_declined, part_missing_part, part_taken, part_distrust (the part lesson's outcome: the learner already knew the part, declined it, could not follow it, took it, distrusted it); part_was_missing (part lessons that showed a part the learner was missing); pairs (learner-kind pairs with a failed lesson); got_part, got_K, made_K (pairs where, within 5 years, the learner learned a missing part, learned the kind, made the kind). Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, recompute each ratio from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute, each with its interval: (1) depth0..depth3 each divided by kinds; (2) miss1..miss3 and deep1..deep3 each divided by failed; (3) tk_all, tk_some, tk_none each divided by failed; (4) part_shown / failed, and each part outcome divided by part_shown, and part_was_missing / part_shown; (5) not helpful = (part_knew + part_declined + part_missing_part + part_distrust) / part_shown; (6) got_part, got_K, made_K each divided by pairs; (7) the reading: 'could help' if tk_all/failed > 0.5 and not helpful > 0.5; 'could not' if tk_none/failed > 0.5; otherwise 'mixed'. Report every ratio to 3 decimals with its interval, and say whether you used code.
