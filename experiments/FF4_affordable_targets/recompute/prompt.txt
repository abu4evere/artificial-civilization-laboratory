Blind recompute for a fault-fix check in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study FF4: when the best target of an action was too far for a person's energy, the whole action was dropped even if a nearer target was affordable; the fix offers the best affordable target instead). Please compute only from the attached file ff4_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = baseline (v15), f = the fix, x = sham; the same 32 seeds (24801-24832) in every arm. Columns per world (years 100-200 of 200): living_weeks; death_starvation and death_killed (deaths by those causes, all ages); census_adults and census_health (adults 16-49 counted at year ends, and the sum of their health); child_weeks (person-weeks of children under 12); child_deaths and child_death_starvation (deaths of children under 12); adult_weeks (adults 16 and over); gifts_kin_children (food gifts to a child under 12 of the giver's kin); extinct (1 or 0); starving_weeks (weekly decisions of people aged 6+ after a week in which they went short); starving_c (of those, weeks where food was feasible but no food option was offered because energy was below its cost); women30_starving_weeks, women30_starving_c, men30_starving_weeks, men30_starving_c (the same for women and for men aged 30-49). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, recompute every quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. For f against c compute the eight guards: (1) starvation deaths per 1,000 living-years (living_weeks / 52 / 1,000) as a ratio f / c: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (2) killing deaths per 1,000 living-years as a difference f - c: raised if above 0.05 and its lower bound above 0; (3) adult health = sum census_health / sum census_adults, difference f - c: raised if below -5 and its upper bound below 0; (4) living_weeks ratio f / c: raised if below 0.90 and its upper bound below 1; (5) extinct worlds: raised if f has 3 or more more than c; (6) child deaths per 1,000 child-years (child_weeks / 52 / 1,000), ratio: raised if above 1.25, its lower bound above 1, and f's rate exceeds c's by 3 or more; (7) child starvation deaths per 1,000 child-years, ratio: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (8) gifts_kin_children per 1,000 adult_weeks, ratio: raised if below 0.80 and its upper bound below 1. Then, described: (9) P = sum starving_c / sum starving_weeks in each arm, and the ratio f / c with its interval; (10) the same P for women 30-49 and for men 30-49 in each arm. The reading: 'harm: investigate' if any guard is raised; otherwise 'no harm'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals), the guards raised and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
