Blind recompute for a read-only audit in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study EG1: does the energy rule stand between a starving person and food?). Please compute only from the attached file eg1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. One arm, 32 worlds (seeds 24701-24732), years 100-200 of 200, people aged 6 and over, one row per world. Each person-week is a weekly decision. A starving week is one after a week in which the person went short. Each starving week falls in exactly one part: a = a food option (forage, or hunt for those 12 and over) was offered and chosen; b = offered but something else chosen; c = food was feasible (food at the best forage tile, or prey found) but no food option was offered because energy was below its cost; d = no food feasible. Columns: starving_weeks, starving_a, starving_b, starving_c, starving_d; starving_c_quirk (of the c weeks, those where a tile looked at had food and was affordable, though the best tile was not); nonstarving_weeks, nonstarving_c, nonstarving_c_quirk (the same for weeks not starving); starvation_deaths_6plus, starvation_deaths_blocked_last4 (of those deaths, the ones with a c week while starving in the person's last 4 decisions); extinct. Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 worlds with replacement, recompute each ratio from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) the primary P = sum starving_c / sum starving_weeks, with its interval; (2) the shares a, b, c, d of starving weeks; (3) nonstarving_c / nonstarving_weeks with its interval; (4) starving_c_quirk / starving_c with its interval; (5) starvation_deaths_blocked_last4 / starvation_deaths_6plus with its interval. Check that a + b + c + d = starving_weeks in every row. The reading: 'energy binds when starving' if P >= 0.20 and its lower bound >= 0.10; 'energy rarely binds' if P's upper bound < 0.05; otherwise 'a minor contributor'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
