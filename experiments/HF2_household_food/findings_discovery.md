Questions and decision rule frozen as `HF2-questions`. Valid: observer read-only in both arms, all 32 replays reproduce
HB1's and F1's saved populations, code unchanged. Full table: `results_discovery.md`.

## Against the frozen rule (discovery only; the rule is applied when the holdout is in)

| | discovery | needed |
|---|---|---|
| dependants per carer-week, H ÷ C | 1.083 | ≥ 1.2 (mouths) |
| carers' share of H's excess starving adult-weeks | 0.499 | ≥ 0.667 (mouths) |
| food from hunting per adult-week, H ÷ C | 0.812 | ≤ 0.8 (meat) |
| total food in per adult-week lower in H | yes (0.958) | yes (meat) |

**On discovery, neither lead is supported.** The extra starvation falls on every group (carers 50%, partnered adults
without dependants 21%, unpartnered adults 30%). Meat is a negligible share of food in ACL (about 0.02% in both arms), so
whatever hunting does, it cannot account for the hunger.

## Noted, not read (a lead outside the rule; to be looked at in the holdout and beyond)

**Gifts fell.** Food received as gifts per adult-week: H 0.449, C 0.759 (H ÷ C 0.591); food given away: 0.586 against
0.934. Total food in per adult-week fell by 0.404 (9.177 against 9.581), of which 0.310 is gifts and 0.101 foraging. In
ACL, food passing between people protects the hungry (P1, G1); under HB1, partners end every week at home, and the flow
of food between people fell. This is a lead, written down before the holdout, not a finding.
