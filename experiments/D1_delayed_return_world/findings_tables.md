### 4. Storability (from the rules, and from W1)

Weeks until half is gone, with no eating: loose food 17 (4% a week); fired or smoke-kept food 69 (1%); seed 138 (0.5%). Of what people hold at the start of winter, 69% is already seed (W1). Seed sown outside the growing seasons loses 3% a week to animals; standing crop loses 5% a week in winter.
