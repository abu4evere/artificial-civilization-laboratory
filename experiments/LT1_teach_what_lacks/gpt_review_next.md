# GPT's choice of the next candidate, after GR2 and LU1 (23 October 2026)

**Source: GPT (ChatGPT, the creator's account), asked by the assistant with GR2's and LU1's results. GPT's opinion,
in summary; not a verified source and not sign-off.** The creator: "go with gpt decision".

- **Run (b) first, "LT1: teach what the learner lacks".** LU1 shows 34% of failed lessons teach what the learner
  already knows: a direct, measurable mismatch that can be reduced without scripting culture.
- The change: keep the teacher's top-3 valued recipes, filter out those the learner already knows, choose among the
  rest by the existing rule; if none remain, behave as before. Do not change acceptance, teaching frequency or value.
- **Primary:** share of offered lessons about useful inventions that are learned, treatment against control.
  Diagnostic: the "already known" share falls. Safety: survival, time-averaged population, and passing on of useful
  inventions over the whole run. Exploratory: whether learners later use what they learned.
- 256 paired seeds initially; frozen before running; paired effect, bootstrap CI, prespecified test. Adopt only if
  learning improves meaningfully, passing on does not worsen, and survival and population pass prespecified margins;
  if uncertain, confirm on new seeds.
- **Why not (a) first:** defining "would help now" from hunger, cold or health risks encoding a theory of what
  agents should learn. Fix the matching before changing the learner's motivation.

## The assistant's objection (recorded before any plan)

GPT's wording has the teacher check "the learner's recorded recipe inventory". In ACL nobody reads another's mind
(`credence` reads only what the listener can perceive). So the plan uses only what a teacher could know: that the
learner **carries** one of that kind of thing, or that the learner **said so** in an earlier lesson (a lesson that
ended "already known", remembered by the teacher). This is weaker than GPT's version, so its effect may be smaller.

## GPT's review of the LT1 draft (23 October 2026)

- **The perceivable definition is acceptable** (carrying one; having said so to this teacher). Caveat: carrying is
  evidence, not proof. Freeze the eviction rule; key the memory by learner and recipe. Add no inferred knowledge.
- **The draft's primary was flawed** (the assistant's question): a take rate per lesson can rise only because fewer
  wasted lessons are counted. Use **useful recipes learned per person-year** (distinct acquisitions; repeats reported
  separately) as primary; keep the take rate as secondary. Also report useful lessons offered and learned per
  person-year, the "already known" share, and passing on.
- Keep 512 seeds, 120 years, the manipulation check and the safety criteria as drafted.
- **Define "meaningful" numerically before the run**; significance alone is not enough.
- Caution: if the number of lessons offered changes a lot, even per-person-year learning can reflect more teaching
  rather than better matching; so lessons offered per person-year is a diagnostic.
- Verdict: freeze after these clarifications.
