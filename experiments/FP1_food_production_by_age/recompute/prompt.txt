Blind recompute for a read-only study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study FP1: food production by age). Please compute only from the attached file fp1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Each row is one simulated world (seed; 32 worlds, seeds 24301-24332), one age band (0-2, 3-7, 8-11, 12-15, 16-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50+) and one sex, summed over years 100-200 of a 200-year run, in one food unit (1 = an adult-day's need). Columns: person_weeks; production (food gained by foraging plus hunting); forage; hunt; need (the food required); active_weeks (person-weeks in which the person foraged or hunted with a gain); active_production and active_need (production and need in those active weeks only). Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, recompute every quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) for every age band and sex, production / need (pooled sums) with its interval; (2) for each sex, the first age band, in the order listed, whose production / need is at least 1 (the crossover); (3) for bands 30-39 and 40-49 by sex, production / need over all weeks and active_production / active_need, each with its interval, and active_weeks / person_weeks; (4) the hunted share, sum hunt / sum production over all rows, with its interval; (5) production per person-week for the grown (bands 16-29 to 50+, both sexes together). Report every quantity to 3 decimals with its interval. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
