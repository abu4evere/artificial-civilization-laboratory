Blind recompute for a study in an agent-based simulation (ACL study PB2: does a memory-based "brain" that guides choices, with or without a self-chosen goal, change how often people finish what they start?). Please compute only from the attached file pb2_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = baseline (a read-only memory only scores predictions), P = choices steered by the memory, G = P plus a goal chosen by learning progress, G0 = P plus a random goal, x = sham; the same 32 seeds (23201-23232) in every arm. Columns per world: m3_g_chains and m3_g_made (gather chains: a person gathered a material they needed for a recipe they value; made = they made such a recipe within 8 weeks), m3_m_chains and m3_m_made (opportunity-matched chains), living_weeks, extinct (1 if everyone died), and for arm c only: se_rhat and se_q (sums of squared errors of two predictors of the week's learning signal: the memory's estimate and the habit table's value) and n_scored (weeks scored). Compute: (1) for each arm, M3 = sum m3_g_made / sum m3_g_chains - sum m3_m_made / sum m3_m_chains; (2) the differences G - P, G - G0 and P - c in M3, each with a 95% interval by a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, recompute M3 from the pooled sums, take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of each difference; (3) the guards for P, G and G0 each against c: the living-weeks ratio sum(arm) / sum(c) with a 95% interval by the same bootstrap (raised if the ratio is below 0.90 and its upper bound below 1), and the number of extinct worlds minus c's (raised if 3 or more); (4) the validity check in arm c: MSE of the memory = sum se_rhat / sum n_scored, MSE of the habit = sum se_q / sum n_scored, and the difference (habit - memory) with a 95% interval by a bootstrap over arm c's 32 worlds (pooled sums); it passes if the interval lies wholly above 0; (5) the reading: "a self-chosen goal helps people finish what they start" only if no guard is raised for G, the validity check passes, and both the G - P and the G - G0 intervals lie wholly above 0. Report M3 per arm (4 decimals), the three differences with intervals, the guards, the two MSEs and their difference with its interval, and the reading.
