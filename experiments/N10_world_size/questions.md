# N10 — How big should their world be? (questions, written and committed before any search)

**Asked by the creator (October 2026):** "40 × 40 feels too small for them to develop." The world's size and its
founding population are settings (`width`, `height`, `founders`; today 40 × 40 tiles and 20 founders), not nature. The
record so far says worlds die from failing to meet partners (F1, F2, P2, G1), not from lack of land: about 20–40 people
use a few patches of 1,600 tiles, and wild food stands near capacity. Before any design (a possible study S1), the
creator asked for the evidence. **Read-only evidence review: no code, no experiment, no choice.**

## Questions

1. **Population:** how many people make up a self-sustaining forager population (a "mating network", dialect group,
   or tribe), and how small can one be before it fails by chance (minimum viable population)?
2. **Density:** how many people per square kilometre do foragers live at, and how does it vary with environment?
3. **Bands within the population:** how many residential bands make up one such population, and how large is a band?
4. **Space per band:** how large a home range does one band use?
5. **Scale in ACL's terms:** does any of this let ACL's tiles be given a size in kilometres without choosing a number?
   (N8 recorded that ACL has no scale.)

## Anchor criteria (fixed now)

- **Population size anchored** if at least **two independent sources** give a quantitative figure for a forager
  mating-network / tribe size or a minimum viable population, from data or a model fitted to data.
- **Density anchored** if at least two independent sources give quantitative forager densities (people per km²), with
  the environment stated.
- **Scale (question 5) anchored** only if the conversion from tiles to kilometres follows from ACL's own existing rules
  plus sourced figures, without any number chosen by us. Otherwise it is recorded as not anchored, and any study must be
  framed in **people per tile** (density) and **number of people**, not in kilometres.
- A figure from a single ethnography, or from a model with assumed parameters only, is recorded as partial.
- **Not decided here:** what world size or number of founders ACL should use, or whether to run S1. That is for the
  creator, after this review.

## Sources and method

PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, bioRxiv, as in N9; each source marked "full text read", "abstract read" or "record only";
no volume/page numbers unless retrieved. Sources already in the record (White 2017 from N5; Pearce 2014; Hill et al.
2014; Binford's database as used by Pearce) are reused and marked as carried.
