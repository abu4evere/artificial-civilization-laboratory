inherited weight on the old hand-written score, **rose** from 1.00 to 1.23. It was meant to fall.

## What it means (the assistant's reading, for the creator and the council)
- **The network did not take over.** Selection pushed g up, toward the old rules. The likeliest reason is that the
  network's advice was worse than the rules', so people who leaned on the rules did better. Learning within life made
  survival worse, which points the same way.
- **Primary 1's "yes" is not evidence that the brain improves.** The same rise appears without learning, and it may
  come from the people's own demography.
- **Its price is real:** more starvation, more killing and more child deaths than v17, although the population is
  larger.
- **The assistant's prior:**
  - viable 55% (yes);
  - g falls 30% (no);
  - primary 1 yes 25% (yes, but see above);
  - inherited improvement 20% (no);
  - most likely "decorative" or "no improvement shown" (decorative).
- **Not decided here:** what comes next (an autopsy of why learning hurts, the reward signal, or a different brain). That
  is for the creator and the council.
