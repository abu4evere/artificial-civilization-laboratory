**On discovery, gifts do not explain the hunger by the frozen rule.** Gifts fell, but starving adults under HB1 had
someone with a surplus within reach as often as in the control (40.0% against 39.0% of starving adult-weeks). Help was
within reach as often; it came less often. **Meetings did not fall** (the assistant's prior, wrong for the third time
running). What fell were **surplus** (fewer adults holding food above two weeks of need plus dependants') and **choice**
(fewer gifts per opportunity), almost equally; the channel is named only if the same one is lowest, ≤ 0.9, in both sets.

**Noted, not read (outside the rule):** starving rose under HB1 even among adults who had received a gift in the last
8 weeks (0.043 against 0.024); and more of the gift food that flowed stayed inside a home (80% against 59%, among givers
and recipients who both had a couple home), with homes of givers and recipients closer together (1.78 against 3.27
tiles). Gifts under HB1 circulated more within households and less between them.
