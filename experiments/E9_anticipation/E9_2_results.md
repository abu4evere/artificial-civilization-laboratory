## Frozen predictions, scored

1. **confirmed** — children of carers with 50+ starved at 0% against 1.11% for carers under 20. With 129 such
   child-winters only about 1.4 deaths would be expected even at the base rate, so the zero alone is weak; the
   strong evidence is the gradient from under 20 to 20–49 (1.11% → 0.20%, 5.5-fold, over 13,000 child-winters).
2. **confirmed** — in Arm 0, 0.2% of child-winters had a carer with 50+ (prediction: under 5%).
3. **confirmed** — A2-full raised that share to 1.8%, nine times Arm 0's.

## What this says

A carer's food at the start of winter is what stands between their children and starvation: a few weeks' more food
cuts a child's risk five-fold. So the reward for laying food by is real and large, but it falls on the children, not
on the person who does the work. That is why an adult's own experience of a winter so rarely teaches them to store
(E9.1): their own risk in any one winter is small, and the lives a store would save are someone else's.
