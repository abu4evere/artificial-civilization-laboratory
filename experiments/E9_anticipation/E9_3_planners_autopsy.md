# E9.3 — The first winter planners · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Written:** 2026-10-06, after E10, before any E9.3 run. Requested by the creator ("the first naturally occurring
winter planners in the entire history of ACL"). Not to be edited after the results.
**Type:** read-only replays of E9 A2-full (A2 code, weight 1.0, seeds 301–304, 120 years), checked against E9's saved
populations.

**Who:** adults who held 80 or more food in hand at some point in an autumn ("planners"), compared with adults who
went short the winter before but did not ("short, not planning").

## Measures

For each planner-autumn and each comparison autumn: age, generation, temperament (care, diligence, anxiety,
curiosity, kindness), foraging skill, number of dependents, winter expectation of going short; and through the
following winter: food given away (give events), food left in heaps (put events), food in hand at the first and last
week of winter, whether they starved, whether any child in their care starved, and whether they planned again the
next autumn.

## Predictions

1. **They are carers.** Planners have more dependents at the start of winter than the comparison group (means).
2. **The food leaves their hands.** On average, planners give away at least a quarter of what they held at the first
   week of winter, during that winter.
3. **Planning is not a habit.** Fewer than a third of planners plan again the next autumn.
