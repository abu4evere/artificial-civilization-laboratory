# After E9 — options for the creator (DRAFT, nothing decided)

**Written:** 2026-10-06 by the assistant while the creator was away. These are options to choose from, not decisions.
None has code, and none should be built before the creator chooses and a pre-registration is frozen.

## Where E9 left things

- A season-ahead feeling of going short, learned from experience the way thirst is, reaches nearly everyone but is
  faint (E9.1): it is learned as the *share* of a winter's weeks spent short, which for most adults is small.
- The few people with a strong lesson did exactly what the hypothesis predicted: foraged in three autumn weeks of
  four and entered winter with six times the food (E9).
- For an adult, a single winter rarely kills (0.6% of adult-winters). Much of winter's toll falls on infants and
  children, through their carers. E9.2 is measuring whether a carer's store protects them.

## The question all options share

Can the lesson of a hard winter be strong enough to change behaviour, while still coming only from what a person
lived through or witnessed, and never from a rule about seasons, storing or tools?

## Options

### O1 — Remember the worst, not the average
- **Change:** the expectation of going short in a season is learned from how bad the worst week of that season was
  (for example, the share of a week's need that went unmet, at its peak), and fades slowly year by year, instead of
  being the share of weeks spent short.
- **Why it is grounded:** people remember a hard season by its worst moments. A single terrible winter would leave a
  strong lesson; a mild one, a weak one.
- **Risk:** a strong lesson from one bad winter could make people over-forage in autumn for years, depleting plants.

### O2 — Losing a child to winter (grief-tied)
- **Change:** when a person's own child or ward starves in a season, their expectation for that season is set high,
  and fades slowly. Grief already exists in the mind; this ties it to the season the loss happened in.
- **Why it is grounded:** the deaths that matter most in ACL's winters are children's, and the people best placed to
  prevent them are their carers. A carer who lost a child in winter has the strongest reason in the world to lay food
  by, and it comes only from what they lived through.
- **Depends on:** E9.2. If a carer's store does not protect children, O2 has nothing to work with.

### O3 — Witnessed hunger (A3 from the candidates)
- **Change:** a weaker lesson from seeing someone nearby starve or die of hunger in a season.
- **Why:** spreads the lesson beyond those who suffered it, including to the young, without anyone being told.
- **Risk:** harder to interpret; best tested only beside a version that works on its own.

### O4 — Stories (A4 from the candidates)
- **Change:** a weak lesson from hearing a remembered story of someone starving in a season.
- **Why:** lets a lesson outlive the people who learned it: the most civilisation-like route.
- **Risk:** the most moving parts; last in line.
