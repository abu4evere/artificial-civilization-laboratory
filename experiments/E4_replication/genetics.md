# Population genetics in the finished worlds (read-only)

Temperament has been inherited since the first version: each child gets the average of the parents' 8 traits plus random drift (sd 0.08). Nothing else is inherited. This looks at what 200 years did to it, in 24 worlds under each code.

## E1 code

- **Diversity within a world:** mean trait variance among the founders 0.0511; among the living at the end 0.0127 (-75%).
- **Islands becoming different:** in 10 worlds with at least two islands holding 5+ people at the end, the share of each trait's variation that lies between islands (0 = alike, 1 = wholly different):

| trait | between-island share (mean over worlds) | mean change from the founders | survival to 16 (correlation) | children, if adult (correlation) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| curiosity | 0.230 | -0.031 | +0.026 | -0.055 |
| diligence | 0.206 | -0.017 | +0.007 | -0.023 |
| sociability | 0.155 | +0.003 | -0.047 | +0.061 |
| kindness | 0.342 | -0.021 | -0.002 | -0.016 |
| temper | 0.208 | -0.017 | +0.002 | -0.011 |
| anxiety | 0.131 | -0.014 | -0.002 | +0.021 |
| care | 0.155 | +0.025 | +0.042 | -0.010 |
| boldness | 0.270 | -0.017 | -0.012 | +0.014 |

## E2 code

- **Diversity within a world:** mean trait variance among the founders 0.0505; among the living at the end 0.0111 (-78%).
- **Islands becoming different:** in 1 worlds with at least two islands holding 5+ people at the end, the share of each trait's variation that lies between islands (0 = alike, 1 = wholly different):

| trait | between-island share (mean over worlds) | mean change from the founders | survival to 16 (correlation) | children, if adult (correlation) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| curiosity | 0.007 | -0.032 | -0.033 | -0.030 |
| diligence | 0.498 | +0.038 | +0.005 | +0.044 |
| sociability | 0.450 | +0.010 | -0.075 | +0.101 |
| kindness | 0.407 | +0.002 | +0.035 | -0.007 |
| temper | 0.456 | -0.009 | +0.002 | -0.038 |
| anxiety | 0.100 | -0.012 | +0.001 | -0.004 |
| care | 0.629 | +0.001 | +0.091 | -0.055 |
| boldness | 0.468 | +0.027 | +0.020 | +0.019 |

Reading the correlations: positive means people with more of the trait were more likely to reach 16, or had more children. With thousands of people, values near ±0.05 are small but real; the sign is the point.
