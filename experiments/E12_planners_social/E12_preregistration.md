# E12 — The planners' social world · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Written:** 2026-10-06, after E11, before any E12 run. Requested by the creator: before any new mechanism, find out

**Type:** read-only replays of E9 A2-full (A2 code, weight 1.0, seeds 301–304, 120 years), checked against E9.

## Known from the code before running (not predictions)

There are only two ways one person copies another in ACL:
1. **Watching** (`knowhow.watch`): someone seen doing well with a thing can be copied, as a recipe for making that
   thing, and only when landing on new shore by raft or hunting together.
2. **Upbringing** (`biology._upbringing`): children slowly take on the learned habits (`q`) of whoever raises them.

A planner's preparation comes from an expectation (`expect["short@3"]`), not from a habit or a recipe, so neither
channel can carry it, and **no adult can copy what a neighbour does**. Whether anyone copied the planners therefore has
a known answer: not directly. What is measured is whether the conditions for copying were there: visibility, respect,
indirect change in those around them, and what their children carried.

## Groups

**Planners**: adults holding 80+ food in an autumn. **Comparison**: adults who went short the winter before and did
not plan. **Neighbours**: adults who spent at least 4 of the 13 autumn weeks within sight (4 tiles) of a planner (or
of a comparison adult), and are neither.

## Measures

For each planner and comparison adult in that autumn and winter: adults within sight per autumn week; respect and
trust others hold toward them (sum over others' relations) at the start of autumn and at the end of winter; food given
and to how many people. For neighbours: autumn foraging share and planning the next autumn. For children in their care
that winter: whether, as adults (16+), they ever planned before the run ends.

## Predictions

1. **Planners were visible.** They had at least as many adults within sight per autumn week as comparison adults.
2. **Planners gained standing.** The respect others hold toward planners rises more from the start of autumn to the end
   of winter than toward comparison adults (mean change).
3. **Neighbours did not change.** Neighbours of planners forage in the next autumn within ±10% of neighbours of
   comparison adults.
4. **Neighbours did not plan.** The share of planners' neighbours who plan the next autumn is no more than 0.5
   percentage points above that of comparison adults' neighbours.
