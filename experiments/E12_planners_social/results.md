## What this says

The creator's question had three possible answers: nobody noticed; people noticed but did not copy; or people already
copy the successful. It is the second. The planners were more visible than most, fed more people, and gained standing.
Nothing around them changed: their neighbours foraged and planned like everyone else's, and their children planned as
adults no more often than anyone's. That matches the code exactly. ACL's people can copy a recipe by watching (only for
rafts and hunts) and children absorb their carers' habits, but a planner's preparation lives in an expectation that no
channel carries, and no adult can copy what a neighbour does. The bridge between a visible, respected planner and a
people that prepares is missing, and it is now precisely located.
