Blind recompute for a study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study OD1: a package of five general laws that open what made things can do, tested in a copy, with a sham). Please compute only from the attached file od1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = baseline (v16), o = the five doors (D1 a held thing within 10 in all ten properties may stand in for a recipe input; D2 a container's carry factor also multiplies the made-thing and material limits; D3 a flexible made thing can bind; D4 a hard made thing can be a hammer; D5 several things add up for warmth worn and for carrying), p = the same doors without D1, x = sham; the same 32 seeds (25301-25332) in every arm. Columns per world (years 100-200 of 200): living_weeks; death_starvation and death_killed (deaths by those causes, all ages); census_adults and census_health (adults 16-49 counted at year ends, and the sum of their health); child_weeks (person-weeks of children under 12); child_deaths and child_death_starvation (deaths of children under 12); adult_weeks (adults 16 and over); gifts_kin_children (food gifts to a child under 12 of the giver's kin); extinct (1 or 0); then the manipulation counts: grown_weeks (person-weeks of people 16 and over), made_by_make (things made by the make action), made_standin (of those, made with a stand-in input), over_items_weeks and over_mats_weeks (grown person-weeks above the old limits of 3 made things and 20 materials), made_binder_used, made_hammer_used, layer_cold_weeks and layer_carry_weeks (grown person-weeks where a second thing adds warmth or carrying). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, recompute every quantity from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. For each of o, p and x against c compute the eight guards: (1) starvation deaths per 1,000 living-years (living_weeks / 52 / 1,000) as a ratio arm / c: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (2) killing deaths per 1,000 living-years as a difference arm - c: raised if above 0.05 and its lower bound above 0; (3) adult health = sum census_health / sum census_adults, difference arm - c: raised if below -5 and its upper bound below 0; (4) living_weeks ratio arm / c: raised if below 0.90 and its upper bound below 1; (5) extinct worlds: raised if the arm has 3 or more more than c; (6) child deaths per 1,000 child-years (child_weeks / 52 / 1,000), ratio: raised if above 1.25, its lower bound above 1, and the arm's rate exceeds c's by 3 or more; (7) child starvation deaths per 1,000 child-years, ratio: raised if above 1.25 and its lower bound above 1; (8) gifts_kin_children per 1,000 adult_weeks, ratio: raised if below 0.80 and its upper bound below 1. Then, per arm, the manipulation counts: made_standin per 1,000 made_by_make, and each other count per 1,000 grown_weeks, with the totals. The reading: o goes live if it raises no guard; if o raises a guard and p raises none, p goes live; if both raise a guard, nothing goes live. A door whose counts all total fewer than 32 in the arm that goes live is 'barely exercised'. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals), the guards raised per arm and the reading.
