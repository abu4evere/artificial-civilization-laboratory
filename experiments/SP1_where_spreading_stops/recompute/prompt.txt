Blind recompute for a read-only study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study SP1: where the spreading of new kinds of made things stops). Please compute only from the attached file sp1_counts.csv; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Each row is one simulated world (32 worlds, seeds 24401-24432). It counts the newly invented kinds born in years 100-190 of a 200-year run and followed to year 200. Columns: born (kinds); offered (kinds that were ever among a teacher's offered lessons); shown (kinds ever picked as a lesson); learned_taught (kinds ever learned through a lesson by someone other than the inventor); learned_social (kinds learned by a non-inventor through a lesson, watching or reading marks); learner_had_parts (kinds where such a learner had all the parts in hand in some week); made_by_learner (kinds made by such a learner); lessons_shown, lessons_declined, lessons_missing_part, lessons_taken (counts of lessons of these kinds, and their outcomes). Use a world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw 32 seeds with replacement, recompute each ratio from the pooled sums of the drawn worlds, and take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) the pooled totals of every column; (2) the five step rates offered/born, shown/offered, learned_taught/shown, learner_had_parts/learned_social, made_by_learner/learner_had_parts, each with its interval; (3) which step has the lowest rate, and whether its interval overlaps any other step's interval; (4) lessons_declined/lessons_shown, lessons_missing_part/lessons_shown, lessons_taken/lessons_shown. Report every rate to 4 decimal places, and say how you computed it (code or by hand).
