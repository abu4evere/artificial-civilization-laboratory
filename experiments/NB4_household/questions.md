**Why:**

- **NB3's lesson:** only the feeling that acts directly on the choice (the inborn reluctance H) changed behaviour.
  Feelings that only reach the learning signal did not.

## The new parts (on NB3's arm f; each with an inherited gene, start values to be set by a scale audit, range 0–2)
- **(a) Household restraint (gene Q):** the score of "court" (and of conceiving, if the world has that choice), minus
  Q × the household's food shortfall. The shortfall is 1 − (food held by the household ÷ the household's need over the
  next 8 weeks), floored at 0. It acts on the choice directly, like H.
- **(b) Reciprocity (gene K):** each person remembers who gave them food in the last 52 weeks, and how much. The
  score of "give" to someone one owes is plus K × what one owes them. Sharing beyond the household then pays back the
  givers.
- **(c) Children's survival (gene S):** a feeling of +S when one's own child reaches age 5, and −S when one's own
  child under 12 dies (added to that week's r). GPT's choice; Gemini warned that the delay is long for a weekly
  learner.
