# GPT's view of TP1 (21 October 2026; GPT, not a verified source)

Asked in a new chat in the ACL ChatGPT project ("AI World (2)" was full). The first message arrived cut off. GPT
first answered generally, then answered again with full context. Its second answer is condensed here; the substance
is kept and labelled as GPT's.

**Verdict:** "mostly fair", and right not to run anything new. Three tightenings:
1. W* = 0 is "the smallest threshold satisfying your chosen statistical rule", not a shown biological tipping point.
   The drop per woman is not a steady 10–20 points (−6, −16, −19, −13, −11 from W 0 to 5).
2. World-years are not independent: each world counts up to five times, and later check years hold only survivors.
   Keep the frozen result; add a one-row-per-world check and per-check-year tables as supplementary, labelled as
   added afterwards.
3. W may be confounded with total population. It suggested a table of W within bands of population size; report
   small cells as unresolved rather than merging them.
- Keep F1's warning sign as a high-risk region, not a point of no return.
- The no-eligible-man comparison does not show the expected relationship. Record that, rather than explain it away.
- Scope: the result describes ACL's 20-founder, 120-year worlds; it is not a real minimum viable population.
