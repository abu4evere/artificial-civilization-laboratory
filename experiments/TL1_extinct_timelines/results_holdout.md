# TL1 Results — Holdout Set

**Sample:** 256 control worlds (80 extinct, 176 surviving to year 120).

---

## Q1. The Reproductive Gap (ΔT = T_extinct − T_last_birth)

- **Median ΔT:** 46.0 years (mean 44.7 years)
- **ΔT ≥ 20 years:** 98.8% (79 / 80 extinct worlds)
- **ΔT < 10 years:** 0.0% (0 / 80 extinct worlds)
- **Frozen Reading:** **Ageing out**

---

## Q2. Mate Availability at Last Birth (t = T_last_birth)

- **Qualifying extinct worlds (`women_single > 0` at t = T_last_birth):** 70 / 80 (87.5%)
- **Mean eligible share:** 60.6% (median 95.0%)
- **Distribution across qualifying worlds:**
  - Share = 100% (all single women had eligible men): 35 / 70 (50.0%)
  - 0% < Share < 100%: 13 / 70 (18.6%)
  - Share = 0% (mate pool exhausted): 22 / 70 (31.4%)
- **Frozen Reading:** **failure to pair**
