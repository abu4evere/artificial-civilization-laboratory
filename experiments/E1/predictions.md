# E1 — Long Histories · Predictions (FROZEN)

**Experiment:** E1 — Long Histories. Features frozen; only measurement added.
**Configuration:** seeds 101–108, 200 years each (results also read at year 100),
20 founders in 4 bands on separate islands, 40 × 40 map, weekly ticks.
**Versions:** World Bible 7.1 · world 0.2 · agent 0.2.
**Written:** 2026-10-02, before any E1 run, by Claude, from what 6–30 year runs
had shown. They must not be edited after the results are in. Each is scored
as **confirmed**, **wrong**, or **unclear**.

The question: given the laws as they now stand, what does the world do over
generations that we did not put in by hand?

---

## Population (context)

1. At least one of the 8 seeds dies out within 200 years. At least half hold
   or grow.
2. Contact between islands happens in most surviving seeds by year 100
   (rafts appear early), but strong trust across islands stays rare.

## Shared symbols (Laws 22, 23)

3. At least 6 of 8 seeds have one or more shared symbols at some point.
4. Shared symbols are short-lived: across all seeds, no more than 3 last 20
   years or more.
5. Fewer than half of all symbols ever formed reach someone born after the
   symbol formed.
6. Symbols almost never span islands: at most 2 across all seeds.

## False beliefs (Laws 24, 25, 26, 49, 71)

7. Wrong versions stay under 10% of the stories people hold as hearsay, at
   both year 100 and year 200.
8. Shared false stories (myths) stay rare: on average no more than 2 per seed
   per 100 years.
9. Superstitions (beliefs crediting flint left lying, or striking without
   flint) make up 5–20% of tested beliefs. In at least one seed, one is held
   by 3 or more people at the same time.

## Inventions (Laws 29, 105–120)

10. All six named kinds (blade, spear, club, carrier, wrap, raft) are invented
    in at least 6 of 8 seeds by year 100.
11. Hundreds of distinct techniques are tried per seed. Fewer than 10% are
    ever held by 3 or more people at once.

## Technique survival and loss (Laws 50, 57, 114)

12. Most techniques die young: the median technique is known for under 5 years.
13. A small core lasts: in most surviving seeds, at least one technique is
    still known at year 200 that crossed 3 or more generations.
14. Losses outnumber rediscoveries at least 3 to 1.

## Trust networks (Laws 36, 56, 84, 92)

15. Mean trust among living people stays positive (0.2–0.5). Strong-trust
    clusters roughly match bands, and the largest cluster is usually under
    80% of the population.
16. People who turn out right as sources are trusted somewhat more. The
    pooled correlation between accuracy and trust is positive but weak
    (0.05–0.35), because claims are rarely tested.

## Surprise loops (Laws 63–66, 75)

17. Most puzzles end without being settled: under 35% end "settled by
    testing".
18. Puzzles do drive discovery: 10–30% of new techniques are first made while
    a puzzle holds the maker's attention.
19. Fire made by hand after a sparks puzzle happens in at least half the
    surviving seeds.

---

## What would change my mind

- If symbols last generations and spread across islands, meaning is far
  stickier than these rules suggest, and Law 23 deserves a closer look.
- If accuracy and trust correlate strongly (above 0.5), the influence chain
  of Law 92 is emerging in full.
- If techniques almost never cross generations, transmission is too weak for
  Law 57 (accumulation) ever to happen, and civilization cannot build up
  under the current laws.
