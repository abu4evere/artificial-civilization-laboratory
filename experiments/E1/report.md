# E1 — Long Histories · Report for review

8 seeds (101–108), up to 200 years each. Features were frozen for the whole experiment; the only code added measures and reports. Predictions were written and frozen before the first run (`predictions.md`). Detailed tables: `summary.md`.

## 1. Prediction scorecard

10 confirmed · 8 wrong · 1 unclear, out of 19.

1. **confirmed** — 2 of 8 died out; 6 held or grew (20+ alive at the end).
2. **unclear** — cross-island strong trust appeared by year 100 in 5 of 6 surviving seeds; at the end it was rare (≤2 ties) in 6. Contact itself is not measured directly.
3. **confirmed** — 8 of 8 seeds had a shared symbol at some point.
4. **wrong** — 55 symbols lasted 20+ years in total.
5. **confirmed** — 261 of 763 symbols reached someone born after they formed.
6. **wrong** — 236 symbols were shared by people on 2+ islands.
7. **wrong** — the highest share of wrong versions among stories held (where 10+ were held) was 76%.
8. **confirmed** — 29 myths over 1462 seed-years = 2.0 per seed per century.
9. **wrong** — superstitions averaged 5% of tested beliefs; the most people holding one at once was 10.
10. **confirmed** — 8 of 8 seeds invented all six named kinds by year 100.
11. **confirmed** — techniques tried per seed: 147–633; 10% were ever held by 3+ people at once.
12. **wrong** — median technique life per seed: 0.2, 24.9, 32.0, 30.9, 21.6, 34.9, 29.5, 22.4 years.
13. **confirmed** — in 6 of 6 surviving seeds, a technique still known at the end was held by people two or more generations after its inventor (three generations in all).
14. **confirmed** — 3828 losses vs 789 rediscoveries.
15. **wrong** — mean trust averaged 0.14; the largest strong-trust cluster was under 80% of people in 93% of seed-years.
16. **wrong** — pooled accuracy↔trust r = -0.066 over 336 people.
17. **confirmed** — 13% of 93437 puzzles ended settled by testing.
18. **wrong** — 197 of 3441 new techniques (6%) were first made while puzzling.
19. **confirmed** — fire struck after a sparks puzzle in 6 of 6 surviving seeds.
