# GPT's review of the G1 draft, with its late view of MR1 (22 October 2026)

**Source: GPT (ChatGPT, the creator's account), asked by the assistant. GPT's opinions, recorded as given in summary;
not verified sources and not sign-off.** The question sent is kept with the assistant's notes; it described MR1's
result and the G1 draft.

## On MR1 (a review of a frozen plan, after its results)
- Agrees it should not be adopted: the primary outcome worsened materially.
- The explanation (established recipes lost before being used enough) is **plausible, not proven** by the results.
- "Built on by a learner" doubling cannot rescue it without its uncertainty, denominator and link to useful knowledge.
- Objection: MR1 changed the memory system too broadly (both what is kept and how long). Test one narrow change at a time.

## On G1
- Supports running it. 13 weeks is sensible as a starting point, but a hypothesis, not an established threshold.
- Objections: **displacement** (an older valuable recipe may be dropped to protect an untested one); protection is
  **passive** (keeping an invention does not make anyone use, value or teach it); the manipulation check alone is not
  enough; the all-new fallback can still drop a new recipe, so **count how often it happens**.
- Add two diagnostics: **displacement cost** (how often protecting a new recipe causes an older useful one to be
  lost) and **the transmission pathway** (inventions used, crossing 0.15, offered in lessons, learned), to tell
  failure at the memory stage from failure at the teaching stage.
- Keep the adoption rule; do not adopt on retention alone.

## Better candidates
- **V1, a small positive starting value** (kept below 0.15 so it cannot skip the teaching threshold): the one it
  would investigate next if G1 fails. Risk: favouring novelty over proven knowledge.
- T1, an early teaching opportunity: risk of making teaching artificial.
- **Do not combine G1 and V1 yet.**

## What the assistant takes from it
- All of GPT's diagnostics are added to G1 as described measures (Revision 1). They change no test.
- V1 is noted as the next candidate if G1 fails. Not combined.
