# GPT's view of GR1's results (22 October 2026)

**Source: GPT (ChatGPT, the creator's account), asked by the assistant after GR1 (frozen as G1) was run and read.
GPT's opinions, in summary; not verified sources and not sign-off. It changes no frozen test.**

1. **Confirm first (option b); do not adopt into v4 yet, and do not reject.** The frozen rule was met, but the
   effect is tiny and the interval touches zero; meeting the rule is not proof the change is harmless. It notes that
   the +0.43-point mean difference and the difference in medians are different quantities.
2. **The population signal deserves a frozen check.** It is not evidence of harm (exploratory, p = 0.057), but a
   memory change could affect survival or reproduction indirectly. Its proposed confirmation ("GR2"):
   - the change unchanged, entirely new seeds, 512 pairs (or more if a prospective power estimate from MR1's and GR1's
     paired variability says so), 120 years, the same pipeline, frozen before data;
   - the same primary (share learned by someone else; paired mean difference, bootstrap CI, Wilcoxon);
   - **a population safety outcome:** time-averaged population over years 1–120, with years 60 and 119, the
     trajectory and extinction reported;
   - **a non-inferiority margin**, provisionally 5% of control's time-averaged population, to be chosen and
     justified by the creator before the run;
   - adopt only if transmission improves by the frozen criterion, the lower bound of the relative population
     difference stays above −5%, and extinction does not meaningfully worsen; if population safety is inconclusive,
     keep GR1 experimental, not default.
3. **Next bottleneck: lesson uptake (offered → learned).** Its candidate "LA1", need-sensitive uptake: the learner is
   somewhat more likely to accept a lesson when the recipe would help with a problem they face now; modest,
   probabilistic, teacher behaviour unchanged. Its objection to its own idea: it is not yet shown that lessons fail
   for lack of relevance. They might fail through distance, time, teacher availability or poor demonstrations. So
   **first instrument the offer-to-learning funnel read-only**, then choose.
