## What each feeling did
- **The inborn reluctance (H) ended the killing.** Killed, as a share of all deaths:
  - n2 12.8%, f0 11.6%; **fH 0.1%, f 0.0%**, f−E 0.2%; **f−H 11.0%**.
  - Attacks fell from 0.26% of adult acts to 0.00%, and stealing likewise.
- **Taking away the world's +0.1 per attack ("no joy in attacking", f0) did almost nothing** (killing 11.6%;
  population 0.22).
- **Guilt, empathy, love, approval and curiosity, without H (f−H), did not stop the violence either** (11.0%).
  They act only through learning, and learning was too slow or too weak to unlearn harm.
  - The feeling that worked is the one that acts directly on the choice, like an instinct.
- **Hunger is now the main killer.** Starvation is 44% of deaths in f (v17 19%); child starvation ×2.28 [1.86, 2.80];
  old age 26% (v17 48%).
  - Gifts to kin's children are at v17's level (×0.98). So the feelings did not make people feed others more.
- **Survival to 16 per birth:** f 0.781, n2 0.812, v17 0.875.
  - f − n2 −0.031 [−0.060, −0.004]; f − fH −0.033 [−0.063, −0.001].
  - The other feelings slightly *lowered* each child's chance, while far more children were born.
  - Empathy changed nothing measurable (f − f−E −0.000).
- **The feeling genes barely moved** in 600 years (E 0.50→0.52, G 0.50→0.54, H 1.00→0.96).
  - **g (the weight on the old instincts) rose a little** (1.00→1.08), as in NB2: the instincts are still favoured.
- **The network still changes the top choice in about a quarter of decisions** (f 0.28), as in NB2.

## What it means (the assistant's reading)
1. **Violence is solved, by an inborn brake, not by learned feelings.** A reluctance wired into the choice works at
   once. Feelings that only change the learning signal did not stop harm within 600 years.
2. **The new bottleneck is food.** With killing gone, the neural people starve about twice as often as the old minds,
   and children most. NB2-A already saw starvation beside kin with plenty. Feelings did not fix sharing.
3. **The next brain question is feeding:**

   - A read-only autopsy of f's starvation (as NB2-A) would show whether it is finding food, keeping it, or giving it
     to children.
