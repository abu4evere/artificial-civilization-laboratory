Blind recompute for a study in an agent-based simulation of hunter-gatherers (ACL study NB3: a neural-network brain that learns from a weekly feeling number; can a people with such brains live in the open world if it is born with human-like feelings?). Please compute only from the attached files; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. Arms: c = the old rule-based minds; n2 = the neural brain of the previous study, no feelings; f0 = n2 without the reward the world gave for attacking; fH = f0 plus an inborn reluctance to attack or steal; f = all feelings (empathy, guilt, love of one's own child, approval and shame, curiosity, the inborn reluctance); fmE = f without empathy; fmH = f without the inborn reluctance. The same 16 seeds (25801-25816) in every arm; 600 years. Files: nb3_worlds.csv (per world: extinct 1/0; living_weeks summed over years 100-600; known_births = people born in years 100-600 whose outcome is known, and reached16 = those of them who reached age 16); nb3_genes.csv (arm f, per world: the mean of inherited genes g, E, G, L, A, C, H at years 1-600; blank after extinction); nb3_garden.csv (phase 2: genomes from generations 1 and 18 of each f world put as all founders into fresh identical worlds, arms g1Z and g18Z, with g held at 0 and learning off; per source world: founders, founders alive after one year, known = children born by year 44, reached16). Use a paired world bootstrap: 10,000 times draw the 16 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds (with multiplicities) for every arm, and recompute each quantity from the drawn worlds; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute: (1) extinct worlds per arm; (2) the population ratio of each other arm to c = sum living_weeks / sum living_weeks, with its point value and interval; (3) survival to 16 per birth for each arm = sum reached16 / sum known_births, and the paired differences f - n2, f0 - n2, fH - f0, f - fH, f - fmE, f - fmH, f - c; (4) the mean over worlds of each gene at each listed year (worlds with a value); (5) phase 2: the mean over source worlds of alive_1y/founders for g1Z and for g18Z, and for each source seed reached16/known in g18Z minus g1Z, the mean difference, each with a bootstrap over source seeds. The primary reading: 'f viable' if f's extinct worlds are at most c's + 3 and the upper bound of the f/c population ratio is at least 0.80; otherwise 'f not viable'. Apply the same rule to every other arm too. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached files with your code tool.
