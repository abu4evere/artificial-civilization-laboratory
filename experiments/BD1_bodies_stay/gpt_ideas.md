# GPT's ideas on bodies, digging and water (23 October 2026)

**Source: GPT (ChatGPT, the creator's account), asked by the assistant at the creator's request ("first ask gpt
about his ideas and give me yours also"). GPT's opinions, in summary; not verified sources and not sign-off.**

Principle: three changes to physics, not three cultural systems. Nature creates consequences and opportunities;
agents invent meanings, practices and taboos.

- **Bodies persist:** on death, a body stays where the person died and changes with time (decay, loss of edible
  tissue, eventual disappearance), at a rate set by temperature, moisture and exposure, not one fixed timer.
  Scavenging and carrying only if existing actions and animals can support them. Grief stays; disgust or avoidance
  should arise from perceivable cues and existing feelings, not a corpse-specific response. A body can be physically
  usable as food, but with no special cannibalism action or preference.
- **Digging:** where terrain and tools permit, at an energy or time cost; makes a lasting pit (or changes the ground)
  that can hold things, may collect water, and can be filled or covered. Pits must not automatically be graves,
  stores or homes.
- **Water:** an object entering water floats, sinks or stays near the surface by its existing float property; drift
  only if water already moves in ACL. Floating things may drift or be recovered; sunk things harder to recover. Do
  not destroy lost items or make them always unrecoverable.
- **Do not add:** a burial instinct, respect for the dead, mandatory corpse avoidance, automatic disgust at corpses,
  a cannibalism taboo, graves or rituals or beliefs, pits that are always for the dead or for storage, guaranteed
  scavengers, guaranteed corpse eating, guaranteed recovery.
- **Evidence it named:** decomposition depends on temperature, moisture, exposure and burial (forensic research);
  storage pits among hunter-gatherers (archaeology); the treatment of the dead varies greatly among foragers. (Named
  by GPT; not yet checked by the assistant.)
- **Order it advised:** water first (most isolated), then digging, then bodies; one Law 126 study each, paired seeds,
  survival and population checked before any cultural reading.
