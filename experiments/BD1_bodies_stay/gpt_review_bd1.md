# GPT's review of the BD1 draft (23 October 2026)

**Source: GPT (ChatGPT, the creator's account), asked by the assistant. GPT's opinion, in summary; not verified
sources and not sign-off.**

- Supports the order and philosophy. The bigger risk is not scripting burial or taboo but accidentally scripting **how
  often bodies are eaten, how belongings are inherited, and how grief changes behaviour.**
- 18 food units: a modelling assumption, not a biological fact; freeze it as such.
- **Ownership of food after death is a social abstraction**; reusing v3's store-and-stealing is defensible as a
  shortcut, but document that it keeps an existing mechanic rather than a law of nature.
- Inheritance extended in time is fine if it is v3's existing kin rule; check it does not give kin unusual advantages.
- Scavengers eating 30% a week is a strong fixed number; it may make bodies vanish too fast.
- Sickness 0.3: define the exposure event and repeat exposures, or repeated contact makes sickness near-certain.
- **Rot disgust as biology: yes, with limits** (tied to visible decay, not to death itself); the −0.1 mood is an
  engineered number; say whether it applies once a week however often someone passes.
- **The hunting-party rule would force eating** whenever a party hunts there. Make eating possible, not compulsory:
  use an existing decision to obtain food, or record the limitation.
- Before freezing: separate physical and social effects in the analysis (BD1 changes several things at once, so it
  cannot say which caused an outcome); specify the decay arithmetic and order; freeze the measures. Treat generational
  change in eating as descriptive, not cultural transmission, unless ACL could transmit such a norm.
- Verdict: worth testing after clarifying harvesting, decay and ownership.

## What the assistant changed (Revision 1)

- **Eating is a choice:** the body's meat lies as food on the tile, owned by the dead, and is taken only through v3's
  own decision to take someone else's food (when hunger is above 0.4; v3's small scruple for the dead). No hunting
  rule. The ownership shortcut is documented in the plan.
- **No new numbers for decay or scavengers:** the body rots at v3's rate for loose food (4% a week, slower under a
  shelter or by a fire); the scavenger rule is dropped (it can be a later study).
- **Rotten** = when 40% would have rotted at v3's rate (13 weeks). **Disgust:** at most once a week per body, through
  v3's place memory (-0.3, like finding food gone, -0.6), with no mood number.
- **Sickness** from rotten meat: chance 0.3 per meal from a rotten body, only if not already sick.
- **Grief at the sight:** not a new number: someone close who had not known of the death learns it from the body,
  exactly as v3's witnesses of a death do.
