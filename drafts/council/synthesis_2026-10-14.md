## 2. When storage appears: one idea is already answered, one is the open lever

| council's condition | ACL's record |
|---|---|
| Defensibility: stores must be hard for others to take (Woodburn, Testart, Peterson) | **Not the bottleneck for stashes.** W1: others took 44–60 units from stashes against 1,255–1,451 taken back by owners; stashes are simply almost unused (S1: three gates, mainly scoring). |
| Anticipation of a remembered lean season (Suddendorf & Corballis; scarcity studies) | E9–E9.4: a season-ahead feeling exists but the lesson undoes itself (preparing prevents going short, so the lesson fades below the threshold). E10 and E11 tried loss and stories. |
| Sharing pressure scales with windfalls, not staples (Winterhalder; Gurven) | **Open, and it meets ACL's sowing result.** D1/D2: sowing really returns 2.36 food per seed, but **52% of the crop is eaten by non-kin** and the sower's family gets back 1.08 per seed while others harvest 1.43. For crops, the problem *is* capture. |

**Reading:** the stash is held back by the decision (S1), not by theft. The **sown crop** is held back by capture
(D2): what one family plants, others eat. Kimi's first and third conditions (exclusion; sharing that depends on the
kind of food) both point at the same open question ACL has already named twice: **belonging and ownership** (ROADMAP
candidates; Law 126 open point 4: "kin and ownership knowledge case by case, or a general rule").
