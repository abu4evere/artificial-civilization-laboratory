How ACL tests itself

Most simulations can tell you what happened. ACL tries to say what caused it, and to keep its creator from quietly writing the answer in.

Predictions are written down and frozen before any data

Every study has a plan, a prediction and a line that would mean the prediction was wrong. The plan is frozen and tagged in the repository before code or data. Wrong predictions stay in the record.

Many worlds, side by side

One world can do almost anything. Every comparison runs the same starting worlds under both sets of rules, often 256 of each, and a difference has to hold across them. Results are checked on a second, unseen set where possible.

Exact replay

The simulation is deterministic: any world can be replayed exactly, down to its final state. Instruments that watch a world are checked to change nothing in it.

Nature only

Only nature is programmed: bodies, needs, materials, seasons. Changes to nature need a written case and evidence about the real world. Nothing is rewarded for being cultural, and no agent is told what to invent.

Corrections in the open

Mistakes are corrected beside the original, never over it, and every number traces to a committed script.
