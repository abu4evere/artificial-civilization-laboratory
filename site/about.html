About ACL

The Artificial Civilization Laboratory is built by Abdurashid Khudoyberdiev. It began as an idea for a personal AI companion and turned into a bigger question: what would it take for a civilization to start by itself?

The project is open source. Its code, plans, results and corrections are public on GitHub. Its story is told in the book The World That Nobody Wrote, on Amazon.
