The ACL Mind

This is what goes on inside one person in ACL, drawn as its own kind of creature. It isn't a human brain. Every part shown here exists in the code of nature v11, and every number is read from a real person in a real world. Their mind does one "heartbeat" a week: it feels, sorts, weighs, chooses, acts, and learns. Press Follow one heartbeat for a guided tour, or click any part.
