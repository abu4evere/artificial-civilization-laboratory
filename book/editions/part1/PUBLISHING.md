## The same text everywhere

- **Title:** The World That Nobody Wrote
- **Subtitle:** Part One: How We Tried to Build a Civilization Instead of Simulating One
- **Author:** Abdurashid Khudoyberdiev
- **Short description (about 150 words):**
  > Twenty people wake on a set of islands knowing nothing: no tools, no fire, no rules. Their bodies, feelings and
  > memories were programmed; everything they make of their lives was not. This is the story of the Artificial
  > Civilization Laboratory, a computer world built to watch culture begin without writing it in, told from its own
  > experiment records. It follows the people who lived there, the questions asked of them, and the answers that came
  > back, including every prediction that turned out wrong. Why did whole worlds die out? Do separate bands ever meet?
  > Are people's habits their own, or copied from everyone around them? Part One covers the experiments to October
  > 2026; the project continues, and Part Two will begin where this ends. Every number in the book traces to a frozen
  > record in the project's public repository.
- **Keywords:** artificial societies; agent-based simulation; cultural evolution; artificial life; hunter-gatherers;
  reinforcement learning; reproducible research

## Zenodo (a permanent, citable DOI)

1. Sign in at zenodo.org (GitHub sign-in works) → **New upload**.
2. Upload the **PDF**.
3. Fill in: **Resource type** Book · **Title** and **Subtitle** as above (Zenodo: put the subtitle after a colon in the
   title) · **Creators** Abdurashid Khudoyberdiev · **Description** the short description · **Keywords** as above ·
   **Version** "Part One" · **Publication date** 2026-10-15 · **Licence**: "Other (Not Open)" with "All rights
   reserved", or choose a Creative Commons licence if you want others to share it (your decision) ·
   **Related works**: "Is supplemented by" → https://github.com/abu4evere/simulated-world.
4. **Publish.** Then send me the DOI: I'll add it to the book page, the README and your profile.

Note: this is the book only. The full project deposit (code and data, `release/`) stays for later, as planned.

## Amazon KDP (paperback and Kindle)

You need: a KDP account (kdp.amazon.com), your tax interview and bank details (only you should enter these).

**Kindle ebook**
1. **Create → Kindle eBook.** Title, subtitle, author, description and keywords as above.
2. **Categories** (pick up to 3): Science → Social Sciences / Anthropology; Computers → Artificial Intelligence;
   Science → Life Sciences / Evolution (choose the closest Amazon offers).
3. **AI-generated content: answer Yes** for **text** (the chapters were drafted with Claude from the records and
   checked) and **Yes** for **images** (the cover picture comes from an AI-generated image). Amazon requires this
   disclosure; the book says so on its "About Part One" page.
4. **Manuscript:** upload the **.docx**. **Cover:** upload `cover_part1.png`. Preview the result in Kindle Previewer before
   publishing.
5. Price: your choice (KDP's 70% royalty applies from $2.99 to $9.99).

**Paperback**
1. **Create → Paperback**, same details.
2. **Print options:** black ink on white paper · trim size **6 × 9 in** · no bleed · matte cover.
3. **Manuscript:** upload the **PDF** (margins already meet KDP's rules for 213 pages).
4. **Cover:** use **Cover Creator** with `cover_part1.png` as the front; it builds the spine and back to the right width.
5. Order a **proof copy** before publishing, to check it in your hands.
6. ISBN: KDP gives one free (Amazon becomes the listed publisher), or buy your own from Bowker.
