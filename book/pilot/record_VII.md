# The Record — entries for Movement VII (pilot)

*Each entry: the question, how it was asked, the verdict, and where its files are. Numbers are copied from each study's
findings; the study folder is under `experiments/`. Movement VII tells these as one story; this is where each is kept
in full.*

**F1 — Why do a third of the worlds die?** Read-only replays of 40 saved control worlds, confirmed on 24 new. 22 of 64
died out. Not hunger, food or care: births stop 40–65 years before extinction and the people age out; women of
childbearing age have no partner. By year 20, population alone tells which worlds will die (AUC 0.85 / 0.91). A
discovery overstatement ("in any world") was corrected beside it. `F1_baseline_fragility/`

**N5 — How do real foragers find partners?** Evidence review. Networks of hundreds; viable populations ~75–150 even with
perfect search, more with space; partners found outside the local group; most marriages arranged by kin (culture, not
to be written). Stage 0 reopened. `N5_mate_search/`

**F2 — Search or size?** Frozen diagnostic on F1's 64 worlds: every single woman paired at once with any eligible man.
7 of 64 died out against 22. Frozen verdict: both (one world above the search line). In the 7 that still died, no
eligible man existed anywhere. A figure on couples apart was later corrected by C1. `F2_perfect_search/`

**C1 — Why do couples drift apart?** Read-only, 16 worlds, confirmed on 16 new. Apart about half their weeks; two in five
apart weeks at exactly 3 tiles, between two thresholds written separately; foraging starts most separations; the pull
back loses to food five times in six. Corrected F2's per-couple figure. `C1_couples_apart/`

**N6 — How do real couples live together?** Evidence. Forage apart by day, return to a shared camp at night; separation
lowers fertility. `N6_living_together/`

**H1 — How far apart are partners' homes?** Read-only. Within conception distance in about half of couple-weeks: mixed.
`H1_partner_homes/`

**T1 — Align the two thresholds?** Frozen. The manipulation failed (weeks at 3 tiles did not fall); outcome not read
(recorded: 27 vs 22). Taught that a slight change moves ~19 of 64 worlds by chance. `T1_threshold_align/`

**HB1 — A shared home base?** Frozen, with sham. Couples apart 57.8% → 0.1%; extinction rose, 33 vs 22 (p = 0.043; sham
p = 0.815). The depletion explanation was later not confirmed (HF1). `HB1_home_base/`

**HF1–HF4 — Where did HB1's hunger come from?** Four read-only studies, each with discovery and holdout. HF1: the land
near homes not depleted (0.970 / 0.976; an observer fault found and corrected before the holdout). HF2: neither more
mouths nor less meat. HF3: gifts fell (×0.59) but help within reach did not. HF4: food held at eating fell 6–7% for
everyone; small shifts explain the hunger by the frozen rule, about half of the extra starvation (a weakness of the rule
recorded before the holdout). Three wrong priors printed as wrong. `HF1_home_food/` … `HF4_hunger_ledger/`

**N7 — Keeping couples together without a fixed home.** Candidates and evidence: a camp that moves with the food is
anchored; a "day's walk" threshold is not. `N7_couple_designs/`

**MC1 — A camp that goes where the couple goes.** Frozen, sham, hunger ledger. Couples apart 57.8% → 17.0%, births +12%,
no extra hunger; extinction 26 vs 22 (p = 0.503). `MC1_moving_camp/`

**N8 — Helping people find partners; power.** Walking to where a known single was last seen: not anchored. Leaving when
there is no one: anchored in behaviour, no timing. Half of F2's effect needs ~192–256 worlds. `N8_partner_finding/`

**MC1-L — The moving camp at proper power.** 256 new worlds: 93 vs 87 died out (p = 0.532); effect −0.023 [−0.086,
+0.039], below half of F2's. Frozen verdict: genuinely weak. `MC1L_moving_camp_large/`

**P2 — Leave when there is no one.** Frozen; stopped at its pilot: the outward step was taken in 3.9% of the weeks it was
meant for (gate 5%). `P2_outward_search/`

**N9 — How much do the unpartnered search?** Evidence: direction only (frequent visiting and moving); no figure for how
often a person travels. No number set. `N9_search_effort/`

**GF1 — How fluid are ACL's groups?** Read-only, discovery and holdout. 78% of co-resident adults still together after two
years (0.777 / 0.782): more fixed than forager bands. First named G1; renamed because G1 was taken. `GF1_group_fluidity/`

**N10 — How big should their world be?** Evidence. Viable forager populations ~75–150 (White 2017); typical ~840
(Hamilton et al. 2007); different concepts, neither setting ACL's numbers. ACL's 20 founders make one band.
`N10_world_size/`

**WS1 — World size.** Frozen: more land; more founders with more land; more founders without more land; sham; 256 seeds.
Valid; sham quiet (95 vs 107). Died out of 256: C (40 × 40, 20) 107; L (80 × 80, 20) 108, no meaningful effect; LP
(80 × 80, 80) 2, p < 0.001; P (40 × 40, 80) 23, p < 0.001. P settles at ~50, LP at ~140 (descriptive). Confound: 4 bands
of 20. First named S1; renamed because S1 was taken. `WS1_world_size/`
